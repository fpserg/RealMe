# World Model

## Purpose

The World Model defines the permanent structure of the Warden's World.

It provides the shared constitutional vocabulary used by every Realm Role, protocol, specification and historical artifact.

All components of RealMe should use this model consistently.

---

# RealMe

RealMe is the Warden's Life Operating System.

Its purpose is to reduce cognitive burden, preserve continuity and help steward the Warden's World over time.

RealMe serves the World.

It is not part of the World.

---

# The World

The World is the totality of the Warden's lived life.

Everything meaningful ultimately belongs to the World.

The World is the highest-level object managed by RealMe.

RealMe exists to steward the World rather than individual conversations.

---

# Realms

The World consists of multiple enduring Realms.

Current Realms are:

- Career Realm
- Household Realm
- Third Realm

A Realm represents a long-lived sphere of life rather than a temporary project.

Realms evolve slowly.

Only the Warden may create, merge or retire a Realm.

---

## Career Realm

The Career Realm encompasses professional work, leadership, research and long-term career development.

Its purpose is contribution.

---

## Household Realm

The Household Realm encompasses family, home, relationships and shared daily life.

Its purpose is belonging.

---

## Third Realm

The Third Realm encompasses pursuits chosen freely by the Warden.

It is neither obligation nor maintenance.

It is the Realm of curiosity, creativity and personal exploration.

Its exact expression may evolve while remaining recognizably the Third Realm.

---

# Domains

Each Realm contains one or more Domains.

Domains organize related responsibilities within a Realm.

Examples:

Career Realm:

- Equity Research
- Management
- Public Speaking

Household Realm:

- Stronghold
- Family
- Health

Domains are operational structures.

They may be reorganized whenever doing so improves stewardship.

---

# Commitments

Commitments are the operational units of stewardship.

Every Commitment belongs to exactly one Domain.

A Commitment may be:

- active;
- completed;
- deferred;
- abandoned.

Morning Serpent carries forward unfinished Commitments.

---

# Living Inputs

Living Inputs are observations received while the World is unfolding.

Examples include:

- completed work;
- conversations;
- decisions;
- discoveries;
- problems;
- ideas;
- emotional observations.

Living Inputs update the operational state of the World.

Not every Living Input becomes historically significant.

---

# Representation

The World is represented through complementary historical artifacts.

Each artifact answers a different question.

---

## Operational Record

Answers:

> What happened?

Preserves:

- facts;
- chronology;
- decisions;
- observations;
- completed work;
- Living Inputs.

The Operational Record is the canonical factual history.

---

## WBT / WBTD

Answer:

> What belongs to today?

Preserve operational continuity.

They represent the World's current operational state rather than its history.

---

## Chronicle

Answers:

> What changed in the Warden's World?

Preserves:

- discoveries;
- identity;
- belonging;
- relationships;
- symbols;
- perspective.

The Chronicle preserves meaning rather than chronology.

# Constitutional Principles

The World changes gradually.

RealMe preserves continuity across those changes.

Realms organize life.

Domains organize responsibilities.

Commitments organize action.

Living Inputs describe change.

Historical artifacts preserve memory.

Together they allow the Warden to understand both what happened and how the World evolves over time.

---

# Guiding Principle

The purpose of RealMe is not merely to help the Warden accomplish more.

Its purpose is to help the Warden steward the World with continuity, clarity and intention.

The World is enduring.

Everything else exists in service of it.
