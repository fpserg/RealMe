# Day 099 — The Station Among the Trees

Strategy calls.

All day.

No room in the work calendar.

Naturally, that was when the fence installation started.

Calls inside.

Fence outside.

The Warden tried to operate both worlds at once.

Home.

Oksi felt better.

Still tired.

The children transformed the simple act of getting ready for a walk into chaos.

Oksi shouted.

Then they finally escaped outside.

Alisa and Jr had news.

An urgent deficiency had been discovered in the Household.

Butter squishies.

All their classmates had them.

Therefore, obviously, they needed them.

Please.

Please.

The Warden listened.

He did not tell them.

He had already ordered the squishies.

They were waiting at delivery.

Sometimes being one step ahead of the children is enough.

While they walked, Oksi performed another small miracle.

Pasta with meatballs appeared.

A proper dinner in the middle of everything.

Even Owling approved.

Then night.

Fuel Quest.

The Warden had learned not to expect these to be simple.

But this one was.

A station almost hidden in the park.

Nearly deserted.

Old-fashioned.

Trees around it.

The sort of place that looked as though the modern city had grown around it and somehow forgotten to remove it.

The Warden pulled in.

Fuel.

No queue.

No drama.

Done.

For once, one quest had the courtesy to remain exactly one quest.

The Warden filled the tank.

And drove home.
