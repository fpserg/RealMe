# Day 082 — The House Appears

Seven in the morning.

Acceptable.

After the recent Night Watches, that alone was a small improvement.

Stronghold woke into an ordinary weekend rhythm. The Warden took Owling out for water and apples — supplies were useful, but the real purpose was partly acoustic. Stronghold would be quieter for everyone else if Owling's morning energy travelled with him.

Back home, the children inspected the bathroom.

They liked it.

That was perhaps a better acceptance test than any contractor could provide.

Oksi was already back in the garden, weeding after she woke. Later, after Owling's daytime sleep, the Warden joined her for more garden work. Then Oksi left for the dentist and command of Stronghold passed temporarily to the Warden.

A truck arrived.

The kitchen.

It was unloaded into the house, another large piece of the construction campaign becoming physical reality.

Then came the surprise hidden inside the delivery.

The cooker.

And the cooker should have a passport.

For months the Gas Campaign had behaved like one of those quests where every completed prerequisite reveals another missing object. At least for the current stage, this appeared to be the last missing piece of the puzzle.

The next obstacle was no longer an object.

It was time.

Somewhere between Strategy calls and the Platform Economy report, the Warden would need to carve out a day and attempt the document submission again.

There was another knock at the door.

The Warden opened it and found Roma.

The children were surprised. Last time they had met, Roma had told them he would not be back until next summer. Yet here he was, asking them to come outside.

They were delighted and disappeared toward the football field.

The Warden took Owling for another walk.

It began in rain and ended in rain.

Along the way he saw a group of people in rubber boots carrying baskets. Mushroom pickers, apparently. Their direction suggested something else: there was a route from Stronghold toward the forest.

The Warden tried to advance along it with Owling on his shoulders.

The ground answered immediately.

Too damp.

The expedition would require rubber boots.

So the forest remained on the edge of the map for another day.

When the Warden returned, Oksi arrived soon afterwards carrying another piece of the future Stronghold: a set of new rose bushes.

Before bedtime the Warden took all the children to the playground, giving Oksi more time for the garden.

There were still practical matters beneath the quiet day. Alexey, Kadyr's landlord, called. They agreed to meet the following week to decide what work should come next. The Warden suspected that Alexey's more immediate reason for the conversation was simpler: Kadyr still had an outstanding wage, expected on September 15.

But the evening itself was quiet.

And Oksi kept saying the same thing.

She loved this place.

It was turning from a construction site into a dream house.

The Warden listened and felt proud.

Stronghold had spent so much of its life presenting itself through what was wrong with it: unfinished rooms, contractors, water, drainage, fuel, deliveries, money, mud, deadlines. Even victories often merely unlocked the next campaign.

Today those things were still present. A gas submission remained ahead. A contractor payment remained ahead. The kitchen still had to be assembled. The forest path was too wet to follow.

But the house was becoming visible between them.

Children liked the bathroom. A friend knocked at the door. Roses arrived. The kitchen sat inside. Oksi gardened. People somewhere beyond the edge of the property walked toward a forest with baskets.

Stronghold was acquiring ordinary life.

In the middle of the night, Owling woke again.

The Warden tried to settle him and, for the first time in several days, opened RealMe development work. Career had consumed the week. Now, inside another Night Watch, the Third Realm quietly reopened.

The construction site was not gone.

But Day 082 was one of the days when the house appeared through it.
