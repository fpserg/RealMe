# Day 078 — The Day That Kept Going

Morning looked almost ordinary again.

The Warden took the children to school and headed for the office. Oksi was still unwell, but after a day spent managing Career from home there was a boundary that could no longer move: Career could not be sacrificed longer.

And Career took the invitation seriously.

The Strategy Report needed to become a coherent draft for Andrey. A large part of the day went into making sure Artem understood the hypothesis that had emerged from the previous review. Perhaps the market was not merely assigning Russian equities an exceptionally high risk premium. Perhaps it was also assigning them lower future earnings.

That distinction mattered. A report that called everything ERP could be dismantled by investors who believed the denominator was wrong as well as the discount rate.

So the hypothesis had to be tested rather than merely phrased better.

Then came the TMT section, the joining of the report's parts, and, in parallel, Alexander and the juniors' work on platforms arriving for review. Lunch disappeared somewhere in the process. Normal office hours disappeared after it.

Stronghold meanwhile produced a problem of unusual physical clarity. The kitchen was ready to be installed. The bath was still standing in the middle of the future kitchen.

There are sophisticated dependencies and there are bathtubs.

This one belonged to Roman.

By the time the Warden returned home, Oksi had already put Owling to bed. She was tired, still unwell and angry about the School Admission Quest. Sergey Jr was still not formally accepted at School 444, and the hardest part remained that she could do nothing to force the next step. The frustration did not remain neatly attached to the school. It spread to the Warden, to Roman, and outward.

It was a disappointing ending to a hard work day.

But not everything in the Household was waiting or blocked.

Alisa came home with two Fives and a Four. Her school year had started remarkably well, and the Warden allowed himself to enjoy that simple fact and hope it lasted.

Then Owling remembered an older tradition.

Bedtime ceased to be bedtime. The Warden found himself back on Night Watch: children's books, cartoons, and a small companion apparently ready to continue long after the Strategy Report, the platforms, the bathtub and School 444 had exhausted everyone else's allotted hours.

The day had begun with the declaration that Career could not be sacrificed longer.

Career got its day.

Then Household took the night.
