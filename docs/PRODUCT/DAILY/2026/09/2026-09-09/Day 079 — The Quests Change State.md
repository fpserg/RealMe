# Day 079 — The Quests Change State

Two large Career campaigns occupied the foreground: Equity Strategy and Platform Economy.

Equity Strategy had now reached Andrey. His verdict on the general part was concise: “you are not so useless as I thought.” In the local language of appraisal, this counted as something close to praise.

The Sectors and Companies part received a less ceremonial response: a bunch of corrections.

So the report had crossed a boundary, but not a finish line. It was no longer merely being assembled. It had entered revision under senior scrutiny.

While Career tasks continued to flow, another long-running campaign reached its own threshold in Tower.

TWR was finally listed.

Alex operated. The Warden watched where he could, moving back and forth between Career work and the market, and stepped into his Whale role to support liquidity. The price held. After the long preparation, that was enough for the moment. Alex should be satisfied.

Then came the message from Household that mattered more.

Sergey Jr was accepted at School 444.

The School Admission Quest that had generated days of uncertainty, procedural barriers, anxiety and enforced waiting finally resolved after Oksi made one last push with the documents. She had wanted action when the process seemed beyond her control; in the end there was one final piece of action left for her to take.

Victory did not look particularly triumphant. She seemed exhausted.

The Warden was already moving again, rushing to the stadium to take Owling while Sergey Jr was at football, freeing Oksi to go shopping for school books.

That was perhaps the most accurate ending for the quest. Acceptance did not produce an empty task list. It produced school books.

The same was true elsewhere.

TWR's listing ended the preparation campaign and began the post-listing one.

Andrey's review ended the first construction of Equity Strategy and began corrections.

School 444 ended admission uncertainty and began ordinary school life.

Some days are marked by completion.

Day 079 was marked by something slightly different: several important things finally changed state.
