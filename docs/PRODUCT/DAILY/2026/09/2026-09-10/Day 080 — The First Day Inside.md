# Day 080 — The First Day Inside

For days, School 444 had been a gate.

Today Sergey Jr walked through it.

The Warden led him there for his first day and was surprised by how quickly the boy seemed to understand the new territory: where to go, who his teacher was, how the place worked. After all the adult anxiety surrounding admission, the child himself appeared to begin adapting almost immediately.

Career allowed little time to contemplate the milestone.

Andrey's Strategy feedback had to be incorporated, and the Warden was coordinating corrections across the team's sections. Artem was sick and working from home, which made the machinery less smooth. A retail brokerage call produced only a brief chance to speak about Ozon before the agenda moved on. Platform Economy continued too, now with a consultation from Alexey of ECM.

There was also another small campaign to organize: the team needed to ask familiar IR contacts for support in the Best Analyst ranking.

Then Oksi sent a screenshot.

“I’m unwell,” Jr had written to her.

The Warden called immediately, already knowing what those two words would do to Oksi after the admission campaign. But she had moved faster. She had contacted his teacher and established that everything was fine.

By evening, the message had become only one incident inside a much larger first-day story.

Jr had spoken English in English class and surprised everyone, including the teacher. He had answered correctly in other lessons. He found Sasha, his friend from preschool. He met Vanya, the third-grade neighbour, who gave him a sweet. He described dealing forcefully with a classmate who tried to bully him.

He ate soup and peeni.

Then he ate another bowl of soup because Sasha did not want his.

And after all that, he wanted to go back tomorrow.

The Warden and Jr walked with Owling before bedtime. Somewhere along the way the Warden secretly bought the new schoolboy a chocolate bar and a soft drink. No ceremony was announced. None was needed.

Alisa had news too: two Fives and a Four. A dramatic downturn from yesterday's four Fives, the Warden joked. In reality, he was simply happy with how her school year was beginning.

Stronghold meanwhile acquired dates. Kitchen delivery: Saturday. Assembly: Thursday and Friday next week.

But before that could happen, logistics demanded another campaign. Oksi had accumulated enough home and garden accessories — including a garden bench — that the family and the cargo would no longer fit into the car together for the next day's trip.

So after the children went to bed, the Warden launched a Night Stronghold expedition.

It was a warm ride with a little adventure attached. On the way back the fuel gauge sank low. One gas station was empty. Then another. Eventually petrol was found and the Warden returned home.

By then the boundary between days had become theoretical.

Owling began his morning at four.

He finally went back to sleep at seven, precisely when the Warden needed to wake the other children for school.

This was the moment the Warden noticed what would happen without intervention: two chapters of the Book of Life would simply collapse into one.

So a boundary had to be drawn.

But Day 080 deserved its own chapter.

It was the day Sergey Jr stopped being a child waiting to enter School 444 and became a schoolboy with a teacher, classmates, two bowls of soup, stories to tell at home — and somewhere he was excited to return to tomorrow.
