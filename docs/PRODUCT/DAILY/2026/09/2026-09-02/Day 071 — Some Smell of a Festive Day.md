# 📜 Chronicle of Day 071

## Chapter: Some Smell of a Festive Day

## 🎒 September First

September 1 arrived with three thresholds at once.

Alisa was starting at a new school.

Maksim was starting at a new school too.

And Sergey Jr was starting first grade.

The Warden went with Alisa, Sergey Jr and Owling to escort Alisa. Oksi took Maksim to his own new beginning.

The family divided itself between school doors.

---

## 📖 The First Grade That Almost Had No First Day

Sergey Jr had been accepted to the best school in the neighborhood.

Normally, he should not have been.

It was not the closest school to home, and the unusual admission path meant that his first grade began without the proper September 1 ceremony that usually marks the moment.

A first day happens only once.

So the family assembled what it could around the missing ritual.

Nice clothes.

Alisa beside him.

A book in his hands for the photograph.

There was still, as the Warden put it, **some smell of festive day**.

---

## 🎈 The Festival Rebuilt

Oksi did not leave it there.

She ordered sweets and balloons for the children.

Sergey Jr received something he had wanted for a long time: a replica of the Football World Cup.

In the photos she sent, he was shining almost as brightly as the cup itself.

Owling found his own treasure in the balloons.

His face on the photographs made their value obvious.

The ceremony the school had not provided was being rebuilt at home.

---

## 🗞️ The Team Moves Without the Warden

Career continued around the family milestone.

Artem handled media comments on interim dividends.

Sergey the Junior Analyst was polishing the pharma-market template. Once finished, the plan was to turn it into a regular note rather than another one-off piece of work.

And Artem had gone to Andrey himself.

He discussed both Strategy progress and his own promotion.

The Warden had intended to be present, but September 1 had taken precedence.

Perhaps another conversation with Andrey would still be necessary.

But something else was visible too.

The team was moving even when the Warden was elsewhere.

---

## 🌆 Capital Evening

Back at Capital, the next school day was already approaching.

Oksi helped Maksim and Alisa prepare.

The Warden took Owling and Sergey for a walk.

The extraordinary first day dissolved into the ordinary machinery required for the second.

---

## 🌳 Guess What

Then, almost inevitably, Stronghold called.

Another night drive.

This time the cargo was pear and peach trees.

The Warden carried them to Stronghold and watered some plants as a bonus.

The garden had already consumed nursery expeditions, sacks of soil, endless pits and wet evenings.

Apparently it was not finished collecting trees either.

---

## 🧱 The Next Perimeter

On the way back, Oksi began insisting on the fence.

Stronghold needed one.

The Warden agreed.

That was the problem.

A needless expense can simply be rejected. A necessary one has to compete with all the other necessary ones.

After Bathroom work, plumbing, doors, plants, soil, kitchen preparation and the continuing Capital equation, the fence appeared as another drag on finances precisely because it belonged there.

---

## 📚 Steward's Reflection

Day 071 was about imperfect beginnings made whole enough.

Sergey Jr did not receive the conventional first September 1 of his life. Bureaucratic geography and an unusual admission path had taken that ritual away. But a milestone is not owned entirely by institutions. His family supplied the clothes, the photograph, the book, the sweets, the balloons and the World Cup.

There was enough celebration left for him to shine.

Career offered a parallel form of maturation. Artem handled public comments and took his own case to Andrey. Sergey the Junior Analyst was preparing work meant to become repeatable. The Warden may still need to intervene, but his absence for a family milestone did not stop the team from moving.

And Stronghold, as usual, waited until night.

Two more trees arrived. Then the fence entered the conversation.

The house keeps approaching completion by revealing the next thing it needs.

But on this day, before budgets and fences and management conversations reclaimed attention, there was a boy holding a book beside his sister and a photograph carrying some smell of a festive day.
