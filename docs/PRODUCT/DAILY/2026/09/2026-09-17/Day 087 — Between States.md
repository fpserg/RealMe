# Day 087 — Between States

Strategy feedback arrived.

The team incorporated it.

That report, after weeks of concentrated attention, was now moving through its final machinery rather than demanding another reinvention.

The Warden's main attention was elsewhere.

Platform Economy.

He consolidated the report and sent it to Andrey.

Then the answer came back.

Constructive feedback.

Good.

Incorporate it.

The report had crossed another threshold: no longer a plan, no longer a collection of sections from different hands, but something coherent enough for Andrey to react to as a whole.

At Stronghold, another long-awaited threshold was more physical.

Kitchen assembly started.

Boxes and components were finally turning into the room they had been purchased to create.

Elsewhere, Sergey Jr's school day went badly.

During a lesson he asked to use the WC and was refused.

There was an accident.

Oksi began investigating in the class chat.

It was exactly the kind of school problem that could quickly become larger in a parent's mind before the adults involved had actually spoken to each other.

Later, the School 444 teacher called.

The issue was mostly resolved.

But School 444 was not the only school in the day's story.

Another message arrived.

Sergey Jr had passed the School 57 preliminaries into Stage 2.

Oksi was happy.

Only recently the family had fought through the procedural uncertainty around getting him formally into School 444. Now he was attending there, building routines and having real school days — including imperfect ones.

And at the same time, the School 57 path remained alive.

Not a transfer.

Not an admission.

Another stage.

Capital had its own visitors coming.

Oksi prepared the apartment while the Warden took the children for a walk.

Owling was tired.

Then scandalous.

The timing became another small logistical calculation: get everyone back just before the viewing without letting the overtired child consume the entire preparation.

They made it.

The visitors were a nice couple.

No children.

They seemed to like the place.

Oksi showed them around.

The Warden rocked Owling.

Capital had been shown to several people by now. Each viewing carried the possibility that the apartment might finally move from family home to sold asset.

But seeming to like it was not an offer.

So that door remained open too.

Late in the day, the kitchen assembler called.

His car had broken down.

He could not come tomorrow.

The Warden did not blame him.

Cars break.

But consequences do not require blame.

The family was heading to Stronghold for the weekend, and the kitchen would remain half-done.

That could make the stay uncomfortable.

There was something almost representative about it.

The kitchen had started but was not finished.

School 444 had produced a problem but mostly resolved it.

School 57 had advanced Sergey Jr but not admitted him.

The potential buyers liked Capital but had not bought it.

Platform Economy had reached Andrey but returned for changes.

Nothing was failing.

Nothing was quite complete either.

Day 087 lived between states.

Perhaps that was simply where much of the World was now.

The large campaigns were no longer waiting to begin.

They had begun.

And beginning, it turned out, created a new landscape of half-built kitchens, second stages, revisions, viewings and next steps.

The doors were open.

The Warden was living in the thresholds.
