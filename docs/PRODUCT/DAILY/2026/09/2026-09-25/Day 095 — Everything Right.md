# Day 095 — Everything Right

The morning began as comedy.

The Warden woke the children.

School preparation.

Oksi sleeping peacefully.

Routine.

Then Owling opened his eyes.

One minute before departure.

The machine exploded.

Oksi jumped awake to make milk.

The Warden and Alisa ran around Owling trying to calm him.

The Warden made Oksi's bed.

Oksi gave Sergey Jr cookies.

The cookies did not fit into the school bag.

The school bag had to be rearranged.

Somehow they got out.

Jr would not be late for lessons.

Breakfast was less certain.

Behind them, Owling peacefully drank his milk.

As though nothing had happened.

On the way to school, Jr began to sing.

Russia's Eaglets.

The song from yesterday's first-graders event.

A day earlier, the Warden had watched him raise his hand eagerly in class.

Now school had followed them onto the road.

Career was quiet for once.

The client calls were done for the week.

The big reports were released.

Andrey was away.

Mostly housekeeping.

Then, on the way home, the message came.

Sanya had died yesterday.

The Warden stopped in a place that had not existed for years.

Once Sanya had been a great friend.

One of the best companions for doing crazy things.

Then came the thing that broke it.

Sanya and Oksi had apparently had an affair after the Warden and Oksi were already together.

The Warden left him behind.

They were almost not in touch after that.

He knew Sanya regretted it.

Years passed.

Then there were no more years.

The funeral would be tomorrow.

They would go.

The Warden arranged pet care.

The family set off for Stronghold.

Oksi cried on the road.

Not because of the affair.

That belonged to another time.

Sanya had been their friend.

Now he was gone.

Stronghold was waiting with something entirely different.

The children saw the new kitchen for the first time.

Wow.

Sergey Jr examined it.

Ancient, he decided.

Maksim and the Warden attacked the refrigerator.

It was damn heavy.

They pushed it into place.

Oksi began filling the drawers with kitchenware.

She could not stop cheering.

The bright-green kitchen was no longer something contractors had built.

The family was moving into it.

Then Oksi found a video.

Old.

Sanya and the Warden.

Goofing around.

As they always did.

Then photographs.

Older still.

All of them together.

Young.

Joyful.

Alive in a world where none of the later things had happened yet.

The Warden looked at Sanya.

It was hard to believe there would be no new photograph.

No later reconciliation.

No accidental meeting years from now.

No more time.

Only the time that had already happened.

The shock seemed to change something between the Warden and Oksi.

More warmth.

More closeness.

Perhaps grief.

Perhaps perspective.

Perhaps, the Warden allowed, the kitchen deserved some credit too.

Later they sat outside on the Stronghold deck.

Warm tea.

Four children sleeping peacefully inside.

The new kitchen behind them.

The photographs somewhere nearby.

The past had been messy.

Some choices had hurt.

Some relationships had broken.

Some things could never be repaired now.

But Stronghold was quiet.

Their children were asleep.

They were sitting beside each other.

No declaration was required.

They looked at the life around them.

And silently agreed.

They had done everything right.
