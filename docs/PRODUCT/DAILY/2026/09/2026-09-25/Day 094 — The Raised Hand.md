# Day 094 — The Raised Hand

The morning began with a change of assignment.

Sergey Jr had a first-graders event at school.

Someone needed to stay with Owling.

So the Warden skipped half a day at the office.

Career did not disappear.

It rarely does.

Two internal calls followed him into the morning.

Stroller moving.

Phone connected.

Work continuing in miniature.

Later Oksi showed him what he had missed.

Photos.

Videos.

Sergey Jr smiling.

Happy.

His hand eagerly in the air during the lesson.

The Warden watched him and saw something that had taken months to secure.

Not admission.

That had already happened.

Belonging.

Jr looked in his shorts at school.

Comfortable.

Where he was supposed to be.

On the way to the office, another message arrived.

Not from Household.

InfoLINE.

A claim.

The marketing agency alleged that its data had been misused in the VseInstrumenty report.

The Warden investigated with Alexander.

There was no instant verdict.

No reason to turn an allegation into a fact.

Andrey would be back Monday.

They would speak to InfoLINE together then.

Another open front.

Evening brought cameras.

The Warden streamed with Artem and colleagues from personal brokerage.

Artem was visibly nervous.

His second appearance.

The Warden knew the feeling.

He had been there too.

The stream went smoothly.

And behind the visible audience was another one.

Household.

Sergey Jr watching.

Maksim watching.

Invisible support from home.

Oksi watched too.

Or at least enough to notice something.

She sent the Warden a message.

He looked much more confident now.

It was a small observation.

But confidence is difficult to measure from inside yourself.

Sometimes someone else has to tell you it has appeared.

Oksi herself had changed too.

She went to the pharmacy.

Then the pickup point.

After days of feeling bad, angry, exhausted and waking the Warden in the night, she was obviously better.

The Household climate changed with her.

Not solved.

Not transformed.

Simply easier.

Day 094 contained several forms of progress that were visible only indirectly.

A school fit revealed in a child's face.

Professional confidence reflected back through a screen.

Recovery measured by an ordinary trip outside.

The strongest image belonged to Sergey Jr.

Hand raised.

Ready to answer.

After all the work required merely to get him into that classroom, he did not look like someone who had finally been allowed inside.

He looked like someone who belonged there.
