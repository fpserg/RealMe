# Day 092 — Handed to Tomorrow

The day was chaotic before it had properly formed.

Strategy was published.

That should have simplified something.

Instead, publication had created the next campaign.

Presentation.

Marketing.

Clients.

At the same time, Platform Economy still had to be finished.

And the surrounding work still had to move.

The Warden navigated between them.

Coordinate the Strategy presentation.

Cut the Platform report.

Orchestrate tasks.

Return.

Repeat.

Normal office hours ended.

The work did not.

Eventually the Warden left.

He made it home almost on time.

Almost enough for Oksi to get to her manicure.

She was still displeased.

The Warden had not replied to her during the day.

From her side, silence.

From his side, no available space in the day to answer.

Both facts could exist at once.

Oksi left.

The Warden took over.

Dinner.

Children.

Bed.

Oksi returned soon afterwards.

Still feeling bad.

Still angry.

The Warden recognized the pattern.

When she is sick, the anger often lands on him.

There was another mission still waiting.

Fuel.

Recently it had become easy again.

Not tonight.

One station.

No.

Another.

No.

Five stations before the Warden finally found fuel.

The old logistical constraint had not disappeared.

It had merely been quiet.

Eventually the house slept.

Then Oksi woke the Warden in the middle of the night.

She felt very bad.

The Warden got up.

Found pills.

Helped her calm down somewhat.

There was no clean ending to the day.

No completed report.

No resolved illness.

No restored sleep.

Only the recognition of what morning would inherit.

Career still crowded.

Oksi still sick.

The Warden now tired too.

Tomorrow would be difficult for both of them.

Day 092 was not about failing to finish.

It was about discovering that sometimes the boundary between one day and the next is administrative rather than real.

The work crosses it.

The illness crosses it.

The tiredness crosses it.

Some days end by closing their tasks.

This one simply handed them to tomorrow.
