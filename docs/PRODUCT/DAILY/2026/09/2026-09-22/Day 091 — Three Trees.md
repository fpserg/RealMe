# Day 091 — Three Trees

The Strategy report was published.

For weeks it had been a campaign measured in drafts.

Sections.

Corrections.

Models.

Feedback.

Production.

Arguments over what the market was actually pricing.

MTS changing its dividend policy.

The Warden being wrong about DPS and deciding that did not make the stock wrong.

Artem carrying parts of the work.

Andrey sending it back.

Again.

And again.

Now the report existed outside the team.

Published.

One large campaign had crossed an important milestone.

The next phase was different.

Marketing.

The report had been made.

Now it had to travel.

There was little time to admire the transition because another report was waiting.

Platform Economy.

The juniors had produced material.

A lot of material.

That created its own problem.

A report is not the sum of everything everyone has written.

The Warden had to cut through it.

Streamline.

Remove repetition.

Find the argument.

And, where the incoming pieces were not enough, add meaningful parts himself.

Production had ended in one campaign.

Editorial construction continued in another.

Stronghold sent an invoice.

The management company wanted the utilities bill paid within ten days.

The Warden had no objection to paying.

There was only one problem.

The numbers did not add.

So he asked management to investigate.

Give the right number.

Or explain the difference.

An obligation does not become less real because its arithmetic needs checking.

Evening.

Sergey Jr had football.

The Warden had Owling control at the playground.

Oksi was still not getting better.

After Owling finally went to bed, the Warden went out again.

Pharmacy.

Medicines for Oksi.

Back home there was another small repair.

A splinter in her finger.

The Warden helped remove it.

And then Captain Sparrow revealed the latest expansion of her botanical empire.

More plants.

Usually this announcement implied more holes.

More digging.

More flourishing territory.

This time the Warden approved.

Three small fir trees.

Finally.

Among the flowers and bushes and endless pits, something different.

A forest corner.

The Warden had wanted one.

Not a landscaped imitation of a forest.

Just enough trees to create a darker, quieter piece of Stronghold amid everything Captain Sparrow was making bloom.

Three trees were not a forest.

The Strategy report being published was not the end of Strategy either.

Both were thresholds.

The report now had to find readers.

The trees now had to grow.

Day 091 was a day when completed work immediately became the beginning of something else.

Production became marketing.

Junior drafts became a report that still needed an author.

A bill became a reconciliation.

And three small fir trees became, at least in the Warden's mind, a forest.
