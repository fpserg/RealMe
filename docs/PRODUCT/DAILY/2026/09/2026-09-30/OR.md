## Reflection

Day 100 raised one Career project and quietly folded one part of the past into the present.

Platform Economy had begun as secondary work under Strategy deadline pressure, with substantial contribution from juniors and AI. Bank management's request for a live presentation next week changes its status. The attention is recognition, but for the Warden it also converts a report that could have been published and forgotten into something he personally has to carry into the room.

The evening offered a different kind of continuity. Sergey Jr now trains on a stadium built over the wasteland where the Warden once played football with Sanya, beside Sanya's old apartment block. Nothing needed to be done with that fact. The same ground simply belongs to another childhood now.

Owling then consumed the remaining night window and accidentally rescued the Warden from a Stronghold quest he was too tired to want.
