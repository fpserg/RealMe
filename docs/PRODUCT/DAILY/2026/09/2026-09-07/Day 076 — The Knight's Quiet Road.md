# Day 076 — The Knight's Quiet Road

The rain stayed.

But unlike yesterday, it did not own the whole day.

Owling slept longer. The Warden felt a little better. Stronghold remained wet and demanding, but the Household found a workable division of the battlefield: Oksi advanced the planting campaign outside, heroically working through rain and illness, while the Warden maintained the defence indoors with the children.

Owling, naturally, was a storm of his own.

Somewhere between weather and childcare the Warden even found time to dig more pits for flowers and trees. Stronghold continued to grow by small increments despite conditions that seemed designed to stop it.

Then came less pleasant news from another part of the Household Realm.

Sergey Jr had been accepted at School 444 by teachers and management, but apparently not yet by the rules. At six years and four months, he is reported to be two months short of the formal age at which he can be admitted. What had seemed settled therefore became uncertain again, and Oksi acquired another thing to worry about.

By evening the family returned to Capital. Owling went to bed. The day might reasonably have ended there.

Instead, Oksi discovered that her bag with the documents was still at Stronghold.

And so the Warden went back.

One hundred and thirty kilometres. Two and a half hours there and back. A noble knight riding through the evening on a treasure quest for his fair lady — except the treasure was a forgotten bag and the steed had an odometer.

It was ridiculous logistics.

It was also quiet.

For the second day in a row, an errand that should have been merely inconvenient became something restorative. Yesterday it was fuel, water and a pharmacy. Today it was the road back to Stronghold. Two and a half hours in which nobody needed to be contained, planted, reassured or put to bed.

The knight had his quest.

And, almost accidentally, he had the road to himself.
