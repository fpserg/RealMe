# Day 098 — No Empty Spaces

Morning Serpent.

Navruz.

Saturday.

Booked.

Sergey the Landscaper.

Basement.

A couple of things to improve.

A solution, probably.

Wait for the next arrival.

Then office.

Three Strategy calls.

The calendar filled.

But everything outside the calendar kept happening too.

InfoLINE called again.

The Warden was angry about the way they had chosen to pursue the issue.

But one fact did not need defending.

The team had used data it did not have permission to use.

That had to be dealt with.

Take the VseInstrumenty report down.

Immediately.

Then investigate.

Alexander received the next quest.

Find the quoted data elsewhere.

Open reports.

Presentations.

Media.

Anything public.

Reconstruct how much had actually crossed the line and how much could independently be found in the open.

Not a comfortable task.

But a concrete one.

Meanwhile, another stream of information arrived.

Tables.

Chairs.

Lamps.

Oksi wanted decisions.

The Warden suspected tactical timing.

Ask him during an extremely busy workday.

Perhaps he will simply approve everything.

A formidable procurement strategy.

Then home.

Maths again.

Alisa had received two 2s.

A sharp reversal after all those 5s.

She did not know.

Oksi had seen them in the electronic diary.

She told her.

Alisa burst into tears.

That surprised everyone.

Alisa had never seemed to care much about good marks.

Apparently, today she did.

Oksi comforted her.

Gently.

Then they made a small plan.

Multiplication table.

Learn it properly.

No grand educational doctrine required.

Just the next thing to do.

Then Owling.

Walk.

Bed.

The Great Battle.

He was exhausted.

He also could not stop jumping.

Then the wall stopped him.

A bump.

Tears.

The Warden's victory suddenly did not feel like victory at all.

Fortunately the crying passed.

Owling settled.

Then slept peacefully.

Late dinner.

Steak.

A huge watermelon.

Too huge, perhaps.

Overripe.

Sirius walk.

And then another conversation about maths.

This time Jr.

Oksi wanted harder Olympiad courses.

Far away.

Jr already had School 57 preliminaries.

The Warden could see another year of schedules becoming campaigns.

He was not convinced.

Oksi tends to push herself hard.

And sometimes everyone around her too.

Was that right for Jr?

The Warden did not know.

He tried to reason with her.

Lightly.

No need to turn a question about a child's schedule into a battle between parents.

No decision was made.

Then another workday began.

And the Strategy calls consumed all of it.

No empty spaces.

Naturally, that was when the fence installation started.

Calls inside.

Fence outside.

The Warden tried to operate both worlds at once.

Home again.

Oksi felt better.

Still tired.

The children transformed the simple act of getting ready for a walk into chaos.

Oksi shouted.

Then they finally escaped outside.

Alisa and Jr had news.

An urgent deficiency had been discovered in the Household.

Butter squishies.

All their classmates had them.

Therefore, obviously, they needed them.

Please.

Please.

The Warden listened.

He did not tell them.

He had already ordered the squishies.

They were waiting at delivery.

Sometimes being one step ahead of the children is enough.

While they walked, Oksi performed another small miracle.

Pasta with meatballs appeared.

A proper dinner in the middle of everything.

Even Owling approved.

Then night.

Fuel Quest.

The Warden had learned not to expect these to be simple.

But this one was.

A station almost hidden in the park.

Nearly deserted.

Old-fashioned.

Trees around it.

The sort of place that looked as though the modern city had grown around it and somehow forgotten to remove it.

The Warden pulled in.

Fuel.

No queue.

No drama.

Done.

Two days had been filled with simultaneous campaigns.

Clients.

Data claims.

Investigations.

Furniture.

School marks.

Olympiad maths.

Owling.

Fence installers.

Walks.

Dinner.

Fuel.

Everything had tried to occupy the same space.

And then, in the darkness among the trees, one task finally had the courtesy to remain exactly one task.

The Warden filled the tank.

And drove home.
