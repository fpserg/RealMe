## Reflection

Day 098 was governed by concurrency.

Strategy calls occupied the calendar, but the rest of life did not queue politely behind them. InfoLINE required a containment decision and a forensic follow-up. Fence installers arrived. Oksi sent furniture choices into the gaps. Navruz and the landscapers acquired their next instructions.

At home, two school questions appeared in different forms. Alisa's two poor maths marks mattered to her far more than either parent expected, and Oksi responded with comfort and a concrete multiplication-table plan. With Sergey Jr, the question ran in the opposite direction: not how to recover from a setback, but how much additional difficulty should be added to a schedule that already includes School 57 preliminaries. That question remained open.

The children also supplied lighter evidence of school belonging. Alisa and Jr urgently wanted the same butter squishies their classmates had, unaware that the Warden had already ordered them.

The day ended away from the calls, claims and construction: a quick nighttime fuel stop at an almost deserted station among the trees.

A rare quest that did not become another campaign.
