# Day 089 — The Neighbours Acquire Names

Owling began the day at six in the morning.

So did the Warden.

Stronghold was awake.

The first expedition was for supplies.

Oksi was still feeling unwell, so the Warden bought lemon and sweets.

Sergey Jr had left his phone charger at Capital.

Another charger.

Small household logistics.

Then the unfinished kitchen returned to the agenda.

Oksi wanted to know when the assembler would come back.

She insisted that the Warden call.

Then took his phone herself.

Oksi wanted to be in charge of everything.

Stronghold had other visitors coming.

Sergey the Landscaper arrived to discuss the next land works.

One important piece was no longer a future tense.

The drainage trench was done.

Alexey came too, to discuss what Kadyr still had to do.

And, naturally, what the Warden still had to pay.

The grass needed attention.

Local lawnmowers were summoned.

Watching another payment attach itself to another recurring Stronghold task produced a conclusion.

Buy a mower.

Sooner or later, ownership would be cheaper.

Then the nursery.

Again.

Oksi and the Warden went out, and Owling discovered something valuable.

A piece of plastic pipe.

To an adult, construction debris.

To Owling, an Artifact.

He fiercely refused to leave it behind.

So the Artifact came home.

On the way back, the Warden finally turned toward another place that had been sitting on the mental map for some time.

A spring in the forest.

The path was rough.

The water was cold and refreshing.

Perhaps tomorrow's Supplies Quest could include spring water.

Another point on the map had become a real place.

Later the Warden walked Owling again.

And finally met the mysterious owner of the Baba Yaga hut and the Wooden Guardian.

Mikhail.

They talked about children.

The Wooden Guardian, it turned out, had been carved by Mikhail himself.

Mystery converted into neighbour.

While they were talking, another man approached.

Did the Warden have any work?

Not immediately.

But plumbing and water works would be coming.

The Warden took his number.

Mansur.

Another name entered the local world.

Then a loss.

Halfway to the playground, the Warden discovered that Owling's soother was missing.

He walked the route there and back.

Nothing.

Perhaps this was the price of acquiring two helpful acquaintances in one walk.

A sacrifice.

To Mikhail and Mansur.

Or perhaps to the Wooden Guardian.

Later, passing the football field, the answer appeared.

There lay Owling's soother.

Nearby, Alisa and Sergey Jr were playing peacefully.

No sacrifice had been required.

The older children had been expanding their world too.

Alisa met a new girl.

Adelina.

She went to Adelina's house.

Tea and sandwiches.

Sergey Jr joined later.

The Stronghold neighbourhood was no longer merely a place where the children accompanied their parents.

They were beginning to have places and people of their own.

Before Owling's bedtime, the Warden dug a few more garden pits while the middles watched him.

Then evening.

Alisa and Sergey Jr had showtime.

Pirates of the Caribbean.

Appropriate.

One of Oksi's RealMe names, after all, is Jack Sparrow.

Day 089 had the shape of an expedition without ever leaving the neighbourhood.

A spring became a visited spring.

The mysterious hut owner became Mikhail.

The Wooden Guardian acquired a maker.

A stranger looking for work became Mansur with a phone number.

Adelina became Alisa's new friend.

A piece of plastic pipe became an Artifact.

Even the missing soother went out into the World and returned.

Stronghold itself had been acquiring rooms, furniture, trenches, plants and fences.

Now something else was happening.

Its surroundings were acquiring names.

A place becomes home not only when the house is finished.

It becomes home when the neighbours stop being mysterious.
