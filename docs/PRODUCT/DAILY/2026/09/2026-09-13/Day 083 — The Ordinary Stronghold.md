# Day 083 — The Ordinary Stronghold

The morning began with what was becoming a regular Stronghold procedure.

Play with Owling.

Wait until the volume rises beyond what the house can comfortably absorb.

Then leave on a supplies quest.

By the time the Warden and Owling were returning, Oksi had woken. The first expedition therefore flowed directly into a second: soil and a few more plants.

Stronghold had once demanded quests for doors, pipes, water and emergency repairs.

Now it demanded soil.

Sergey the Landscaper arrived late again.

There were still serious pieces of the old construction campaign to resolve. The Warden discussed the basement cover and drainage with him. But Owling was tired, and his objections to continuing the meeting became increasingly impossible to ignore.

The Warden left in the middle of the conversation to put him to sleep.

Oksi sorted out the rest.

The older children had their own Stronghold world now.

Alisa and Sergey Jr were playing with Roma at the football field when some of Roma's older friends appeared. They began making fun of him for playing with girls. Alisa asked Roma whether she should leave.

He said yes.

She came home in tears.

The Warden called Sergey Jr back for lunch so that she would not have to sit with the rejection alone.

Lunch passed.

Then both of them went back.

The football field had hurt, but it had not become forbidden territory.

Meanwhile Owling's sleep created one of Stronghold's familiar temporary work windows. The Warden took the opportunity and dug new flower pits.

Eventually it was time to return home.

Traffic was heavy on the regular route. The Warden looked at it and made a tactical decision: take a curve around the congestion.

It was the wrong decision.

The alternative route delivered the family to a railway crossing, where they spent almost an hour waiting.

They reached home late, with school waiting the next morning.

The Warden switched immediately into recovery mode: children to the shower, children to bed, restore the weekday schedule as much as possible.

But someone had been operating at home while they were away.

Maksim.

He had prepared a surprise.

Salad.

Apples, neatly cut.

And pancakes he had made himself.

His first pancakes.

They were good.

The Warden was genuinely surprised. He could not have done that at Maksim's age.

In fact, he still could not.

It was a small domestic achievement, but perhaps that was precisely why it landed so well after a day made of small frictions. Nobody had assigned Maksim a campaign. There was no contractor, no deadline and no repair involved.

He had simply decided to make something for the family and succeeded.

One final logistical obligation remained.

Fuel was running low.

The Warden went out late for another fuel quest, prepared for the possibility that the familiar shortages and queues had returned.

They had not.

The nearby station had fuel.

There was no queue.

Done.

Day 083 had contained no great threshold like the kitchen delivery the day before. The basement was still unfinished. The landscaper was still late. A child came home crying. A clever traffic detour failed spectacularly.

Yet almost everything was absorbed by ordinary family machinery.

Oksi finished the conversation when the Warden had to leave. Sergey Jr came home when Alisa needed company. Alisa and Sergey Jr went back when they were ready. The Warden dug while Owling slept. Maksim cooked while everyone was away. Fuel was simply there when it was needed.

Stronghold was becoming ordinary.

And after everything required to build it, ordinary was beginning to look like an achievement.
