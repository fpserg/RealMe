# Day 077 — Where the Next Move Belongs

The Warden woke on the floor beside Owling's bed.

At some point in the middle of the night Owling had awakened, been rocked back to sleep, and the Warden had apparently surrendered the remaining distance to his own bed. Morning arrived anyway. The children went to school.

Career was waiting too, and unusually impatiently. Two short deadlines meant that every day mattered.

But when the Warden returned home, Oksi was sick and facing Sergey Jr's School Admission Quest. Taking Owling with her felt impossible. So the Warden stayed.

It was a real cost to Career, but not a complete stop. Artem became the hands. The Warden remained the judgment.

Oddly, the School Admission Quest itself demanded little from Oksi. The school staff told her that the current step was on them. The problem was no longer something she could solve by pushing harder, which did not make it emotionally easier. She wanted movement and could not produce it herself.

The Warden offered a RealMe answer: calm down and wait while the externally controlled step resolves.

Later he took Owling and Sergey Jr outside. At the playground they found Alisa with a classmate. She had asked to walk on her own after school — another small piece of independence appearing while the adults were occupied with the machinery of admission.

Career returned through Artem.

The Strategy report draft arrived. The new part was fine. But in the older argument the Warden found something more dangerous than unfinished prose: reasoning that had become too convenient.

The report argued that the market's equity risk premium was extremely high. That could explain depressed valuations neatly. Too neatly. The market might also be pricing lower future EPS. If investors saw that immediately while the report did not, the thesis would be vulnerable at precisely the point where it was supposed to be strongest.

So the Warden did not do the manual rewrite himself. He diagnosed the weakness and sent Artem an action plan.

That was the pattern of the day.

At School 444, the next move belonged to the school. More Household effort could not manufacture the missing step.

In Career, the next move belonged to Artem. But only after the Warden determined what move needed to be made.

Knowing when to act is useful.

Knowing when the next move belongs to someone else may be just as important.
