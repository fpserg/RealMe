# Day 084 — Before the Verdict

Equity Strategy was almost there.

The Warden was finalizing it while, alongside it, compiling the Platform Economy report. Two large Career campaigns occupied the desk at once, but both had moved beyond their earlier exploratory stages. The work now was increasingly about bringing pieces together.

Then another October piece appeared on the calendar.

A new IPO was coming.

Promomed was also planning a one-to-two-day trip to its plant.

The dates appeared to overlap.

That was the sort of collision better discovered while calendars were still soft, so the Warden checked with Alexey of ECM. The first answer was reassuring: the plant trip probably did not fall on an actual IPO milestone.

A one-day trip could be fine.

Not resolved yet. But probably manageable.

Another unresolved question had been occupying the Warden for longer.

MTS dividends.

He called a fellow investor and walked him through the thesis.

The investor found it useful.

That was encouraging, but it did not answer the question the Warden actually found interesting.

Was he right?

Or was the market?

The company would eventually provide the evidence. Until then, the disagreement itself remained part of the attraction.

Stronghold had its own next test approaching.

The Warden called fence manufacturers.

They would come on Sunday to measure the land plot.

That was a milestone.

Oksi had been talking about the fence for a long time. Earlier it could still feel like another expensive item on the endless Stronghold list. But the logic had strengthened as the house changed. There was more equipment inside now. More things in the garden. More of the place had become finished enough to protect.

The Fence Campaign was moving from conversation toward dimensions, scope and eventually price.

In the evening Sergey Jr went to football.

Something had changed there too.

He now had a branded shirt from his own team.

So instead of wearing Ronaldo's shirt, he wore his own.

Owling, meanwhile, found a different object worthy of complete attention: a plastic pipe protruding from the fence.

Everyone has his own symbols.

By night, the Warden and Oksi were exhausted.

Usually that condition did not necessarily mean sleep. There was often another report, another household problem, another Night Watch, another hour that could still be extracted from the day.

Not tonight.

They went to bed before midnight.

For the first time in a long while.

Day 084 did not deliver many verdicts.

The IPO collision looked manageable, but dates still had to settle. The MTS thesis had survived a peer conversation, but the market had not yet rendered judgment. The fence had a measurement date, but no specification or price yet. Strategy was being finalized, not celebrated as finished.

Yet the next tests were now visible.

Sometimes progress is not knowing the answer.

It is getting the question into the right place to be answered.
