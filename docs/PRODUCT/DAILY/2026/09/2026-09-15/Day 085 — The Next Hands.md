# Day 085 — The Next Hands

The Warden changed reports.

Equity Strategy was still alive, but it no longer needed to occupy the center of the desk. Artem was making the final changes. Production and compliance had the report.

So the Warden switched to Platform Economy.

That transition mattered after weeks in which Strategy had repeatedly demanded another revision, another argument, another deadline. It was not finished in the absolute sense. Corrections could still return. Client calls still lay ahead.

But the next hands were on it.

Another Career campaign was also becoming more structured.

The analyst rating.

The Warden spoke with Mikhail, Head of Sales, and they decided on an enrollment strategy. What had previously been a collection of coordination tasks now had an approach.

The next hands were being positioned there too.

Stronghold was preparing for a more literal handoff.

Kitchen assemblers were coming on Thursday.

The Warden arranged for Kadyr to be there as well, ready to move the kitchen things when the assemblers needed space. The kitchen had arrived; now the choreography around installation had to work.

At home, Stronghold announced another requirement without saying a word.

New plants.

A whole set of them.

Another night expedition.

But first there was Oksi.

She was tired and sad.

The Warden took Owling and Sergey Jr for a walk, carrying some of the evening away from her for a while.

Then came bedtime for the children.

After that, the Warden and Oksi got into the car and set out for Stronghold.

The Warden had a headache on the way.

Still, the trip itself was smooth. No fuel queue. No railway crossing trap. No burst pipe waiting at the destination. Plants went where plants needed to go, and the two of them came back.

Meanwhile another old Stronghold campaign was approaching a threshold.

Drainage and basement works were about to start with Sergey the Landscaper.

For a long time those words had belonged to the category of discussions, unanswered messages, future visits and unresolved plans.

Now they were approaching physical work.

Day 085 contained little spectacle.

Instead, several things advanced because the Warden no longer needed to hold every next action personally.

Artem could finish Strategy changes.

Production and compliance could carry the report.

Mikhail could help shape the rating campaign.

Kadyr could support the assemblers.

Sergey the Landscaper could begin the drainage and basement work.

Even at home, when Oksi had little energy left, the Warden could take two children outside and temporarily carry that part of the evening.

The Warden's own attention moved where it was needed next.

From Strategy to Platform.

From home to Stronghold and back.

From one campaign to another.

Not every advance requires the Warden to make the final move himself.

Sometimes continuity means making sure the next hands are ready.
