# Day 090 — The Border Moves

Owling woke at 6:15.

A schedule was emerging.

Unfortunately, it was Owling's schedule.

He made enough noise to wake everyone else in Stronghold.

The resulting looks were directed largely at the Warden.

So the Warden grabbed the little troublemaker and retreated.

Supplies Quest.

This time there was a new mission.

Water.

Yesterday the forest spring had been discovered.

Today it had to be harvested.

The path ran through mist and hills.

Birch roots crossed the slope like stairs.

Owling rode on the Warden's shoulders.

The Warden carried the canister.

They reached the spring.

Filled it.

Carried it back.

A full canister.

A quest completed.

The Warden hoped the water would justify the journey.

But then again.

Water is water.

The canister went into the car.

The next mission was about to begin when a man with a dog waved.

The Warden stopped.

What did he want?

A spare cigarette.

In exchange, the man made his dog wave a paw at Owling.

Owling looked puzzled.

Stronghold's surrounding world continued to produce encounters with no obvious quest logic.

Then fruit.

Oksi was still unwell.

Owling's nose was running.

Alisa was beginning to feel unwell too.

Bananas for Owling.

Apples for Alisa.

Peaches because they looked good.

Back at Stronghold, the guilds arrived.

Sergey the Landscaper brought his people.

Strawberry patches.

Pits for peach trees.

The garden was acquiring intended geography rather than simply accumulating holes.

Almost at the same time came Konstantin the Fence Measurer.

Measurements.

Calculations.

And finally a timetable.

The fence should begin in a week.

A week after that, it should be finished.

For weeks the fence had been an argument between protection and Treasury.

Now it was becoming construction.

A long-awaited milestone.

With one casualty.

The Hare.

Once the border closes, the Hare will no longer be able to wander onto the plot.

Stronghold gains a wall.

Its wild visitor loses access.

Then another border moved in the opposite direction.

The information had actually arrived yesterday but had escaped the Chronicle.

Oksi had met Adelina's mother.

Yulia.

A neighbour.

And Yulia's sister owns the plot immediately beside Stronghold.

She may be thinking of selling it.

Captain Sparrow understood the strategic implications instantly.

Territorial expansion.

Another province.

More garden.

More Stronghold.

The Treasury, presumably, understood a different set of implications.

For now there was no sale.

No price.

No commitment.

Only an agreement.

If Yulia's sister decides to sell, the Warden and Captain Sparrow hear about it before anyone else.

An option.

Nothing more.

After the guilds left, Owling went to sleep.

The Warden considered the obvious next steps.

Nap.

Perhaps lunch.

Captain Sparrow had another interpretation of his commitments.

Garden.

An argument followed.

The Warden returned to the pits.

Then broke a shovel.

By accident, naturally.

Evidence had now been physically produced that garden labour was taking place.

The Warden retired for his deserved nap.

After sleep and lunch came something increasingly recognizable.

An ordinary Stronghold Sunday.

Oksi in the garden.

The children helping her put the cut grass into bags.

The Warden moving between Owling and the garden.

Work without a campaign name.

Family without an event.

Then evening.

The long drive back.

Late arrival.

Tired, as always.

Day 090 moved two borders.

One border is about to close.

Konstantin measured it, and soon the fence will rise around Stronghold.

The Hare will remain outside.

The other border may someday move outward.

One neighbouring plot.

One possible seller.

One Captain Sparrow already imagining expanded territories.

Nothing has happened there yet.

But the possibility now exists.

Stronghold spent much of its history trying to become inhabitable.

Now it is beginning to ask a different question.

Where does it end?
