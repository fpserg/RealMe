# Day 097 — The Enduring Mission

07:30 again.

The Warden liked this new arrangement.

Five or six had been considerably worse.

Owling, however, had apparently made no corresponding commitment to conserve the Warden's energy.

The morning mission began.

Navruz arrived at Stronghold.

There was water to connect in the kitchen.

A sink to connect in the bathroom.

Two campaigns that had spent a long time being almost finished could move another step toward ordinary life.

Then Owling slept.

Briefly.

The clock started.

Lunch.

Quick.

Then outside.

Bricks still lay beneath the pine.

The Warden carried them to the shed.

There is a particular economy to life with Owling now.

A sleeping child is not merely rest.

It is a temporary production window.

Eat.

Move bricks.

Do whatever fits.

Because soon—

Owling was awake.

The enduring mission resumed.

Navruz had left for another order, but he returned.

And this time there was a result that could be tested immediately.

Turn the tap.

Water in the kitchen.

Turn another.

Water in the bathroom sink.

After all the campaigns, contractors, delays and unfinished edges, water simply came out where water was supposed to come out.

Progress.

Stronghold acknowledged this achievement in its customary way.

By leaking somewhere else.

Another leak appeared in the first-floor bathroom.

And the Royal Bathroom sink still had things left to do.

So Navruz's campaign was not actually over.

Next weekend.

Another visit.

Another round.

Back home, Household physics produced one final task.

Oksi accidentally broke the toilet-seat cover.

Nothing profound.

Nothing symbolic required.

Just another line for the ledger.

Buy.

Replace.

Yesterday the Warden had stood beside Sanya's coffin and spoken across twenty-five years.

Then he had walked home under the full moon with an empty wheelbarrow.

Today there was no such weight.

There was simply life after it.

Owling demanding energy.

Bricks demanding relocation.

Pipes demanding connection.

Leaks demanding repair.

A toilet seat demanding replacement.

The Kitchen campaign moved forward.

The Bathroom campaign moved forward.

Stronghold remained unfinished because Stronghold is lived in.

And the Warden's longest campaign of the day had no contractor, no completion certificate and no final checkbox.

Owling woke.

The enduring mission went on.
