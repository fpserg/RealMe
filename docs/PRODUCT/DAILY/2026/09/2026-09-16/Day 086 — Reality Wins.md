# Day 086 — Reality Wins

The verdict arrived.

For several days, the Warden had been waiting to discover whether his MTS dividend thesis or the market's expectations were closer to what the company would actually do.

Now there was no need to wonder.

The Warden was wrong.

MTS kept total shareholder distribution unchanged.

But it changed the mechanism.

Instead of directing the full amount into dividends, the new policy allocated 70% to dividends and 30% to buybacks.

DPS would therefore be lower.

There had been something enjoyable about carrying a contrarian thesis while the answer remained hidden. The Warden had modeled it, discussed it with Sales and pressure-tested it with another investor.

Then the company published the answer.

That was that.

The Warden went to the stock exchange for the MTS presentation.

There was not much more substance beyond what had already been announced. Some clarifications. Some networking. Useful context, but no secret second interpretation that restored the old thesis.

Back to the office.

Rewrite the MTS section.

Strategy was still passing through its final corrections, and the new policy had arrived late enough to require another one. At the same time, retail brokerage tensions needed sorting out.

Reality had changed one paragraph, but the rest of the working day did not stop around it.

Neither did home.

Oksi was totally exhausted.

Owling was in a wild mood.

So the Warden took Owling and went to collect Sergey Jr from football.

On the way back, father and son discussed the habitat of Amur tigers.

Far East forests entered the evening somewhere between football and an overtired toddler.

At home there was another Stronghold acquisition.

Oksi had brought a new set of garden furniture: a table and chairs.

The garden that only recently had been pits, soil, weeds, plants and unfinished work was gaining somewhere to sit.

Then night arrived.

Owling disagreed.

For him, apparently, it was daytime.

The Night Watch consumed almost everything that should have been sleep. By the time Owling finally yielded enough for the Warden to lie down, only a very short nap remained before morning.

Day 086 had begun with a thesis meeting its answer and ended with night meeting a child who refused to recognize it.

Only one of those disagreements could be resolved by evidence.

The MTS thesis had been useful precisely because it could fail. It generated analysis, conversations and a clear expectation. Then the company announced something else.

No reinterpretation was needed.

The model had to change.

The Warden returned to the office and changed it.

That is part of research too.

Reality wins.
