# Day 088 — What the Thesis Was About

The Warden had been wrong about MTS.

That was already established.

The dividend policy had not delivered the higher DPS he had expected. Instead, MTS kept total shareholder distribution unchanged while redirecting 30% into buybacks.

Yesterday, reality had won.

Today came the next question.

What exactly had been disproved?

The Warden was finalizing Strategy and had to decide what to do with MTS.

The easy reaction would have been to retreat.

The dividend call was wrong. Sentiment was weaker. Lower DPS was visible and simple to explain.

Remove it from the top picks.

But the Warden went back to the investment case.

The distribution had changed form.

His fair value had not changed.

So MTS stayed.

The failed dividend forecast and the stock recommendation were related, but they were not the same thesis.

There was no reason to surrender one merely because the other had failed.

Strategy moved closer to completion.

So did Platform Economy.

After the latest round of substantive work, the Warden handed the Platform report to Artem for formatting over the weekend.

Sorry, buddy.

That's IB.

Another part of Career was becoming more transactional in the ordinary sense.

A fellow broker was interested in FabricaONE bonds and in Polyus after its dividend cancellation.

The Warden arranged a call with colleagues who could help.

And then asked him to vote for the team in the upcoming analyst contest.

The enrollment strategy discussed with Mikhail was no longer an abstract plan.

It had entered conversations.

Then home.

Oksi was tired and in a bad mood.

Friday again.

The drive to Stronghold was nervous.

The half-finished kitchen did not improve the arrival.

The assemblers had left their mess behind, while the car breakdown meant the work itself was still incomplete.

So the Warden cleaned.

Not the symbolic version.

The actual mess.

Gradually the construction site receded.

The family settled.

The evening slowed.

Stronghold began becoming Stronghold again.

Then, right beside the plot, a hare appeared.

A small wild visitor at the edge of the increasingly inhabited garden.

Oksi slowly calmed.

The kitchen was still unfinished.

Nothing about the hare fixed it.

Nothing about the calmer evening erased the nervous drive.

But the unfinished thing stopped occupying the whole field of view.

That was also true of MTS.

One part of the thesis had failed.

It did not need to occupy the whole investment case.

Day 088 was about deciding what new information actually changes.

Sometimes it changes everything.

Sometimes it changes one line in the model.

The work is knowing which.
