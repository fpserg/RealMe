# Day 093 — Bright Green

Owling did not wake.

After the night before, that counted as a gift.

The Warden moved quietly.

Wake the other children.

Help them prepare for school.

Let Oksi sleep.

Sergey Jr had his own disaster.

The cap from his pen was gone.

He was upset.

The Warden promised to bring another one from work.

A solvable problem.

Then Career began.

Four Strategy client calls.

Almost the entire day.

Almost no breaks.

Only days earlier the Strategy report had been something the team was still making.

Now the Warden spent his day talking about it.

This was what marketing looked like.

Not an abstract next phase.

Hours on calls.

Questions.

Arguments.

Clients.

And almost no empty space between them.

Somewhere inside that schedule the Tower dividend report was supposed to fit.

Somehow.

It mostly did not.

Oksi kept writing.

She was angry that the Warden had gone to work.

Angry that he was not replying.

The Warden wished circumstances were different.

But the client calls were continuous.

There was no physical space to conduct both conversations at once.

Eventually he came home.

Oksi was still angry.

Then she went to her manicure.

The Warden had another journey.

Stronghold.

Again.

The night road was becoming ordinary.

This time the cargo was bags of soil and three little fir trees.

But there was another reason to go.

The kitchen.

Assembly had finished yesterday.

After all the delays.

The delivery.

The half-built kitchen.

The assembler's broken car.

The uncertainty about when he would return.

Finished.

The Warden walked in and saw it.

Bright green.

Oksi's favourite colour.

His too.

And it was very nice.

More than nice.

It changed the room.

Stronghold immediately felt cozier.

The kitchen had spent weeks as a Campaign.

Now it was simply part of the house.

There was another retrospective threshold to record.

Sunday.

Tower had launched City of Heroes.

The Warden had waited for it in two different roles.

As a player, because he wanted to know what the game would actually be like.

As a finance person, because virtual worlds have economies too.

The first impression was encouraging.

Players had another reason to spend what they earned.

A sink.

Something that might help the economy circulate rather than merely accumulate.

The Warden would watch.

That was what happened when anticipation ended.

You stopped imagining the thing.

You started observing it.

The same was true of Strategy.

The same was true of the kitchen.

A report became client calls.

A game became an economy.

A kitchen became a home.

The Warden loaded the road behind him again.

Back from Stronghold.

Late.

Tired.

But somewhere behind him, under the night, stood a house with three little fir trees waiting outside.

And inside it, finally, a bright-green kitchen.
