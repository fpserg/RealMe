# Day 137 — Which Way Is Home?

Morning at Stronghold.

The Warden waits for Oksi to wake. There is a long journey ahead, and the day's obligations are scattered across the map.

Azamat is coming to connect the Internet. The family will be away. Kadyr is working at Aleksey's, and the Warden does not want Alisa left alone to manage the installation.

An arrangement is made: Aleksey will receive a call thirty minutes before Azamat needs to work inside.

Then Oksi wakes. Jr and Comandante are ready.

They leave.

***

The birthday venue is reached on time, but the journey is not quite over.

They need cash for a present. There must be an ATM somewhere.

The surrounding site is under construction, and finding a way through takes time. They locate the machine, then the room where the children are gathering for a cooking master class.

Jr arrives a little late.

The Warden leaves him among his friends and returns to Oksi and Comandante.

Next: the ceramics warehouse outside Moscow.

***

The warehouse database is not working.

The workers must find the order by hand.

After the previous day's trouble with a different warehouse, the Warden has reason to be wary. But this time the search is mercifully short.

Five small boxes are loaded into the car.

Was all this travelling worth it?

There is still time before Jr must be collected.

Oksi asks to go home.

The Warden turns toward Stronghold.

***

Halfway there, Oksi remarks that she did not know this was the quickest way to Capital.

The Warden looks at her in surprise.

He thought that when she said *home*, she meant Stronghold.

But Capital makes more sense for the birthday pickup.

A U-turn. A change of destination.

The word remains between them.

***

At Capital, Oksi goes to shower and the Warden looks after Comandante.

Aleksey calls about the Internet installer. He and Kadyr have been waiting.

The Warden phones Azamat, who says he is almost finished. Alisa has already let him inside.

The waiting men are no longer needed.

Then another call comes.

Julia, the neighbour at Stronghold, wants to speak with them.

She does not say why.

But the Warden remembers an agreement: if Julia ever decided to sell her land, she would contact Oksi first.

He thinks this might be the reason.

When Oksi emerges from the shower, he tells her immediately.

It is something she has dreamed of.

There is no confirmation yet. Only a phone call and a possibility.

***

The Warden returns to collect Jr.

At the venue, the hostess looks at him.

She recognises whose father he must be. Jr looks so much like him.

Then she praises the boy.

The Warden feels proud.

He collects Jr, returns to the car, and sets off for Stronghold again.

***

At Stronghold, Julia's lights are on. Her cars are there.

The Warden calls, but she does not answer.

The conversation will have to wait.

There is a quick dinner.

Then the children watch a film using the new Wi-Fi.

The Internet works.

After a full day on the road, everyone goes to bed.

***

Five boxes have crossed the region. A birthday has been attended. The Internet is finally alive.

But the day's most curious discovery was made in a car headed the wrong way.

For the Warden, *home* had already become Stronghold.

For Oksi, in that moment, it was still Capital.

And somewhere next door, behind a lit window and an unanswered telephone, there may be another piece of the future.

No one knows yet.
