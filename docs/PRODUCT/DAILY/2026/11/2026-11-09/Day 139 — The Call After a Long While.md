# Day 139 — The Call After a Long While

Comandante wakes at five.

He plays while the household is still gathering itself for the day.

Around seven, he falls from his bed.

His head strikes the floor.

For a moment, the ordinary morning becomes frightening.

The Warden comforts him. Comandante settles and sleeps peacefully.

Everything seems all right.

The Warden hopes it is.

***

At work, the week ahead looks crowded.

Three earnings calls, investor days, the ordinary demands of the office.

The T-Cian deal is still being discussed. A former colleague, now at Avito, exchanges views with the Warden and suggests breakfast. Thursday might offer a window.

The Warden writes the substantive part of a Cian one-pager and sends it to Artem for the numbers.

Another piece of work moves forward.

***

At Stronghold, Kadyr intends to begin the kitchen ceramics.

Some materials are missing. He must find them first.

There are also options for exterior decorations to consider with Oksi.

The house advances by small dependencies: one missing supply, one decision, one call at a time.

***

Then Oksi calls.

Jr must be collected from School 444 and taken to 57.

Comandante has only just fallen asleep. Neither Maksim nor Alisa is home to watch over him if he wakes.

The Warden calls Vasya, but Vasya cannot help.

The morning's fall is still in the Warden's mind.

Oksi calls Maksim and asks him to miss his last lesson and hurry home.

She sets off for Jr.

In the end, the timing works.

Oksi and Jr return just as Maksim arrives.

Comandante is still peacefully asleep.

***

A little spare time appears.

The Warden calls his father.

It has been a long while.

They speak of Gazan, the holidays at Stronghold, the progress of construction, and the housing possibilities that have vanished and appeared.

There is much to tell.

Both are glad to hear the other's voice.

***

Later, the Warden collects Jr from 57.

At home, Oksi has her Spanish lesson, so he takes over Comandante.

The boy is noisy, busy, and entirely unwilling to go for a walk.

He is behaving like himself.

At last, the Warden feels calmer about the morning.

***

The day began with the sound of a fall and a father's fear.

It passed through reports, calls, school journeys and arrangements that almost failed.

But it also held a conversation long overdue, and the simple comfort of a familiar, stubborn child.

Not every important thing in a busy week belongs to the calendar.
