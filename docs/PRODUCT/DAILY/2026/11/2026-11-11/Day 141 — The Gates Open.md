# Day 141 — The Gates Open

Andrey sends a link to a conference on the Platform Economy.

The instruction is to attend.

It lasts all day.

The Warden listens through one earbud while trying to carry on with everything else. Neither task gets quite the attention it needs.

Then something catches his ear.

The head of Sber speaks of an economy moving beyond platforms and toward agents.

The Warden wonders whether the report his team has been preparing must now be reconsidered.

A long day of listening has yielded at least one unsettling question.

***

Meanwhile, Oksi is sending messages.

She is exhausted.

The Warden feels sorry for her, and irritated too. He is at work and cannot change the situation from where he sits.

The messages reach him anyway.

***

After work, he collects Oksi and Comandante.

They hurry to a private kindergarten nearby.

Perhaps this could ease the burden.

But Comandante is too young.

Another possibility closes.

***

There are decorations to collect.

The new set is so large it barely fits inside the car.

They will have to take it to Stronghold tonight.

First, however, Comandante must go to sleep.

He takes longer than the Warden would like.

The road is waiting.

Eventually the boy sleeps, the preparations are made, and they set off.

***

During the drive, Oksi becomes calmer.

She apologizes for being rough.

She knows how she has been behaving, she says, but exhaustion leaves her little room to manage it differently.

The Warden listens.

They continue toward Stronghold.

***

At the gates, something has changed.

The electric drive-in gates work.

Roman has made them operational.

The entrance opens as it should.

Inside, they unload the decorations.

One of them is badly bent.

It cannot be used.

After the late drive, this is particularly unwelcome. The Warden fears it will spoil the visit for Oksi.

The house has gained working gates and received a broken ornament on the same night.

***

On the way back, Oksi speaks about her health and state of mind.

The road is long enough for reflection.

They arrive late.

Maksim is watching over the sleeping children.

There is little left to do but go to bed.

***

The conference had offered a vision of agents taking over the work of platforms.

At home, no such transformation has arrived.

There are still children to settle, journeys to make, heavy decorations to carry, and tired people trying to find room for one another.

Yet at Stronghold, the gates finally open by themselves.

A small piece of the future is working.
