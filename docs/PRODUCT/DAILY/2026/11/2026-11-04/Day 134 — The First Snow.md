# Day 134 — The First Snow

Stronghold wakes beneath snow.

Only yesterday, the family arrived to find the new sofa waiting.

This morning, the landscape has changed.

It is cold. Comandante has a runny nose. The supplies are sufficient, and the car still wears summer tyres.

There is little reason to go shopping.

***

They try a walk.

The Middles argue, then argue again.

The Warden's patience gives way. He intervenes sharply and takes everyone home.

Comandante's health is a real reason to keep the outing short.

It is also a reason no one can dispute.

***

After lunch, Comandante refuses to sleep.

Perhaps a drive will help.

The Warden sets out for baby milk, imagining a quiet journey with the little commander.

Oksi and Jr come too.

The expedition is less peaceful than he had hoped.

Back at Stronghold, the garden offers no refuge. The cold keeps everyone indoors.

Comandante remains lively.

The Warden grows tired.

***

Oksi's Spanish lesson is at six.

Just before it begins, Comandante finally sleeps.

So does the Warden.

When he wakes, the lesson is over.

Oksi is angry. The Middles, it seems, have spoiled the hour she had set aside for herself.

There is little appetite for lingering.

They pack and leave for Capital.

At least the road is easy.

***

Home does not immediately bring calm.

Sirius has stayed behind, without a walk for at least a day. The Warden worries about him.

Oksi is furious with the Warden's concern, then with the children's disorganization.

The day has been full of demands on everyone, and there is little patience left.

The Warden spends time with Comandante before his night's sleep.

The Middles go to bed.

Gradually, Oksi calms down.

***

The first snow has come to Stronghold.

It has closed the garden for the season, shortened the walks, and reminded the Warden that the car is not yet ready for winter.

The family came for one day of relief.

They found a warm sofa, a cold landscape, and many of the same arguments they had hoped to leave behind.

Still, they made the journey.

And now, with the children settling and the house quieter, the day finally ends.
