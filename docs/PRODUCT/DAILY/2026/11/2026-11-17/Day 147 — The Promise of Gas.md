# Day 147 — The Promise of Gas

The Warden carries a small worry into the office.

Yesterday, after the Yandex meeting, he did not return.

He had not told Andrey.

Perhaps there will be a question.

But Andrey does not dwell on it.

The worry passes.

***

The MTS third-quarter preview goes out to consensus.

One more piece of the reporting season is sent on its way.

***

Oksi calls.

There is small talk, and then Roman.

The exterior sockets at Stronghold remain unfinished.

Oksi is frustrated.

The decorator is expected on Thursday.

The Warden asks her to be calm.

Roman is his bro, and he has promised to help.

Surely the sockets will be done.

The promise has become important.

***

The winter tyres are waiting at the delivery point.

They have finally arrived, after a quest already longer than it ought to have been.

But Oksi has a doctor's appointment, and the Warden must be home.

The tyres will wait another day.

***

There is also news the Warden forgot to record yesterday.

The gas service called.

They are ready to turn on the gas at Stronghold.

After all the work, all the waiting, the house has reached another threshold.

But readiness is not connection.

The Warden must take time away from work.

Some pipes still need attention.

And the pipes, too, are Roman's responsibility.

The Warden asks the gas service to come late next week.

Another deadline joins the calendar.

Another promise rests with a man whose reliability has lately become a subject of debate.

***

At home, the Warden settles Comandante to sleep.

Oksi returns from the doctor.

She hopes they might make a trip to Stronghold and back.

But the Warden has had enough for the day.

He says no.

***

The gas is nearly there.

The sockets are nearly there.

The tyres are physically here, but not yet on the car.

So much of the household seems to exist in this narrow space between *ready* and *done*.

Today, at least, the Warden does not add another journey to the list.

Some things can wait until tomorrow.
