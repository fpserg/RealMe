# Day 142 — Two Lives

The Warden meets Kostya for breakfast.

They used to work together.

Kostya is now Head of Strategy at Avito. He moved to Vienna in 2022, and he and his wife divide their lives between Vienna and Moscow.

They talk about the T-Cian merger, artificial intelligence, and the changes taking place in their industry.

Toward the end, the Warden tells him about four children and Stronghold.

What a contrast.

***

Back at the office, the market has more to say.

HeadHunter's numbers look weak.

An MTS preview call needs arranging.

Artem's fund-flows report is still on its way.

Then Alexey from ECM arrives with colleagues and a new possibility: TrizTech, a spinoff from POSI, might become an IPO candidate.

The Warden knows something of the business through POSI.

He likes its leader: a bright IT man who also owns football club Volna as a hobby.

If the IPO proceeds, the Warden would be pleased to write the report and make the pitch.

For now, it is only a possibility.

***

There is good news from Promomed.

The company has given positive feedback on the Pharma report.

The Warden passes the message to Sergey Junior.

Praise belongs to the people who helped earn it.

***

At the department meeting, attention turns to trading ideas for the brokerage.

The Warden proposes Promomed, expecting strong numbers.

The pitch to brokers is scheduled for Monday.

Another thesis to prepare, another argument to make.

***

The Warden collects Jr from School 57 and heads home.

Oksi is in a better mood today.

She has tried to restore her driving licence, without success.

But the evening is quieter than the previous one.

The children go to bed.

The Warden and Oksi watch an episode of the Danish crime series *Murder*.

Her favourite sort of story.

***

At breakfast, Kostya's life had seemed so different from the Warden's.

Two cities, a career in strategy, a household moving between Vienna and Moscow.

The Warden's own days are filled with research reports, children, school pickups, and the unfinished construction of Stronghold.

Neither life needs to be weighed against the other.

For tonight, there is simply the familiar room, Oksi beside him, and a story on the screen.
