# Day 131 — The Reaper Waves Goodbye

At 4:50 in the morning, Comandante announces the beginning of the day.

The Warden disagrees, but only silently.

With one eye half-open, he watches the little commander make a thorough inspection of his surroundings.

This is the last day of the Stronghold holidays.

It is starting early.

***

Comandante finally surrenders to sleep just as the rest of the house wakes.

Oksi needs soil from the nursery, but lets the Warden rest.

When he is properly awake, they set out together.

The skies above Stronghold are clear. Beyond the gates, thick fog closes around the road.

Oksi embraces him, wearing a witchy expression.

The nursery yields soil, a few more plants — inevitably — and diapers for Comandante.

By the time they return, the skies are clear again.

Oksi takes up her shovel.

The Warden takes charge of Comandante.

***

Then something moves beyond the window.

The Grim Reaper has come loose.

The balloon that presided over Halloween is drifting away from Stronghold, its scythe seeming to wave farewell.

The Warden calls the Middles.

They rush outside without stopping to dress properly, eager to catch a glimpse.

Too late.

The Reaper has vanished.

***

An Internet technician arrives.

Stronghold needs faster Internet, and the wiring is discussed. The work will begin next Saturday.

One more improvement to the house, still ahead.

Before darkness, the Warden helps Oksi in the garden. She finishes everything she had planned.

Then the family packs.

They leave for Capital.

The holidays are over.

***

On the road, the Warden asks the children whether they enjoyed their time at Stronghold.

Yes, they say, eagerly.

Oksi says nothing.

She looks sad.

The Warden knows what he believes that silence means: she already misses the house.

Comandante sleeps.

Sirius sits quietly among the bags.

Bella is silent too, while Shakti meows without pause.

Oksi puts on Egyptian cat music.

Shakti falls quiet.

Bella starts meowing.

An imperfect exchange of peace.

***

Capital greets them with a domestic puzzle.

Shakti needs washing.

But the tap gives only hot water. No cold.

Comandante has been woken and is deeply displeased.

Maksim has returned from Baikal and wants to tell everyone about his trip. The timing is unfortunate; Oksi has no patience left for stories.

The neighbours have cold water.

The Warden calls the utility service.

Then a thin stream appears.

It strengthens.

Normal water returns without an explanation.

Oksi manages to wash Shakti.

The children wash too, and go to bed.

The chaos recedes.

***

A warm bath.

Tea.

Bed.

Tomorrow brings the familiar routine.

Behind them lie the nursery, the fog, the garden, the flying Reaper, the crowded journey, and the house they have begun to miss before they are fully away from it.

The Warden had wondered whether these would be the first Stronghold holidays he regretted ending.

Now they have ended.

The Reaper has waved goodbye.

And Sir Home is waiting for their return.
