Last day of Stronghold holidays. Unexpected wake up from Comandante at 4:50 AM, and this is not a short sleep break but proper morning. I silently watch him messing around with one eye half-open.

LI

Comandante surrenders to sleep just when the Stronghold wakes. Oksi needs to nursery for soil, but lets me rest.

After proper awakening we set off to nursery. The skies are clear around Stronghold, but thick fog embraces us just as we leave the gates. Oksi hugs with a witchy face.

Got soil (and a few more plants of course), and diapers for Comandante.

Back. Clear skies again. Oksi wields a shovel and gets to garden. Comandante on me.

Through the window I see Grim Reaper untying and flying away, waving me with its scythe. I tell middles and they rush outside undressed to see it and run around for quite a while, but the Reaper vanished.

Internet master arrives as we need to wire the Stronghold for fast Internet. Discussing the installation, the actual work will start next Saturday.

Some help to Oksi in the garden while it hasn’t become dark. She finishes everything she was planning to, we quickly pack and set off to Capital. Holidays are over.

On the way I ask kids whether they liked their holidays and they eagerly say yes. Oksi is silent and sad. I know she’s already missing Stronghold.

On the way Comandante sleeps, Shakti meows all the way and Bella is silent, as well as Sirius who is sitting among bags in the trunk. Oksi turns on egyptian cat music, and Shakti stops meowing, but Bella immediately starts.

First thing at home - wash Shakti. But! There is no cold water in the tap, just hot! I’m struggling with Comandante who was force awaken and is very displeased.

Maksim who has just arrived from his trip to Baikal tries to share his experience amid this chaos but is told off by Oksi. Oksi and I are both astonished with how unempathic he can be sometimes.

Oksi somehow manages to wash Shakti. Short investigation with neighbours - everyone has cold water. I call utility service and report a problem. But it somehow resolves, the water starts running with a thin jet but then restores to normal. Kids wash and beds. 

Chaotic evening turns into a regular night. Warm bath and tea, and bed, preparing for tomorrow’s routine. Holidays over.
