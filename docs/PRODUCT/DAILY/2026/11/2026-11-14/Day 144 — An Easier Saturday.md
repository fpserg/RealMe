# Day 144 — An Easier Saturday

The morning at Stronghold is cold and damp.

There is little reason to spend it outdoors.

The Warden and Comandante stay inside, apart from a short expedition for supplies.

On the road, near Neroschino, machines are at work among the trees.

Perhaps another settlement will soon appear there.

For now, it is only machinery in the woods, and a glimpse of possible change beyond Stronghold's boundaries.

***

In the afternoon, father and son sleep.

They wake with more energy than they had before.

It is a small luxury, and a welcome one.

***

Not everything at Stronghold is ready.

The exterior sockets are still disconnected.

Roman has left this part unfinished, and Oksi is worried: the decorator is due on Thursday and will need somewhere to plug in.

There is time, but not much.

The Warden makes a note to call.

***

The weather does not invite a walk.

Instead, the Warden, Oksi and Comandante go to the DIY store.

There are more New Year decorations to buy, and household things besides.

While Oksi shops, Comandante discovers bags of sand.

They are evidently more interesting than ordinary sand in a playground.

He investigates them with enthusiasm.

Oksi finds wooden slats that might close the gap between the ceramics and the wall.

Another job for Kadyr.

The house keeps generating small tasks, even as the larger ones move toward completion.

***

Back home, the Warden calls Roman.

They agree that he will come before Thursday and finish the sockets.

A promise, not yet a completed connection.

But the immediate uncertainty is smaller.

***

The evening follows a familiar order.

A movie for the children.

Bedtime.

The litter quest.

Nothing spectacular has happened.

The sockets still need work. The ceramics still need finishing. The decorations are not all in place.

And yet the day has been easier than so many previous weekends at Stronghold.

Perhaps the difference is not that there is less to do.

Perhaps, for once, there has been room to rest while doing it.
