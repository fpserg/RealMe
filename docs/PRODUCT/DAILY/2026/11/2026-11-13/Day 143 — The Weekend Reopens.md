# Day 143 — The Weekend Reopens

The Warden takes Jr to school and goes on to the office.

The weekend has already been arranged.

There will be no Stronghold this time.

Oksi has a doctor's appointment tonight. Tomorrow they intend to visit Sanya's grave with another of his friends. On Sunday, Oksi plans another addition to her tattoo.

For once, the country house will have to wait.

***

At work, MTS has its preview call.

Nothing groundbreaking, at first glance.

There has been plenty else to discuss this week, though.

At the brokerage call, the Warden has a great deal to share.

Then comes HeadHunter's Investor Relations Day.

He had considered attending in person, but the travel would consume time. He watches online instead.

The executives speak about navigating difficult conditions. The Warden hears little that is new, and no sharp questions about the possibility of dividend cuts.

He is content not to have made the journey.

***

Meanwhile, the weekend changes shape.

Oksi's doctor is ill, so her appointment is postponed.

The friend who was to accompany them to Sanya's grave is away on business. That visit must wait as well.

Two plans disappear.

An opening appears.

Stronghold is possible after all.

***

At home, Oksi goes to collect deliveries.

She returns with the trunk full.

The Warden is not pleased by the amount, but they pack their things and set off.

On the road, he speaks to Jr about a complaint from his teacher.

The conversation grows into a broader discussion of behaviour at school.

Alisa joins in.

She sounds more mature now, as the Warden thinks she should.

***

They reach Stronghold without arriving too late.

The children settle in for a movie.

The adults have a little time to relax.

Then everyone goes to bed.

***

The weekend was supposed to lead elsewhere: to an appointment, to remembrance, to obligations away from the house.

Those obligations have not vanished. They have only been postponed.

But tonight the road has brought the family back to Stronghold.

No urgent construction task defines the evening.

No late delivery must be unpacked before sleep.

For a little while, the house is simply somewhere to be.
