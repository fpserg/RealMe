# Day 133 — The Sofa Awaits

The Warden's working day begins with Transneft.

The investment case is being reconsidered. In a discussion with ECM, the search for useful comparisons expands beyond American midstream companies. The question is no longer simply which peers are familiar, but which can help explain the business most clearly.

Then comes VK.

The stream is a pleasure, as these conversations usually are.

***

A message arrives from Oksi.

She is tired. The return to routine has already become too much.

Tomorrow is a holiday.

The Warden decides they will spend it at Stronghold.

Not a long vacation. Just one day.

Perhaps one day will be enough to change the mood.

***

He rushes home before Comandante falls asleep.

There are bags to pack and food to order. Even an improvised escape requires logistics.

Maksim joins them.

The pets remain behind.

The road is easy.

And at Stronghold, something new is waiting.

The sofa.

***

Oksi unwraps it while the Warden rocks Comandante to sleep.

Then Maksim and the Warden move it into place.

It is as cozy as hoped.

After the long succession of purchases, deliveries and unfinished work, here is something simple: a piece of the house that is ready to be used.

The children settle in for a movie.

The Warden goes out for litter and water, then returns to fix the curtains in Jr's room.

There is always one more small job.

***

Jr falls asleep in front of the film.

The Warden lifts him and carries him to bed, the way he carries Comandante.

For a moment, the older child is small again.

Maksim and Alisa stay to finish the movie.

The parents do not wait for the ending.

They go to bed.

***

Two days ago, the family left Stronghold reluctantly.

Yesterday, Capital reclaimed them with alarms, errands and exhaustion.

Tonight they are back.

The house is not finished. The holiday is brief. Tomorrow will bring its own demands.

But the sofa is in place.

The curtains are fixed.

A child has been carried to bed.

And Stronghold, once again, has offered somewhere to rest.
