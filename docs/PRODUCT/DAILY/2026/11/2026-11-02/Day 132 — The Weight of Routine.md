# Day 132 — The Weight of Routine

Comandante wakes the Warden before the alarm.

The holidays have ended, but the little commander sees no reason to grant an extra minute of sleep.

Jr goes to school.

The Warden goes to the office.

Routine has returned.

***

The desk is full of things accumulated during the absence, though few seem urgent.

There is Transneft.

The investment case needs a cleaner frame. The Warden sends Artem to examine American midstream companies, rather than relying only on comparisons with the oil and gas majors.

There is VK.

Tomorrow's stream requires preparation. On the preliminary call, the subjects are agreed, including a question the Warden intends to press: how convincing is management's EBITDA guidance?

The office day passes in the familiar work of sorting, directing, preparing.

***

Stronghold has not disappeared from the agenda.

The Warden contacts Mosoblgaz about the next steps in the Gas Campaign.

He calls Roman about the garden wiring and the liquid needed in the pipes.

Even from Capital, the unfinished house keeps a place in the day's business.

Then he collects Jr from School 57.

Home.

***

Comandante is tired.

Oksi is angry.

The Warden bathes the little commander and tries to settle him for the night. Comandante cries. Oksi takes over, abruptly, rocking the bed herself.

After the warmth of the Stronghold holidays, the return to ordinary life feels painfully fast.

The Warden has little time to dwell on it.

There are errands waiting.

Petrol.

Medicines.

A car wash.

The car is dirty enough, especially inside, to turn a simple stop into a long undertaking.

By the time he returns, midnight has passed.

***

He is tired.

Sirius still needs walking.

Then, at last, there is time to speak with Oksi.

The day's anger has a context. She describes how quickly the ordinary responsibilities have overwhelmed her since the family returned.

The conversation does not undo the difficult evening, but it makes the day easier to understand.

***

Only yesterday, Stronghold was a place they were reluctant to leave.

Today, Capital has supplied the school run, the commute, the office, the bedtime struggle, and a maintenance expedition stretching beyond midnight.

The Warden has advanced his work, contacted the people needed for Stronghold, and kept the household moving.

Yet the day feels less like progress than the weight of things beginning again.

Finally, bed.

The routine has reclaimed its territory.
