# Day 135 — The Vanishing Apartment

The Warden returns to the office.

There is a Transneft pitch to finish, a new way to explain the business, and trading ideas for retail investors to consider.

Work has resumed its familiar rhythm.

***

Roman and Alexander are on the phone about Stronghold.

There is electrical wiring to arrange outside and pipe maintenance inside.

The house keeps advancing, one practical conversation at a time.

Then Oksi calls.

There may be serious buyers for Capital.

At last, perhaps, the apartment they have been trying to sell is attracting the right attention.

But there is another piece of news.

The listing for the flat they hoped to buy has vanished.

The Warden contacts the owner.

It is no longer for sale.

The possibility on one side of the relocation has grown more real just as the possibility on the other side has disappeared.

***

The Warden waits for Jr at School 57.

A call comes from Basis IR.

The stream is confirmed for November 19.

Another date enters the working calendar.

He collects Jr and heads home.

***

Oksi is tired.

Comandante needs putting to bed.

There is dinner, then a walk with Sirius.

The Warden offers to drive Oksi to collect ceramics and decorations.

Perhaps both errands can be finished in one journey.

But the ceramics are not ready.

Only the decorations can be collected.

The Warden would rather avoid a separate expedition. Yet Oksi has already arranged the pickup with the maker.

The evening's logistics have acquired their own momentum.

***

The day has moved several things forward.

A pitch approaches completion.

A stream has a date.

Stronghold's maintenance is being coordinated.

Capital may have buyers.

But the apartment they wanted is gone, and even the small hope of combining two errands into one has failed.

The Warden has spent much of the day arranging what comes next.

Some arrangements hold.

Others vanish from the map.
