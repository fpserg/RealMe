# Day 135 — The Vanishing Flat

The Warden returns to the office after the break.

Transneft is nearing its next form. The repositioning pitch needs its final touches, and there are trading ideas for retail investors to consider.

Stronghold is never far from the day's work.

Roman and Alexander are involved in sorting out the exterior electrical wiring and the pipes inside the house.

The unfinished tasks travel with the Warden even when he is back at his desk.

***

Then Oksi calls.

There may be serious buyers for Capital.

For a moment, the long relocation campaign seems to have found fresh momentum.

But the advertisement for the flat they hoped to buy has disappeared.

The Warden contacts the owner.

The answer is definite.

It is no longer for sale.

The potential buyers are still only potential.

The lost replacement flat is real.

***

Later, while waiting to collect Jr from School 57, another call arrives.

Basis IR confirms a stream for November 19.

One more date enters the calendar.

The Warden collects Jr and returns home.

***

Oksi is tired.

There is Comandante's bedtime, dinner, and a walk for Sirius.

The Warden offers to drive Oksi to collect ceramics and decorations for Stronghold.

Perhaps they can finish two errands in one journey.

But the ceramics are not ready.

The decorations are, and Oksi has already arranged the pickup with their maker.

The Warden had hoped to save a trip.

Now there is no graceful way to withdraw the offer.

***

The day has offered an odd mixture of progress and reversal.

A stream is booked.

The Transneft pitch moves forward.

Stronghold's wiring and pipes are being organized.

And the Capital campaign may finally have buyers — just as the intended destination disappears.

There is no resolution yet.

Only the next call, the next errand, the next place to look.

The Warden keeps moving.
