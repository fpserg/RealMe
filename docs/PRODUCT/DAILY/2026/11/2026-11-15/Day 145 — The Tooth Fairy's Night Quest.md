# Day 145 — The Tooth Fairy's Night Quest

The family leaves Stronghold early.

Oksi has her tattoo appointment, and the day must be arranged around it.

The drive home is unusually smooth.

Almost no friction.

At home, Oksi sets off.

Comandante falls asleep.

So does the Warden.

***

After they wake, there is a walk with the children.

Then home again, where Comandante resumes his usual investigations and disorder.

The Warden watches over him.

Dinner follows.

Comandante goes to bed.

The older children have a little time to themselves.

For a while, the evening seems settled.

***

Oksi comes home late.

From the middle children's room, the Warden hears whispers.

He goes to tell them to stop.

But Jr has news.

Another tooth has fallen out.

***

The Tooth Fairy has been summoned.

The Tooth Fairy, tonight, is the Warden.

The required treasure is one thousand rubles.

It is already night.

Finding cash is less straightforward than it ought to be.

The Warden sets out on a small expedition through the sleeping city.

Eventually, good old Raiffeisen provides what is needed.

The quest is complete.

***

Home again.

Finally, bed.

But Comandante wakes several times during the night.

Sleep comes in fragments.

***

It has been a day of modest duties: the drive home, the walk, the children, the evening routine.

And then one more small obligation, discovered behind the door of a whispering room.

The Tooth Fairy does not always work convenient hours.

But she has a reliable deputy.
