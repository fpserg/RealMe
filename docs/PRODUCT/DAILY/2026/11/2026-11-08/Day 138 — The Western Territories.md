# Day 138 — The Western Territories

It is an ordinary morning at Stronghold.

Comandante is investigating the New Year decorations, discovering the small objects that have lately filled the house.

The Warden takes the children outside.

On their walk, they meet Julia and Grigory, their neighbours, accompanied by their daughter.

The children wave hello.

And then the conversation turns to the land.

***

Julia and Grigory explain their situation.

They want a house closer to the apartment where they currently live. The journeys to work and school take too long.

If they can find somewhere better suited to their lives, they are prepared to sell their land at Stronghold.

They discuss a possible price.

It is not yet an offer. No decision has been made.

But the reason for Julia's earlier call is no longer a mystery.

***

The Warden phones Oksi.

The connection is poor, but the essential news gets through.

She sounds excited.

Back from the walk, they consider what this might mean.

The apartment they had hoped to buy in Capital is no longer available.

Yet here, at Stronghold, another possibility has appeared.

The Warden and Oksi agree: if the neighbours come with an offer, they want to take it.

For now, the land remains someone else's.

But it has begun to enter their plans.

***

They drive back to Capital.

On the road, Oksi receives a call from Nadezhda. There are problems with water meters in a flat they have already sold.

Then Ksenia calls about the next steps in the Capital campaign.

The children are noisy. The parents grow angry.

The journey home is less peaceful than the conversations at Stronghold.

***

At Capital, the familiar routine resumes.

Dinner. Washing. Beds.

Later, when the children are settled, the Warden and Oksi watch *A Good Year*.

And they talk again about the land next door.

The Western Territories.

***

Only yesterday, Julia's unexplained telephone call was a source of speculation.

Today, she and Grigory have explained what would make them leave.

Nothing is settled. They must find another home before there can be a sale.

But the Warden and Oksi have already made a decision of their own.

The hoped-for future in Capital has receded, while the boundaries of Stronghold might yet expand.

For one quiet evening, that possibility is enough to talk about.
