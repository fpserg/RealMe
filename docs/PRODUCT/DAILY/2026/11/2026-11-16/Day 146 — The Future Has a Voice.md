# Day 146 — The Future Has a Voice

Comandante has been waking throughout the night.

So has the Warden.

Morning arrives late, followed by hurried preparations.

Jr looks beneath his pillow.

The Tooth Fairy has not come.

The Warden, who spent the previous night finding a thousand rubles for the occasion, is disappointed in his own deputy.

***

At the office, the day moves toward the future.

The Warden attends Yandex's Search & AI investor meeting.

Management describes a world only three or four years away, in which voice assistants may become the most common way people interact with computers.

The Warden listens and begins to wonder what such a change would mean for e-commerce.

If the interface changes, what happens to the places where people shop?

It is an intriguing question to carry away from the meeting.

***

The present keeps sending messages.

The winter tyres have finally arrived at the delivery point.

Roman, however, has not arrived at Stronghold.

His car is broken.

The exterior sockets remain unfinished, and Thursday's decorator visit is approaching.

Roman remembers the deadline.

For now, that will have to do.

***

The Warden rushes home.

Oksi has an appointment with the road police to recover her driving licence.

The children need arranging.

The Warden collects Jr from School 444, and neighbour Sergey takes him onward to 57.

At the flat, boxes of decorations seem to be everywhere.

The Warden has had enough of them.

***

Oksi returns with her driving licence.

It has cost time, money and nerves, but the long-running errand is finally complete.

They go out for coffee and to a pharmacy.

On the way, the Warden learns that Oksi offered Sergey six thousand rubles for taking Jr there and back.

To him, it is far too much for a small favour between neighbours.

Another disagreement joins the day's collection.

***

Oksi goes home.

The Warden takes Comandante for a walk.

Later there is a shower, bedtime, and another episode of *Murder*.

Then the adults go to bed too.

***

At Yandex, the future had spoken in the voice of an assistant.

At home, everything still required people: someone to collect a child, someone to repair a socket, someone to find winter tyres, someone to remember the Tooth Fairy.

One licence has been recovered.

Several small quests remain open.

The future may have a voice.

Tonight, the household still needs hands.
