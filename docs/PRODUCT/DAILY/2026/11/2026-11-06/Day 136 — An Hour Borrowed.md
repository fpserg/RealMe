# Day 136 — An Hour Borrowed

The Warden takes Jr to school.

Then he sets off with Oksi and Comandante to collect the decorations he promised to help retrieve.

The plan is simple: there and back, quickly, before too much of the working day is lost.

Rush hour has other ideas.

Still, they reach the warehouse at nine, just as it opens.

Oksi goes inside.

The Warden and Comandante wait.

***

An hour passes.

At ten, Oksi finally returns.

The owner, it turns out, had not expected them so early and arrived only then.

The Warden is angry.

An hour of the morning has disappeared.

***

At home, he learns what else has happened during that hour.

T-Tech has made an unexpected takeover offer for Cian, at a premium the Warden considers significant.

The news belongs to his working world, and he has missed its arrival while waiting outside a warehouse.

He calls Artem with instructions for a rapid response.

Then he rushes to the office.

***

Artem and Lisa have a draft.

The Warden moves quickly through it.

Andrey makes no comment about his late arrival.

There are media comments, the brokerage call, and the usual business of the office.

The day began late, but the work continues.

***

Meanwhile, Saturday is gathering complications.

Jr has been invited to a classmate's birthday.

The ceramics still need collecting.

The Internet technician is due at Stronghold to install the connection.

They had not planned to go to Stronghold at all.

But if the logistics will be difficult either way, the Warden suggests leaving tonight.

He knows how much Oksi will enjoy it.

***

Home.

Quick preparations.

The car is full of decorations.

They set off.

There may be ice on the road, and the car still has summer tyres. The Warden drives carefully.

They reach Stronghold.

Oksi is delighted, moving around the house with her decorations.

The Warden carries boxes to the litter plot.

Then everyone goes to bed.

Tomorrow promises to be demanding.

***

The decorations have finally arrived.

They cost more of the Warden's working day than he intended, and the timing could scarcely have been worse.

But the market news was handled, the office routine survived, and the family has reached Stronghold.

At the end of a day spent watching an hour disappear, the Warden has found a way to give Oksi another evening in the house she loves.
