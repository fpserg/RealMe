# Day 140 — The Umbrella

The reporting season has taken possession of the day.

First BAZA.

Then POSI.

A BAZA call follows, and in the evening there is POSI's Investor Relations Day.

The Warden moves from one obligation to the next.

***

At Stronghold, the smaller decisions continue.

Exterior decorations still need sorting out.

So do the ceramics inside.

Roman arrives with wiring and plumbing work.

Some of it gets done.

Some remains.

The house, as usual, advances in pieces.

***

The POSI event offers no great revelation.

There are familiar faces, though.

The Warden meets acquaintances he has known for years, including a former colleague who has moved to Ozon Bank.

He comes away with an umbrella.

A small souvenir from another evening spent in the company of the market.

***

At home, the mood is different.

Oksi has just taken Comandante to bed.

She is tired.

She is not pleased to see the Warden arriving from an evening event.

To her, it looks as though he has been enjoying himself at work while Household duties have remained with her.

The Warden has spent the day on reporting, calls and professional obligations.

Yet the account of the day is not the same on both sides of the front door.

***

There is no resolution recorded tonight.

Only a busy day, some progress at Stronghold, an umbrella brought home, and a tired household that has been waiting.

The Warden's Career has its calendar.

Household has its own hours, and they do not stop when the working day runs late.
