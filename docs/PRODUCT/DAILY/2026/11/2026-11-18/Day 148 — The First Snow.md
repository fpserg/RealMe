# Day 148 — The First Snow

Snow covers the morning.

The tyre quest, which has been waiting patiently on the Warden's mental board, moves at once to the top.

Not someday.

Now.

On the commute, Oksi sends a message.

She wants to go to a warehouse in Khimki for more decorations.

The Warden looks at the snow, imagines the traffic, remembers the summer tyres still on the car.

This seems the worst possible day.

But Captain Sparrow wants her treasures.

Now.

***

Roman has not come to Stronghold.

The sockets have been discussed for two weeks.

The decorator is due tomorrow.

The Warden calls, discovers another delay, and loses his temper.

Oksi is furious too.

Later Roman sends a message asking him not to be angry.

He is setting off.

Perhaps he had simply forgotten.

The Warden arranges access through Alexey, who can let Roman in.

On the way to Cian's investor day, the Warden calls again.

The sockets are done.

Roman also has an idea about the bad smell in Maksim's room.

For once, a lingering task has moved from promise to completion.

***

At Cian, another question remains unresolved.

The room wants to know about a possible takeover bid.

Management will not comment.

The Warden suggests a wager among fellow analysts: could this be Cian's last investor day?

He tries approaching the question indirectly.

What are the advantages of a standalone, single-product platform compared with a larger ecosystem?

Still no clarification.

The rest of the presentation sounds familiar.

The most interesting possibility remains the one nobody will discuss.

***

Home.

The flat is crowded with boxes.

Oksi has a manicure appointment.

The tyre quest falls to the Warden, with Comandante as his companion.

At the delivery point, the attendant helps carry the tyres to the car.

The first tyre shop is fully booked.

So is the second.

At the third, close to home, there is a narrow opening.

The Warden leaves the car and walks home with Comandante on his shoulders.

Later, while the older children watch their youngest brother, he retrieves the car.

The tyres are changed.

One last errand remains: the garage.

***

During Night Watch, the Warden works on a new GIFTED-themed card game.

The code can be coaxed into existence.

Balance is harder.

Another small world has begun to take shape, with rules that do not yet quite hold together.

***

Oksi returns.

She hopes for another trip to Stronghold, but the hour is too late.

The Warden goes to the garage.

When he comes back, Oksi is sitting on the bed with sparkling Christmas tree balls.

He looks at them.

They are beautiful.

Perhaps her treasures are not entirely unreasonable.

Their number, however, remains another matter.

***

The first snow has made the day urgent.

There has been anger, rushing, a meeting full of unanswered questions, and a succession of practical obstacles.

But the tyres are finally on the car.

The sockets are finally connected.

And at the end of the day, the treasures shine.
