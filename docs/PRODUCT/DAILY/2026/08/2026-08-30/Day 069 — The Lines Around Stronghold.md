# 📜 Chronicle of Day 069

## Chapter: The Lines Around Stronghold

## 🥤 The Ritual Expedition

The morning began with Owling and another Stronghold supplies expedition.

Soft drinks this time.

The Warden noticed that the expedition had become more ritual than necessity.

Stronghold had accumulated routines of its own: walk, car, supplies, return.

Some tasks survive after urgency disappears because they have already become part of the place.

---

## ✉️ A Message from Mother

There was another kind of return.

Mother had written the day before, after a long while.

Relations had soured through the confrontation between the Warden's parents and Oksi.

The message was simple in its pain: they badly missed Alisa and Sergey Jr. What had they done wrong to deserve this attitude?

Oksi had her own answer.

When help was requested, she felt the grandparents did everything except what they had actually been asked to do. They tried to raise the children according to their own ideas rather than the parents'. Agreements were repeatedly reopened. She saw a clear preference for Alisa over Sergey Jr.

The Warden could see sense in some of these arguments.

Not all of them.

What he wanted most was not to decide a winner.

He wanted the confrontation to end.

---

## 🌱 Fifteen More Holes

Stronghold demanded the body again.

The Warden dug roughly fifteen more planting pits, perhaps more.

There was at least a pocket of rest while Owling slept.

Oksi barely stopped at all.

She planted for six hours, then took Maksim and went back to the nursery for more soil.

The garden that had been running ahead of its builders was finally being chased down.

---

## 🚪 The Smaller Boundary

Alisa and Sergey Jr spent the day outside with their friend Roma.

That was welcome.

His repeated attempts to get inside Stronghold were not.

The Warden and Oksi wanted privacy.

It was a small problem beside the family confrontation opened by Mother's message, but strangely related to it.

A home needs doors.

The difficult part is deciding when they should be open.

---

## 🌧️ Planting Through Rain

Oksi came back from the nursery and immediately returned to planting.

Then the rain began.

She did not stop.

Wet and exhausted, she continued with the trees and flowers until most of the job was finally done.

The backlog that had seemed capable of reproducing itself faster than the family could clear it was substantially broken.

But Stronghold collected the difference in fatigue.

---

## ⛽ The Gauge

On the road home, the fuel gauge fell low enough to make the Warden genuinely anxious.

The fuel crisis had turned an ordinary warning light into the possibility of being stranded midway home.

Fortunately, the nearest station's queue was not long.

They filled the tank.

Another immediate danger closed.

---

## 📑 Before Sleep

At home, Oksi fell asleep before the Warden.

That almost never needed explanation.

She was exhausted.

Before his own sleep, the Warden checked Lisa's Banking report.

And RealMe had advanced too.

The current app-development roadmap, by his estimate, had only another one or two days left.

Even inside a weekend consumed by soil, plants, children, rain and fuel, another world continued taking shape.

---

## 📚 Steward's Reflection

Day 069 drew several lines around Stronghold.

One was literal: the family wanted Roma to remain a welcome outdoor friend without assuming access to the house.

Another was generational: Oksi wanted parenting decisions and agreements respected by the grandparents. The grandparents, from the other side of that boundary, felt the absence of Alisa and Sergey Jr and asked why the door had closed.

The Warden stood between these accounts without fully accepting either one.

His wish was simpler than the dispute: for the confrontation to end.

Meanwhile the garden offered a physical version of the same lesson. A boundary cannot merely stop things coming in. The nursery pipeline had been filling Stronghold faster than the family could plant it. Today, through fifteen pits and Oksi's extraordinary hours in the rain, that imbalance finally narrowed.

Stronghold needs doors.

It also needs them to open at the right speed.
