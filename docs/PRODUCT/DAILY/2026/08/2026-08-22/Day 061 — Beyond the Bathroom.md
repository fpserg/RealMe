# 📜 Chronicle of Day 061

## Chapter: Beyond the Bathroom

## 🐇 Visitors at the Edge

At night, a hare appeared near Stronghold.

In the morning another animal crossed the road: black, quick, something like a squirrel but not quite. Perhaps a marten.

There was no certainty about the second visitor, only the brief impression left by something disappearing across the road.

Stronghold's surroundings were becoming known in fragments — not from a map, but from repeated nights and mornings spent there.

---

## 🛁 The Missing Pieces

The Bathroom still needed its missing pieces.

So the Warden went with Oksi and Owling to collect the ceramics and other materials required to finish it.

The campaign had stretched through contractors, deliveries, dependencies and repeated journeys. This trip did not finish the room by itself, but it removed another reason why finishing might have to wait.

On the way, more plants came home from a nursery.

Construction materials and living things travelled toward Stronghold together.

---

## 🍽️ The Next Room

Even before the Bathroom was complete, attention began moving toward what followed.

The kitchen was coming.

Painting the kitchen walls was negotiated, along with preparing the surfaces for ceramics before the kitchen arrived.

The sequence was changing. The Bathroom was no longer the only frontier.

Another room was entering the campaign.

---

## 📚 Steward's Reflection

There is a point in a long construction campaign when progress becomes visible not because one task has finally disappeared, but because the horizon expands beyond it.

The missing Bathroom materials were collected. Yet on the same day, preparations for the kitchen began to take shape.

Meanwhile plants kept arriving, a hare appeared at night, and another unknown animal crossed the morning road.

Stronghold remains unfinished.

But it is increasingly difficult to describe it only as a building project.

The work is moving beyond the Bathroom, and the place is growing around the work.
