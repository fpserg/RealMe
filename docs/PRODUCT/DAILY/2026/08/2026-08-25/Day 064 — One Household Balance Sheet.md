# 📜 Chronicle of Day 064

## Chapter: One Household Balance Sheet

## 📊 Back Into the Models

The Warden returned to deep modelling.

After days in which Career work had repeatedly competed with Stronghold emergencies, construction logistics and long journeys, the models again received sustained attention.

But elsewhere in the Household, numbers of another kind were beginning to demand their own model.

---

## 🏙️ The Price of Capital

Potential buyers came to see Capital.

Their arrival forced the arithmetic into focus.

Stronghold had consumed more money. At the existing listing price, selling Capital would no longer leave enough to buy the intended replacement apartment.

The two campaigns could no longer be treated as financially separate.

The Warden told Ksenia, the real-estate broker, to raise the price.

Capital would now have to carry more of Stronghold's cost before it could be surrendered.

---

## 🧱 Help From Next Door

Stronghold's kitchen needed materials coordinated while the Warden was away.

So another local connection was activated.

Neighbour Levon was asked to help.

Whether the handoff would work remained uncertain, but the principle mattered: a distant property becomes easier to operate when not every physical dependency requires the Warden's own arrival.

---

## 🔧 Bro Rates

Roman sent his plumbing price list.

The numbers were close to market.

That was not outrageous. It was, in fact, precisely the problem.

The Warden had expected something better from his bro.

Stronghold had acquired another small lesson in the difference between personal trust and commercial pricing.

---

## 🎒 Two Schools, One Morning

Tomorrow, Sergey Jr would sit the School 57 exam.

At the same time, Oksi needed to attend Parents' Day at School 444.

Two schools required the Household in two places.

The solution was simple enough: the Warden would work from home.

Career would bend around Family for the day.

---

## 📚 Steward's Reflection

Day 064 made the Household's hidden connections unusually visible.

Stronghold spending changed the required selling price of Capital. Distance from Stronghold created a need for Levon's help. Roman's plumbing work became a question not only of trust but of price. Two school commitments determined where Career work could happen the next day.

These are different Domains, but they do not possess separate money, time or attention.

The Household has one balance sheet in more ways than one.

Today, that became difficult to ignore.
