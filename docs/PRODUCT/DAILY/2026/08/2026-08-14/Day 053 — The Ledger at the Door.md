## 🪙 The Ledger Waiting at Home

Capital did not advance toward the apartment being pursued.

## 📚 Steward's Reflection

Some days grow heavy because little is finished. This day grew heavy despite much being finished.

Career and Tower produced visible results. Yet completion in one part of the World did not reduce the demands waiting in another. At the door stood the unresolved Capital Campaign and a fresh cluster of expenses.

The Warden named the resulting truth without ornament: tired.

The ledger records more than money. It records how many claims arrive at once, and how much of the Warden they ask to carry.
