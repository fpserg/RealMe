# 📜 Chronicle of Day 055

## Chapter: The Work Outlives the Workshop

## 🛠️ The Closed Workshop

A familiar workshop ceased to open its doors.

Google AI Studio no longer worked with Russian accounts, and the RealMe app could not remain where it had been built. The Warden began recovering it and moving it elsewhere.

The migration was not yet complete.

But the work had already proved that it belonged to more than the tool that first carried it.

---

## 🏙️ Time Held at Capital

New potential buyers came to Capital.

They were friendly, though the Warden felt they had not been fully impressed. No decision followed them out of the door.

For once, uncertainty did not only delay the Capital Campaign. It also left the Relocation reserve more time to accumulate interest.

Waiting revealed that it could hold value as well as cost it.

---

## 🏡 Hands Around the Household

The day was divided between the children and the movement of RealMe.

While Oksi enhanced her tattoo, the Warden held his share of the Household. Owling behaved. When Sirius became a menace, Maksim took him out.

Responsibility did not remain in one pair of hands.

The Household adjusted and continued.

---

## 📚 Steward's Reflection

A workshop may close, a buyer may hesitate, and a necessary person may be unavailable.

Continuity does not require every path to remain open. It requires the purpose to survive the path.

On this day, RealMe began moving beyond a blocked platform, delay at Capital became time held in reserve, and the Household redistributed what needed to be carried.

The work outlived the workshop.
