# 📜 Chronicle of Day 063

## Chapter: After the Flood, the Rainbow

## 💥 Apocalypse at Stronghold

The message from Stronghold sounded catastrophic.

Kadyr had been trying to repair the pipe when it exploded. Water was flowing everywhere. The pipes looked damaged enough that perhaps the whole system would need replacing.

The Warden stopped what he was doing at work, called Roman for an emergency, and headed home to collect Oksi and Owling before going to Stronghold.

For a while, the day reorganized itself around disaster.

---

## 💧 Smaller Than It Looked

By the time the consequences were understood, the apocalypse had shrunk.

Kadyr had managed to stop the water and wipe it away before the furniture was damaged.

Roman came.

The pipes were fixed.

The feared complete replacement was not required.

And while attention had been captured by water, another long-running Stronghold campaign quietly crossed its finish line: the inner doors were properly installed.

The house had survived the flood and gained its doors on the same day.

---

## 🌱 A Few More Flowers

Once the emergency loosened its grip, ordinary Stronghold work returned almost immediately.

The Warden dug a few more pits.

Oksi planted a few more flowers.

It was an almost absurd return to normality after the earlier panic: from exploding pipes to arranging plants in the earth.

Perhaps Stronghold approved.

A bright double rainbow appeared.

Then appeared again.

---

## 🌈 Gratitude, Then Rain

The rainbow did not mean the weather had finished with them.

On the way back, a rainstorm arrived.

Stronghold's apparent gratitude had been vivid but brief.

The day still had another scarcity waiting.

---

## ⛽ The Fuel Quest

The tank was almost empty.

The first petrol station had no fuel.

So did the second.

The Warden drove toward a last-resort station with increasingly little room for another failure.

On the way, negotiations began with the car itself.

Find petrol, and there would be a proper wash.

And new winter tyres.

At the final station there was fuel — any fuel at all would have counted — and almost no queue.

The tank became full.

The bargain became binding.

---

## 📚 Steward's Reflection

Day 063 repeatedly looked worse before it became better.

An exploding pipe looked like a ruined plumbing system. It became a repair with no damaged furniture.

Two empty petrol stations looked like the beginning of another mobility crisis. The third had fuel and almost no queue.

Between those two reversals, the doors were finally installed, more flowers entered the ground, and Stronghold placed two double rainbows over the day before sending everyone home through a storm.

Some days advance through careful execution.

This one advanced by repeatedly avoiding the disaster it seemed about to become.

And the car is still owed a wash.
