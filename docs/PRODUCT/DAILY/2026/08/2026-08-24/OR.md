# Operational Record

## Household Realm

### Stronghold Domain

- Kadyr's attempt to repair the leaking pipe escalated into a major water release, initially creating concern that the pipe system might require complete replacement.
- Kadyr stopped the water and removed the spilled water before it damaged the furniture.
- Roman responded to the emergency call and successfully repaired the pipes; the apparent plumbing catastrophe proved substantially less severe than first feared.
- Inner-door installation was completed properly while the plumbing incident was being resolved.
- The Warden dug several more planting pits and Oksi planted several more flowers.
- Stronghold produced a bright double rainbow twice after the crisis had passed; a rainstorm followed during the journey back.

### Mobility / Fuel

- After returning, the Warden began another fuel search with the tank almost empty.
- Two petrol stations were checked and both had no fuel.
- On the way to a last-resort station, the Warden promised the car a wash and new winter tyres if fuel could be found.
- The final station unexpectedly had petrol available and almost no queue.
- A full tank was secured, removing the immediate mobility risk.
- A good car wash is now an explicit carried commitment; winter tyres were also promised for the appropriate season.

## Career Realm

- Work activity was interrupted and deprioritized when the Stronghold plumbing emergency was reported.

## Reflection

The day began as an apparent Stronghold disaster and resolved into an unexpectedly strong recovery. The feared water damage did not materialize, Roman repaired the pipes, and the long-delayed inner doors were finally installed. Landscaping even advanced after the emergency, with more pits dug and flowers planted.

The double rainbow provided an unusually vivid punctuation to that recovery, though the return journey immediately restored the day's harsher weather. The later fuel search created a second resource scare: two empty stations and an almost empty tank brought mobility close to becoming constrained again. The last-resort station reversed that outcome with available petrol and almost no queue.

Two acute Stronghold-support risks that had dominated recent days — water and fuel — therefore ended the day resolved rather than open.
