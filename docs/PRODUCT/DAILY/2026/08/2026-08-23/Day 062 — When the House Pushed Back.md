# 📜 Chronicle of Day 062

## Chapter: When the House Pushed Back

## 🌱 The Pits That Nobody Dug

The day began with an absence.

Sergey the Landscaper and his team were not coming.

The pits for the flowers still had to exist, so the work moved back across the boundary from contractor to Household.

The Warden and family would have to dig them themselves.

It was an unwelcome transfer of labour, but at least the response could be practical. An expedition to the nursery brought soil and, more importantly, a wheelbarrow — a simple machine that promised to make the land work less punishing.

---

## 🚪 Dust Inside the Stronghold

Then the door installers arrived.

This time they had come to work, but their work filled the house with noise and dust until staying inside became nearly impossible.

Stronghold was progressing and becoming temporarily less inhabitable at exactly the same time.

The doors moved closer to completion. The family moved out of their way.

---

## 🦔 The Hedgehog

At the nursery, Owling found a hedgehog.

A figurine, technically.

But he called it by its name very clearly, and when the adults tried to take him away from it, anxiety arrived immediately.

So the hedgehog joined the soil and wheelbarrow on the expedition home.

Among a day dominated by materials, contractors and repairs, Owling had selected an artifact of his own.

---

## 💧 No Water

Then the pipe leaked again.

The Warden collected Kadyr and drove to a nearby shop for spare parts.

Some were found.

Not all.

Without the missing pieces, the incident could not be resolved. Stronghold was left without water.

Suddenly the hierarchy of unfinished work became very simple. Doors could wait until tomorrow. Landscaping could wait. The Bathroom campaign itself could wait.

Water could not.

---

## 🌙 Retreat to Capital

Night had already arrived when the family finally left Stronghold.

The drive back to Capital was late and exhausting, though at least the roads were kinder than they would have been earlier.

Behind them remained an unfinished set of doors and an unresolved pipe.

Ahead was Capital, reached later than anyone would have wanted but with the difficult day finally ending.

---

## 📚 Steward's Reflection

Stronghold pushed back from several directions at once.

A contractor failed to arrive, transferring physical work to the Household. Contractors who did arrive filled the house with dust and noise. Then the plumbing failed again and removed something more fundamental than comfort: water itself.

Yet the day was not empty of gains. There is soil ready for planting, a wheelbarrow that will make the work easier, doors close to completion, and a small hedgehog that Owling refused to leave behind.

Long campaigns are rarely defeated by one dramatic obstacle. They exhaust through accumulation — another missing worker, another shop, another unavailable part, another late drive.

Day 062 ended in retreat.

Not surrender.
