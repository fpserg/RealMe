# Operational Record

## Household Realm

### Stronghold Domain

- Learned that Sergey the Landscaper and his team would not come to dig planting pits, forcing the Household to take on the work directly.
- Door installers arrived and worked amid enough noise and dust that remaining inside Stronghold became impractical.
- Went to a nursery for soil and acquired a wheelbarrow to make landscaping work easier.
- Also acquired a hedgehog figurine after Owling clearly called it by name and became very anxious when the family tried to leave it behind.
- A pipe leaked again late in the day.
- Took Kadyr to a nearby shop to obtain replacement parts.
- Not all required parts were available, leaving the pipe problem unresolved and Stronghold without water.
- Returned to Capital in a late and exhausting night drive; traffic was better than it would likely have been earlier.
- Door installation remains incomplete and is expected to be finished the next day.

## Reflection

The day exposed how quickly several small contractor dependencies can compound into a difficult Stronghold campaign. Landscaping work unexpectedly fell back onto the Household, door installation made the house temporarily unusable, and the recurring pipe failure ultimately removed water service altogether.

Some useful capacity was nevertheless added: soil was collected and a wheelbarrow should materially simplify future land work. The hedgehog figurine also became a small Family artifact through Owling's unusually clear recognition and attachment to it.

The weekend ended not with resolution but with retreat to Capital. Doors remain one day from expected completion, while restoring Stronghold's water is now the more urgent unresolved dependency.
