## Reflection

Day 068 was almost entirely shaped by Stronghold. The practical objectives advanced: supplies were obtained, water testing was initiated, a hose and fertilizers were bought, and ten more planting pits were dug. Yet the campaign's throughput problem became clearer. Nursery acquisition is capable of creating planting work faster than the Household can absorb it, turning each successful purchase into additional backlog.

Fatigue again affected the experience of completion. A long nursery visit, Owling logistics, an overloaded car and heavy rain were followed by the water-lab stop; by then both adults were exhausted enough that the expedition ended in argument. RealMe development still found an evening slot, but only around Owling walks and before another late Stronghold dinner.
