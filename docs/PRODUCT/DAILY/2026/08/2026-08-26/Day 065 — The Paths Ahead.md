# 📜 Chronicle of Day 065

## Chapter: The Paths Ahead

## 🏠 Work From Home

The Warden stayed home because the Household needed to be in several places at once.

Career did not stop.

Astra 2Q26 was completed, along with the conference call.

The arrangement worked: Family determined the geography of the day, while Career continued inside it.

---

## 🧭 Artem's Three Roads

Artem called again about his future at the bank.

His performance was not the problem.

There was simply no budget to promote him.

That transformed an abstract career concern into three increasingly concrete roads: take a similar role at a larger bank, move into ECM, or remain where he was and wait.

Waiting had once looked like the default. With no promotion budget, it was becoming a choice with its own cost.

The Warden and Artem agreed on the next move. When Andrey returned from vacation, the conversation would become more blunt.

---

## 🎒 Two Schools

Oksi and Sergey Jr went to School 57 for the exam.

Later, Oksi returned from Parents' Day at School 444 carrying a very different kind of result: excitement.

She liked the school.

She liked the teacher very much.

The Warden could only hope that Sergey Jr would feel the same if School 444 became his place.

For now, one school still held an exam outcome and the other had already won Oksi's confidence.

---

## 🔧 Special Treatment

At Stronghold, Levon's help worked.

The materials arrived and work could resume, though Kadyr still needed a little more.

Another negotiation concerned Roman.

His plumbing prices were close to market, and that continued to bother the Warden. Paying market price was one thing. Paying it to his bro, when better terms had been expected, was another.

The dissatisfaction was made explicit.

Roman agreed to speak to his co-worker and explain that Stronghold needed special treatment.

The price was no longer merely a number on a list. It had become a negotiation.

---

## 🚗 A Promise Kept

During the fuel quest two days earlier, the Warden had made a bargain with his car.

Find fuel, and there would be a proper wash.

The fuel had appeared.

Tonight, sitting at the car wash, the Warden paid his side of the bargain.

Winter tyres would have to wait for winter.

---

## 📚 Steward's Reflection

Day 065 was full of paths that had not yet reached their endings.

Artem could stay, move to ECM or leave for a larger bank. Sergey Jr stood between the uncertainty of School 57 and the unexpectedly strong impression School 444 had made on Oksi. Roman's plumbing economics moved from disappointment into negotiation. Stronghold could resume work, but still needed a little more material.

Not every unresolved path requires an immediate choice.

But uncertainty becomes easier to carry once the next conversation, next result or next missing piece is known.

And amid all those open paths, one small promise was simply completed.

The car got its wash.
