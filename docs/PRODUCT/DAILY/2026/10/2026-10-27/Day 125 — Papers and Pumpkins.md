# Day 125 — Papers and Pumpkins

The Warden is certain the papers are missing.

The Gas Campaign begins with anxiety.

On the way to the gas authority, he takes his favourite hilly road. The hills improve his mood a little. The paperwork, he assumes, will not.

At the office, he hands over the cooker passport.

And learns that everything else has already been submitted.

The documents he feared were lost are on file.

There are signatures to arrange, a cooker number to provide, and further procedures. But now he understands what remains.

The invisible obstacle has become a manageable list.

He would like to enjoy the discovery.

Oksi has other plans.

***

There are boxes to collect.

Halloween decorations.

New Year decorations.

They hurry to the pickup point. Comandante waits while the boxes are carried.

On the way home, he falls asleep.

Twenty minutes.

Too short for a proper afternoon rest, but perhaps long enough to prevent one.

The Warden knows what this means for the hours ahead.

***

They go walking together: the Warden, Comandante and the Middles.

Recently Comandante has objected to walking.

Today he does not.

A small mercy.

Later, the Warden takes him to the shop, not because anything is urgently needed, but because an evening has to pass somehow.

Eventually there is *Kung Fu Panda 3*.

Then bedtime.

***

Oksi opens the boxes.

Pumpkins.

Ghosts.

New Year decorations.

The contents exceed even the Warden's expectations.

Oksi is absolutely happy.

The Gas Campaign has given him relief. The decorations have given her delight.

Both have received an unexpected gift from the day.

But the Warden is exhausted.

He takes Sirius for a walk and goes to bed.

Oksi remains with her treasures.

The house is quiet.

Tomorrow there will still be signatures and cooker numbers, errands and children, and more work to make Stronghold function.

But the missing papers are not missing.

And the pumpkins have arrived.
