# Day 109 — Comandante Comes to Stronghold

Friday.

The Warden stays home.

Oksi has a dentist appointment.

And Owling is Owling again.

At least, that is how he seems today.

The fever has receded from the foreground.

The little boy is in bed.

Looking at a book.

Open at a particular page.

“Conquering the World.”

The parents laugh.

Of course.

Oksi looks at their brave little boy and finds a name.

Comandante.

It sounds like a name that was waiting for him.

The Warden immediately wonders whether it belongs in RealMe too.

Not as something invented by the application.

As something discovered in the Household.

Oksi goes to the dentist.

The Warden stays with Comandante.

On her way back she shops for warm autumn clothes for the children.

Picks Jr up from school.

Another delivery of clothes arrives.

Then the family sets off.

Stronghold.

But the road does not share the morning's humour.

Comandante pulls Jr's hair.

Jr cries.

Oksi shouts.

Alisa is instructed to sit between the boys.

The final minutes are tense.

Arrival does not immediately release it.

Alisa reaches for her phone almost as soon as they enter.

Oksi responds fiercely.

The house is there.

The family has arrived.

Peace has not arrived at precisely the same moment.

And now there is no water.

The Warden heads out again.

The shop is about to close.

Just outside Stronghold, a familiar figure appears.

The hare.

An old friend of these roads.

For a moment it seems to show the way.

The Warden follows the road, not the hare's intentions.

The shop is still open.

Barely.

Water.

More food.

Sweets.

The shopkeepers are angry that closing time has come.

The Warden grabs what he can and heads back.

Stronghold has its supplies.

The children are asleep.

Oksi is tired.

It has been a day of care, errands, travel, tears, raised voices and a last-minute run for water.

But she says something that changes the ending.

With every minute here, she feels better.

Not all the tensions of the journey are resolved.

Nothing about the house is magically finished.

Yet the place is doing something.

The day began with a little conqueror and his new name.

It ends with a tired household inside its walls.

And with Oksi feeling, minute by minute, more at home.
