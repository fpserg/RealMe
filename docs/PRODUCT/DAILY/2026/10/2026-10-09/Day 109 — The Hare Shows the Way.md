# Day 109 — The Hare Shows the Way

Friday.

The Warden stays home.

Oksi has a dentist appointment.

Owling seems better.

Not the weak, silent child who fell asleep in his father's arms.

Owling again.

In bed, he is studying a book.

It happens to be open at a particular page.

*Conquering the World.*

His parents see him there.

They laugh.

Oksi gives the little conqueror a name.

**Comandante.**

The Warden hears something that might belong in RealMe too.

Not a character designed at a desk.

A name discovered in the Household.

In a bed.

Beside a book.

After an illness.

Oksi goes to the dentist.

The Warden watches Comandante.

On the way back she stops for autumn clothes.

Warm things for the children.

Picks Jr up from school.

Another delivery of clothes arrives.

Then the family sets off.

Stronghold.

But Comandante has not retired from campaigning.

In the car he pulls Jr by the hair.

Jr cries.

Oksi shouts.

Alisa is told to sit between the boys.

The last minutes of the drive are full of tension.

Stronghold.

At last.

Alisa immediately reaches for her phone.

Oksi pushes back fiercely.

Arrival has not yet become rest.

Then the Warden discovers a more ordinary emergency.

No water.

The shop is closing.

He leaves again.

Just outside Stronghold, there is someone familiar.

The hare.

Their old friend.

For a moment it is almost as if the animal is showing him the way.

The Warden follows the road.

Reaches the shop in time.

Water.

Some more food.

Sweets.

The shopkeepers are angry.

Closing time.

He takes what he can find.

Heads back.

The children are asleep.

Oksi is tired.

But something about the place is working.

She says she feels better with every minute here.

No grand conquest today.

A book.

A new name.

A difficult drive.

Water before closing.

A hare beside the road.

And a tired Household settling into the Stronghold.

The hare shows the way.

The Stronghold does the rest.
