# Day 126 — Sir Home

Oksi needs soil.

A few bags, and therefore an expedition.

Captain Sparrow, Comandante and the Warden set out for the nursery.

On the way back, the telephone rings.

Mosoblgaz.

The documents that were supposedly submitted are missing after all.

Yesterday's relief vanishes in a sentence.

The Warden is frustrated. But he has a Plan B.

He gathers the children and heads for Capital.

***

The children behave on the road.

At Capital, the Warden turns the document folders inside out.

Nothing.

The second wave of frustration arrives.

Then he notices a folder he does not recognize. Its cover resembles a museum picture.

He opens it.

The gas papers.

Not lost.

Not submitted, apparently.

Simply waiting in a place nobody had thought to look.

For a moment, the Gas Campaign has a hero: an unfamiliar folder.

The Warden buys soft drinks for himself and the Middles.

A modest celebration, entirely earned.

Then they set off for Stronghold.

Two hours, he estimates.

He calls Oksi and asks her to prepare dinner.

Their first proper homemade dinner there.

Meat and potatoes roasting at home.

***

Rush hour.

The Warden works his way through traffic.

Then remembers the milk bottle.

Comandante's bottle is still at Capital.

Back they go.

An extra hour added to the journey.

Another call to Oksi. Dinner will have to wait.

The bottle is recovered.

They depart again.

Then the Middles need a toilet.

Urgently.

The first petrol station has none.

The second has none either.

The third is closed.

A solution is found behind the station.

Another mission completed.

The road resumes.

***

At last, Stronghold.

The roast is waiting.

It is still warm.

Oksi is not entirely pleased. She had imagined a better setting for the first proper family dinner: not an arrival delayed by a forgotten bottle, traffic and three unsuccessful petrol-station stops.

The Warden understands the disappointment.

But the roast is fantastic.

And he thanks her.

For turning The Brick into Sir Home.

***

Stronghold has acquired its identity slowly.

A bathroom. Doors. Lamps. A deck. A tree planted in the ground. Boxes of pumpkins and ghosts.

Much of its history has been written in construction work, deliveries and errands.

Today its first proper homemade family dinner arrives late.

It is not the evening Oksi planned.

The roast is still warm.

The family is finally there.

And the Warden has found a name for what she has been building.

Sir Home.
