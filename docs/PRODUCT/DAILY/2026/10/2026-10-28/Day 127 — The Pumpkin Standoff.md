# Day 127 — The Pumpkin Standoff

The journey from Capital has ended.

Its consequences have not.

Comandante spent nearly the whole previous day in the car. Now his clock has lost its bearings, and much of the night belongs to him.

He is sneezing, too. His nose is running.

By morning, the Warden is tired before the day has properly begun.

But the Gas Campaign does not care about sleep.

***

They return to the gas authority.

The papers, rescued yesterday from the mysterious museum folder, still need corrections.

A few more adjustments.

Then, perhaps, they are right.

The Warden is careful with that word.

*Hopefully.*

The next steps are online. A ladder of documents and signatures, to be climbed one rung at a time.

After the lost papers, the unexpected phone call and the journey to Capital, an online ladder sounds almost merciful.

Almost.

***

At Stronghold, Comandante will not sleep.

He has a cold, so the Warden keeps him indoors.

Kadyr arrives to hang shelves and decorations. Comandante's room is among the places being worked on.

Even if the little commander were willing to rest, his room is not ready for him.

The shelves go up.

The decorations find their places.

At last, Comandante sleeps.

And so does the Warden.

Not for long.

Long enough to remember what rest feels like.

***

Oksi is making pumpkin stew.

She works on it late into the evening.

The Warden is hungry. And tired.

He says, with more irritation than he intends to hide, that perhaps they can eat it on Halloween.

Oksi looks at him coldly.

She takes the stew out of the oven. It is not ready anyway, she says.

Later, the children leave some of theirs unfinished.

Oksi believes the Warden's remark has spoiled the meal.

The Warden believes they simply do not like pumpkin.

Neither interpretation makes the evening easier.

Yesterday, he thanked her for turning The Brick into Sir Home.

Tonight, he wonders how much more the house can demand of him.

***

The children go to bed.

There is still the litter.

Still Sirius to walk.

The Warden performs the familiar evening rituals on weak legs.

Then he, too, can finally go to bed.

Oksi is warmer now.

She says she hopes they will not quarrel here so much.

It is a small sentence.

After the papers, the drives, the interrupted nights, the shelves and the stew, it may be the most important one spoken that evening.

Stronghold has gained another set of decorations.

The gas documents are another step closer to completion.

But the work of making Sir Home a place where they can both be happy is still underway.
