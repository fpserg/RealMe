# Day 117 — The Ice Cracks

Half past seven.

For once, Comandante has been merciful.

After two mornings begun at half past five, the Warden is allowed another two hours of sleep.

There are supplies to gather.

Oksi leaves for the plant nursery.

Comandante agrees to an afternoon nap.

And the Warden returns to the unfinished business of Sir Oak.

The pit is finally ready.

A place in the earth for the enormous tree.

When Oksi returns, she lets him rest.

She notices the bed he has been using in Comandante's room.

It belonged to Jr when he was three.

The Warden hardly fits.

Perhaps, she suggests, he could sleep in Maksim's bed instead.

An ordinary practical kindness.

No ceremony.

In the evening, the family eats together.

The Warden takes Comandante for a walk, then puts him to bed.

The Middles watch the second Pirates of the Caribbean.

But Jr falls asleep before the pirates finish their adventure.

The film is paused.

Another litter drive in the dark.

Then back to Stronghold.

Tea on the deck.

Warm cups.

Warm conversation.

And a question the Warden had not expected.

Would he like to invite his parents to see Stronghold?

For a moment, something that has long been frozen begins to move.

He answers carefully.

Of course he would love to.

But he has not wanted to hurt Oksi by asking.

She does not pretend she would enjoy the visit.

She says plainly that she would not.

Yet she is willing to do it for him.

No invitation has been sent.

No date has been chosen.

The old grievances have not disappeared.

But an opening has appeared where there was a wall.

The Warden spent the day digging a place for a tree.

In the evening, Oksi made room for someone else.

The ice cracked.
