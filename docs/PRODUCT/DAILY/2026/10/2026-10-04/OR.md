## Reflection

Day 104 began at 04:00 and never fully recovered the missing sleep.

Stronghold still moved. Alexey inspected Kadyr's work. The children's rooms can proceed to paint. The stair colour cannot. Kadyr measured the pipes connected to the smell from Maksim's room and took the keys.

For a while the day left Stronghold altogether. The neighbouring settlement changed the scenery: hills, a house beside a lake, a river through the plot, ducks and a sandy beach. The Warden could already imagine the middle children there in summer.

Then the existing Household conflict resumed. The Warden chose rest over approaching Oksi in the garden because he was exhausted and expected confrontation. At departure he spoke roughly to Jr; Jr cried. The sequence then crossed a concrete boundary when Oksi hit the Warden in the face. He reports intense anger and that he deliberately did not hit back.

The drive home was silent. The ordinary nighttime routine did not happen.

No reconciliation is inferred. No larger judgment is added. The day closes with the conflict unresolved.
