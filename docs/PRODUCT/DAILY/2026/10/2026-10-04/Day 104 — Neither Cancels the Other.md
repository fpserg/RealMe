# Day 104 — Neither Cancels the Other

Four in the morning.

Owling wakes.

So the Warden wakes.

At six, Owling decides the day was premature.

Sleep again.

Until nine.

Then morning begins for the second time.

Alexey is due at eleven.

He is coming to inspect Kadyr's work and decide what comes next.

The Warden takes Owling outside first.

A calming walk.

They meet Alexey on the way.

Turn around.

Back to Stronghold.

The inspection produces decisions.

The children's rooms can proceed to painting.

The stairs cannot.

The colour is wrong.

The paint must go back.

Another colour must come home.

Then Alexey asks again.

Come see my house.

Oksi refuses again.

The Warden decides another refusal would be impolite.

He takes Owling.

They go.

It is only the neighbouring settlement.

But the landscape changes completely.

Hills.

An almost alpine shape to the place.

Alexey's house sits at the bottom beside a lake.

A small river runs directly through his land.

Inside:

Pool.

Hamam.

Everything neat.

Everything classy.

Alexey says he wants to show what Kadyr is capable of.

Kadyr built much of it.

The Warden thinks Alexey may also simply want to show off.

Perhaps both can be true.

Owling is less interested in construction quality.

Restrictions on his freedom are unacceptable.

The tour becomes messy.

Then the lake.

Ducks.

A sandy beach.

Water clean enough to swim.

The Warden looks at it and immediately imagines the middles here in summer.

A place they could love.

Then back to Stronghold.

Back to what the Warden calls the cold war.

Oksi goes to the garden.

The Warden comes home with Owling.

Then playground.

Owling attempts his new normal.

Run.

The Warden intercepts him halfway.

Back home.

Lunch for the middles.

Oksi still in the garden.

Kadyr arrives.

There is another Stronghold problem.

The smell from Maksim's room.

Pipes need replacing.

Kadyr measures.

Takes the keys.

Leaves.

Then Owling to bed.

The Warden hesitates.

Go to the garden.

Ask Oksi how he can help.

Or lie down.

He is exhausted.

And he is convinced the first path will become confrontation.

He has no energy for that.

He chooses himself.

Rest.

Owling wakes.

More time together.

Dinner.

The Warden says none was offered to him.

Then departure.

Walking to the car.

The Warden is fastening Owling's belt.

Jr enters with his seat folded.

The Warden speaks roughly to him.

Jr bursts into tears.

Oksi appears.

Pulls Alisa from the car.

The Warden says Alisa is not to blame.

Oksi turns on him.

Shouts.

Hits him in the face.

The Warden feels fury.

Enough that controlling it takes effort.

He does not hit back.

The family gets into the car.

They drive home.

Silence.

Oksi cries a little.

The Warden does not feel sorry for her.

He wants her to feel as bad as he does.

He calls what she did unacceptable.

Then stops there.

RealMe does not judge.

Neither does the Chronicle need to decide what the family is.

It records what happened.

Home.

Quick bath for the children.

Beds.

No regular nighttime together.

The Warden goes to the bedroom.

"Let it be war."

Earlier that same day there had been a lake.

A small river through a garden.

Ducks.

Sand.

A place where the Warden could already see his children swimming.

That happened too.

The beautiful place does not soften the ending.

The ending does not erase the lake.

Day 104 holds both.

Neither cancels the other.
