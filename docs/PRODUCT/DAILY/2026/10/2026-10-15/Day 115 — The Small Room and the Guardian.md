# Day 115 — The Small Room and the Guardian

At half past five, the Warden is awake.

A taxi.

An airport.

A plane.

Everything on time.

A rare morning when the machinery of travel asks for nothing more.

Back home, another journey is taking place.

Oksi gets everyone up and off to school.

Vasya calls.

Jr has been delivered to 444.

Yesterday's offer has become today's relief.

At the gate, the Warden meets Dima from another bank.

His first question is about bonuses.

Theirs have been cut.

One more sign, perhaps, of a difficult season in banking.

Then Saransk.

A pretty town glimpsed on the way to a factory the Warden has seen before.

Two years ago, before the IPO, he walked these grounds.

Much is familiar.

But there is a new oncology department.

A second line for Tirzetta.

And somewhere inside, a small room where a medicine that brings in billions is made.

The contrast stays with him.

So much commercial weight.

So little physical space.

Walking beside the CFO, he asks about sales and guidance.

He hears of strong momentum and a deliberate caution in forecasts.

The Warden recognizes his own thesis in the answer.

His conviction grows.

Yet the conversation is quiet, heard by few.

What was said, what it means, and what may be used publicly are not the same thing.

The factory tour ends.

Other guests can retreat to hotel rooms.

The Warden has no room.

He is flying home tonight.

So he goes to the museum.

With Dima, Katya and Aleksey, he discovers that a place he might ordinarily have skipped is unexpectedly rich.

They stay until closing.

Then an evening quiz.

Promomed and Mordovia.

The Warden joins a team of fund managers and analysts.

They call themselves the Tricretins.

He likes quizzes.

He gets carried away.

He feels himself taking the lead.

Then the organizers tell him the airport is waiting.

If he wants dinner, he had better eat.

He leaves the table.

They call him back.

The results are being announced.

The winners are...

The Tricretins.

A small triumph at the end of a very long day.

At the airport, he gathers gifts for Oksi and the children.

Among them is Rod, a little figure of an old Slavic god.

A future guardian for Stronghold.

Home at last.

Oksi has prepared roast meat.

The Warden tells his stories, still bright with the day.

She is tired.

Her welcome is real; her energy is limited.

He goes to bed after more than twenty hours awake.

He has seen a small room producing something enormous.

He has won a quiz with a ridiculous name.

And he has brought home a guardian who has not yet found his place.

The Stronghold will be waiting.
