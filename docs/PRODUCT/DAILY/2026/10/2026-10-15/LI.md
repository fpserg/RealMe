Important finding: I walked by CFO and discussed things, and he said that Tirzetta sales are exploding. I asked - why do they still guide so modestly? And he said that it is us analysts who taught them to be extremely conservative. I’m reading this as a confirmation of my hypothesis that guidance beat is inevitable.
