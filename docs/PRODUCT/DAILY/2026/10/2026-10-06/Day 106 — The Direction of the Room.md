# Day 106 — The Direction of the Room

Five in the morning.

Owling is awake.

No return to sleep this time.

He simply stays awake until everyone else's morning arrives.

Jr does not join it properly.

He feels sick.

Stays home.

Then office.

One more Strategy call.

Lisa remains on her deserved vacation.

She does not join.

So Pasha, the economist, fills the gap.

The call goes ahead.

The campaign is nearly done.

Except the Strategy itself is not finished travelling.

Denis and Alexey want the Warden to present it at a university round table.

Academic society.

Business society.

And among the expected participants is someone the Warden knows from another arrangement of the same world.

A professor.

International stock markets.

Master's degree.

Back then, the Warden sat in the room and listened.

Now he may return carrying his own Strategy.

The subject has not changed completely.

The direction has.

Next week suddenly looks very full.

Platform Economy.

Bank management.

Strategy.

University.

Promomed.

Plant.

Three professional rooms in one week.

Three different audiences.

And today one of them becomes more real.

The Platform presentation draft is ready.

Submitted to Andrey.

Not finished.

Not accepted yet.

But no longer merely being assembled.

Home.

The atmosphere there has not transformed as cleanly.

Hostility remains.

Still, a few words are exchanged.

After the preceding silence, that is a change.

Only that.

Not reconciliation.

Jr is still sick.

His teacher says several other children from the class stayed home too.

No explanation follows.

Owling offers the opposite problem.

Energy.

Plenty of it.

Then Night Watch.

Dinner.

Sirius walk.

No dramatic ending.

Quietly to bed.

Next week is waiting with bank management, a factory, and a university room where the Warden may find an old professor sitting on the other side.

Once he listened.

Now he presents.

The direction of the room has changed.
