# Day 114 — The Artichoke

The morning begins with a neighbour.

Vasya.

A familiar face on the school run, and suddenly a useful one.

His son now goes to Jr's school.

Tomorrow the Warden must leave early to visit Promomed's production facilities.

There will be no time to take Jr himself.

Oksi has been worried about the commute.

Vasya says he can help.

Nothing is settled yet.

But the possibility alone changes the shape of the problem.

Sometimes a difficult morning needs no grand plan.

Only a neighbour going the same way.

At the office, Career is unusually quiet.

The Warden turns some attention to Tower and RealMe.

Two other worlds that rarely stop asking to be built.

Then home.

Comandante and Jr need collecting.

Football is waiting.

The family races against the clock.

Almost on time.

The weather is wet.

Comandante objects.

He has his own opinion of rain.

And then, at Stronghold, a different kind of weather changes.

Oksi's spirits lift.

There are new artifacts.

A lantern from another century.

A ladder that is also a shelf.

A little ceramic house.

And an enormous plaster artichoke.

An artichoke.

Of all the things a house might need.

The Stronghold has spent so many days demanding pipes, paint, pits and repairs.

Now it is acquiring objects that do not have to solve anything.

They only have to belong.

The school commute is not yet arranged.

Tomorrow's factory visit still lies ahead.

But tonight there is a neighbour's offer, a football run nearly on time, and an artichoke large enough to change the mood of a room.
