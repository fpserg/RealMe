# Day 120 — A Degree in Finance

The Warden carries waste paper to school with Jr.

A respectable beginning.

The paper will be recycled.

The Warden will go to work.

Between these two obligations, he stops for a cigarette and an energy drink.

A stranger asks him to buy a beer.

He does.

The stranger thanks him and admires his appearance.

Does he work for the government?

No.

A bank.

This interests the stranger.

He has a degree in finance, he says.

And so, beside a bench and a newly acquired beer, two men discuss monetary policy.

The Central Bank.

Nabiullina.

Matters usually reserved for offices, conference rooms and people who have brought their own coffee.

Then the Warden goes underground.

The stranger stays with his beer.

Only later does the Warden recognize the scene.

Dovlatov.

There is nothing to add.

***

At the office, the pharmaceutical market report is finally ready.

After revisions and Andrey's earlier objections, it leaves the Warden's hands and reaches Andrey's desk.

Not approved yet.

But finished, and awaiting judgment.

Ozon's model is being rebuilt for the third-quarter preview.

The Warden leaves early.

Oksi is supposed to attend Parents' Day.

Instead, a terrible headache keeps her away.

The Warden takes Jr to football.

Comandante comes too.

Jr plays.

Comandante protests the arrangement with sustained tears.

Oksi visits a furniture shop.

***

Dinner.

Bath.

Bedtime for Comandante.

And then the Warden receives a presentation.

Oksi has found a sofa.

She is certain he will love it.

The Warden understands.

The important vote has already been cast.

The sofa is not purchased.

Not yet.

But he suspects its future is more secure than that of many proposals he has seen at work.

In the morning, he bought a stranger a beer and discussed the Central Bank.

In the evening, he listened to a proposal for household capital expenditure.

Between them, he delivered a pharmaceutical report for review and accompanied a small, furious spectator to football.

The day began with waste paper.

It ended with a sofa that may soon exist in their home.

And somewhere on a bench above the metro, a man with a degree in finance had a beer.
