# Day 130 — The Face at the Window

Comandante finds his third apple in two days.

The Warden has gone to the shop for Halloween sweets. Comandante has a simpler objective: seize the fruit and let it accompany him through the morning.

The next expedition takes everyone to Dmitrov in search of Halloween accessories. It does not go smoothly. Comandante grows restless, and the Warden waits with him in the car. Oksi returns from shopping upset with the Middles. The cause remains unclear.

***

Back at Stronghold, Oksi returns to the garden. Comandante refuses his nap, so the Warden drives out again and brings back coffee.

Approaching the house, he spots a Grim Reaper balloon floating above the fence.

A splendid sight.

He calls Oksi to see it and hands her the coffee. For a moment, the mood lifts.

But the household is still strained. The Middles quarrel when asked to watch Comandante. The Warden loses his temper and shouts. Everyone is upset, including the youngest, frightened by the noise.

Halloween appears to be slipping away.

There is time apart, a walk, a nap, gardening, lunch, and eventually a conversation about behaviour. The children seem remorseful.

Then comes a Halloween film.

And waiting.

***

A knock on the window.

The Warden turns and sees a white face, black eyes and black lips.

For one instant, the fright is real.

Then he recognises the guisers.

Three girls have come to Stronghold.

The Middles appear in vampire cloaks, with theatrical blood on their faces. Oksi joins them. Sweets are exchanged, and the visitors admire the decorations.

The Warden imagines Oksi's pride.

The children are delighted.

The day is saved — not because its difficult hours disappear, but because it still finds a better ending.

***

The Middles help with Comandante after the film. Bedtime goes smoothly. Oksi and the Warden finally have time together.

And the Warden realises something new: these may be the first holidays at Stronghold he will regret seeing end.

After so many months in which the house demanded work, money and patience, it has begun to offer something in return.

A place where the family wants to remain.

***

Late that night, on the litter errand, the window is iced and visibility poor. A hare darts across the road. The Warden brakes, and the animal disappears into the darkness.

Another unexpected visitor.

The day began with an apple, almost fell apart in the afternoon, and found its way back through a face at the window.

For one Halloween night, Sir Home was not merely decorated.

It was visited.
