# Day 111 — The Water Runs Again

Stronghold morning.

No expedition to begin with.

No great declaration.

Comandante is covered in red dots.

Yet he seems well.

The Warden watches him with the uncertainty that comes after a child's illness.

Then Navruz arrives.

The bathroom sink.

One of the last remaining marks on a campaign that has stretched across so many days.

He fixes it.

Almost the final tick.

But there is another matter beneath the ground.

The sewage cover.

Navruz helps open it.

Paper has blocked the pipe.

Something has to be done.

Navruz leaves.

The Warden calls the family.

Come and watch.

He is going down.

Into the hole.

The children and Oksi are there as he descends.

A shovel.

A blocked pipe.

An unpleasant smell.

And then—

Water.

Moving again.

It runs.

A simple result, entirely visible.

The Warden feels good.

Not because the work was pleasant.

Because it worked.

Climbing out, he gets his jacket dirty all over.

The children help clean it.

The Stronghold has another small victory.

Above ground, another campaign continues.

Oksi is trying to finish the gardening before sunset.

Comandante goes for a walk.

On the way, the Warden digs a few shovelfuls of earth.

A beginning for the place where Sir Oak will stand.

The huge tree has a name.

And now, almost, a place.

Then the road home.

The Middles remember something.

Homework.

Not done.

So the evening compresses.

A quick shower for Comandante.

Quick homework for the Middles.

Beds.

The Warden and Oksi are exhausted.

The kind of exhaustion that should send them straight to sleep.

Instead, they watch Instagram Shorts.

A few more moments of the day, borrowed from the night.

The sink is fixed.

The blockage is cleared.

The water runs again.

The oak is still waiting.

And the Stronghold, little by little, becomes a place that works.
