# Day 112 — The Key One

In Denis' office, the Warden is asked a question.

Could the Ministry of Finance take the Russian market on a roadshow to the Middle East?

Would he be willing to go?

He says yes.

There are moments when hesitation feels like the wrong answer.

Meanwhile, his phone fills with missed calls.

Jr must be registered for the maths olympiad.

Now.

Denis catches him looking at the screen.

The Warden waits until the meeting is over.

Then the registration is done.

Another meeting.

Another rehearsal.

The Platform Economy presentation should take thirty minutes.

It still takes sixty.

Then the presentation itself.

Only one person comes in person.

But she is the key one.

Oksana, a vice president.

Once a colleague at Raiffeisen.

Does she remember him?

He cannot tell.

The presentation goes well.

Perhaps the report should become a regular publication.

On the way out, another thread from Raiffeisen.

Anastasia's husband.

A few words.

Please pass along my greetings.

The Warden remembers his old boss.

The best he has had, he still thinks.

And sometimes he misses those days.

Alexey from ECM calls.

The IPO is postponed.

Suddenly, there may be room for a week at Stronghold during school holidays.

He calls Oksi with the news.

Her response hurts.

He knows she is tired.

He knows his busy week is hard on her as well.

But he did not expect this.

School 57.

Jr collected.

The boys to Oston for haircuts long overdue.

Oston thought they might have been offended.

They were only busy.

Home.

Quick snacks.

Beds.

Oksi is still in a bad mood.

The day has brought recognition, opportunity, old memories and a little space in the calendar.

But the household is tired.

One person in the room was enough for the presentation.

There is no such shortcut for everything waiting at home.
