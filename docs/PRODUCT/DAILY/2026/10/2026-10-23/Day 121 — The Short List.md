# Day 121 — The Short List

The Warden takes Jr to school.

Then transfers Oksi the money for the sofa.

Yesterday it was a proposal.

Today it has financing.

Whether the sofa has actually been purchased is another matter.

At the office, the usual work continues.

Ozon's model needs attention.

The pharmaceutical report is with Andrey, awaiting his judgment.

There is a brokerage call.

Junior Analyst Sergey has built a Telegram bot to watch prices for GLP-1 medicines.

A small machine to keep an eye on a market that refuses to stand still.

Then Andrey calls the Warden and Alexander.

There are targets to meet.

More short recommendations.

More pair trades.

Good ideas, naturally.

The difficulty is that the list of stocks approved for short recommendations is itself short.

A short list of shorts.

Andrey wants quantity.

Andrey wants quality.

Both, preferably without compromise.

Afterward, the Warden quietly tells Alexander that they should choose good ideas rather than manufacture them.

Perhaps, he suggests, they should not hurry to close successful recommendations.

The targets have begun to influence not only what is recommended, but how long a recommendation might live.

***

The Warden and Alexander record a discussion about the platform economy.

It is not live.

Errors can be edited away.

But one deadline cannot.

Jr is waiting at School 57.

The Warden leaves the world of platforms and picks him up.

At home, Oksi is in a bad mood.

The reason gradually emerges.

She has bought more decorations for Stronghold.

All the savings set aside for Stronghold decorations are gone.

Spent, as it happens, on Stronghold decorations.

The destination of the money has not changed.

Only its intended itinerary.

***

School holidays are approaching.

There are tickets to a Gazan concert.

The Warden is not entirely sure who Gazan is.

The children, apparently, are.

Somebody must accompany them.

The other parent will remain with Comandante.

The Warden discovers that the concert assignment is likely his.

At least it is a fair division of duties.

Late at night, the children are still whispering in their room.

Perhaps they are thinking of the break.

Perhaps of the concert.

They are not ready to sleep.

The Warden has spent the day navigating lists.

The approved list of shorts.

The list of research obligations.

The list of things Stronghold still needs.

And the family calendar, where the most important assignments have a way of appearing after the workday is supposedly over.

Some lists are too short.

Others never seem to end.
