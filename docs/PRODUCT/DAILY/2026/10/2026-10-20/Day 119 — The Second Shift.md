# Day 119 — The Second Shift

The office is quiet.

Not empty of work, exactly.

Just free of the urgent demands that usually decide the shape of the day.

The Warden turns to Ozon.

A financial model needs rearranging before the third-quarter results arrive.

There are Artem's notes from the Oil & Gas forum to read.

And the pharmaceutical market report, still waiting to become what Andrey asked for: shorter, sharper, finished.

The Warden wants to complete it today.

Whether he does remains outside the record.

Then home.

A walk with Comandante and Jr.

Oksi leaves for her manicure.

The Warden takes the evening watch.

There are dad-made pelmeni for dinner.

There is time to find for the Middles.

And there is Comandante, who has no interest in following the Warden's timetable.

The hours pass.

Ten o'clock.

Then eleven.

At last, Comandante sleeps.

The Middles are still making noise.

Oksi is still away.

A quiet day at the office has turned into a long shift at home.

She returns around midnight.

She sees the tiredness on his face.

Two days ago, she noticed it before he did and offered him rest.

Tonight, the same tiredness becomes a point of conflict.

The Warden goes to bed frustrated.

Later, Oksi apologizes.

The apology does not give back the hours, or make the evening easy.

But it changes where the story ends.

There was no great victory today.

No completed model or published report recorded.

Only an ordinary working day, followed by the other work that waits at home.

And a small repair, made after midnight.
