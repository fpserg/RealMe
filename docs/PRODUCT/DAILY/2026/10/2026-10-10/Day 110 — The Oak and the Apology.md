# Day 110 — The Oak and the Apology

The Middles want to come along.

It is a supplies quest, but they ask to join it.

So the Warden drives them to the forest spring.

The path runs downhill.

Slippery.

Alisa declares that she always seeks adrenaline.

Jr has a different philosophy.

He always seeks safety, he says.

But he always has to follow Alisa.

And then he slips.

At the spring, everyone finds a role.

Jr helps pour water into the canister.

Alisa helps carry it uphill.

Comandante surveys the operation from the Warden's shoulders.

A victorious commander who does not need to carry the water.

Afterwards, sweets and soft drinks.

Payment for a completed quest.

Back at Stronghold, the Warden remembers last weekend.

He does not want another failure carried forward.

So he goes straight to the garden.

Flower pits.

Tree pits.

Quite a lot of them.

There is work to be done and, this time, he does it.

Then Comandante sleeps.

The Warden lies down beside him.

A deserved nap.

Oksi goes on her own quest.

For soil.

She returns with a huge oak.

The Warden is not surprised.

A large tree has joined the campaign.

Then another expedition is proposed.

The DIY hypermarket.

Paint, and other things they still need.

The Warden wants to leave quickly.

Oksi erupts.

The road is silent.

At the shop the tension continues.

Yet the supplies are bought.

The mission is completed.

Comandante sleeps in the back seat.

Home.

Quick snacks for the children.

Beds.

Then Oksi says she is sorry.

Perhaps, she says, she often overreacts.

Often?

The Warden's question remains in parentheses, but not out of the story.

The apology is real.

So is his skepticism.

No account of the day requires one to erase the other.

A spring.

A slippery path.

Two different philosophies of life.

A commander on his father's shoulders.

Pits dug into the earth.

A great oak brought home.

A difficult evening.

And an apology before the day ends.
