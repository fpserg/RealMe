# Day 110 — Adrenaline, Safety, and an Oak

The Middles want to join the quest.

It is supposed to be a supplies run.

But the spring is in the forest, and the path slopes downhill, slippery underfoot.

Alisa has a declaration to make.

She always seeks adrenaline.

Jr has a declaration of his own.

He always seeks safety.

Unfortunately, he adds, he always has to follow Alisa.

And then he slips.

There is something almost perfectly timed about it.

At the spring, the expedition finds its order.

Jr helps pour water into the canister.

Alisa helps carry it uphill.

Comandante observes the whole operation from the Warden's shoulders, victorious without needing to carry a thing.

The reward is sweets and soft drinks.

The quest is complete.

But the Stronghold has other quests waiting.

The Warden remembers last weekend's failure and does not want to bring it into this one.

He starts digging.

Pits for flowers.

Pits for trees.

Quite a lot of them.

The work is real, even if the planting is not yet finished.

Then Comandante sleeps.

And the Warden, who has carried children and water and dug the earth, lies down beside him.

A deserved nap.

Oksi has gone on a soil quest of her own.

She returns with a huge oak.

The Warden is not surprised.

Of course she has.

The day might have ended with that tree.

Instead, there is paint to buy.

And other things.

The Warden suggests they hurry to the DIY hypermarket.

Oksi explodes with fury.

The road is silent.

At the store, the tension continues.

Still, they get what they came for.

On the way home Comandante sleeps in the back seat, uninvolved in the adults' quarrel.

Home.

Quick snacks for the children.

Beds.

Then Oksi says she is sorry.

Perhaps, she says, she often overreacts.

*Often?!*

The Warden hears the word and measures it against his own experience.

It is an apology.

It is not proof that everything has changed.

The day holds both things.

The forest expedition and the quarrel.

The pits dug and the tree still to plant.

A little boy high on his father's shoulders and later asleep.

Alisa seeking adrenaline.

Jr seeking safety.

And, by night, an acknowledgment that perhaps the adults have been seeking their way through something difficult too.
