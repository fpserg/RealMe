# Day 113 — The Same Formula

The Warden arrives at the office with another audience ahead of him.

The Higher School of Economics.

A round table.

Before that, the usual machinery of research must keep moving.

Juniors are assembling pharmaceutical market statistics.

Artem is working on a CNRU–HEAD trading idea.

There is also Yandex.

Damage to data centers.

An attempt to measure its consequences.

A number emerges, rough and provisional.

One hundred billion rubles.

A number that still needs its meaning and its evidence.

At the department meeting, another question.

Should they enter a rating hosted by their peers?

The room does not agree.

No decision yet.

Then the Warden leaves with Alexey from ECM.

At the round table there are academics and business professionals.

Different disciplines.

Different languages for measuring the same world.

A professor speaks about a formula.

The Warden listens.

It is familiar.

His team has used the same underlying approach in its market estimates.

They had not known its academic name.

An unexpected meeting between practice and theory.

Not proof that the numbers are right.

But a reason to look more closely.

His presentation is welcomed.

Afterwards, the host professor returns to an earlier idea.

Perhaps he could give research workshops.

This time the Warden gives her his business card.

They will stay in touch.

Another possibility is left open.

Then home.

Oksi is exhausted.

She moves through the household chores without speaking.

Almost mechanically, in the Warden's eyes.

He feels sorry for her.

And part of him wonders whether she is overplaying it.

He cannot see the whole of her day.

Only its end.

Two rooms.

In one, the Warden's work finds recognition and an unexpected connection.

In the other, the work of the household continues in silence.

At the university, two approaches turn out to share the same formula.

At home, the equation is harder to solve.
