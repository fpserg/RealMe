# Day 118 — Roots and a Tooth

Quarter past seven.

Comandante has been almost generous again.

Oksi looks at the Warden and says he is tired.

He disagrees.

Perhaps she sees it before he does.

At the playground, Comandante finds an artifact.

His mission accomplished, he races to the stroller and demands transport home.

A familiar expedition: find treasure, call for extraction.

At Stronghold, there are lamps to be hung.

Aleksey is expected to explain the task to Kadyr.

For a handful of lamps, the Warden wonders whether everyone is spending more time discussing the work than doing it.

Comandante's nap is less cooperative than yesterday.

Eventually, the Warden escapes to the garden.

The pit is ready.

Sir Oak has been waiting.

He asks Oksi whether they should plant him.

They do.

Soil.

Water.

Another request.

And perhaps that familiar displeased expression on the Warden's face.

The ghost of Stronghold's old quarrels hovers nearby.

They recognize it.

This time, they try to keep it from taking hold.

The oak goes into the earth.

A tree that might stand long after the work of building this house is forgotten.

The Warden feeds the Middles and restarts the pirates' interrupted voyage.

He takes what little rest Comandante's nap permits.

Oksi works outside until sunset.

Autumn is stealing minutes from every evening.

Dinner.

Packing.

The road back.

At home, irritation returns quickly.

Something Maksim's grandfather has done or said feels intrusive to Oksi.

The details remain outside the day's record.

The Warden puts Comandante to bed and goes looking for fuel.

Then Jr emerges after bedtime.

He has something to show.

A tooth.

One that has just fallen out.

He knows what this means.

The Tooth Fairy owes him a visit.

Fortunately, the treasury has a thousand rubles in cash.

One thing was planted today.

Another came loose.

Sir Oak has roots in Stronghold's soil.

Jr has a gap where a tooth used to be.

Both are signs of things growing, in their different ways.

And somewhere between the garden and the children's bedrooms, the Warden is still learning how to keep the peace.
