Home. Oksi has Spanish and I continue with Owling. He is weak and silent, falling asleep in my arms.

When Oksi is done with Spanish she gives Owling medicine. It doesn’t remove fever but Oksi notices red dots on his feet and tells me call ambulance. I’m sure its overreaction, but I’m absolutely not in a position to argue so I call.

When ambulance arrives Owling feels way better. The doctor quickly checks him and leaves. Oksi and I sit beside again energetic Owling and I feel warmth between us that seemed gone for the entire week.

When Owling is asleep we spend a normal couple evening together. The hatchet is buried. At cost of Owling’s illness, but I’m sure this tough guy will recover soon.
