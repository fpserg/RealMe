# Day 116 — The Shape in the Fog

Another half past five.

Yesterday, a plane to Saransk.

Today, Comandante.

The morning runs late despite its early start.

Jr worries about school breakfast.

There are many things a child might fear missing.

For him, breakfast is near the top of the list.

At the office, Andrey is sick and absent.

The pressure eases, but the work does not.

An international investor presentation still needs to go out.

Alexey ECM and Artem are involved, though Artem is not there.

With Junior Sergey, the Warden is trying to rescue a pharmaceutical market report from its first review.

Andrey found the draft long and boring.

Now the ambition is smaller, and perhaps sharper.

One page of words.

One page of charts.

Cian announces a dividend above guidance.

The shares do not behave as the Warden expects.

He nevertheless feels that his call to buy Cian against HeadHunter was timely.

Promomed is still on his mind.

He tells colleagues about the possibility of a guidance beat.

Mikhail, Head of Sales, agrees with the logic.

But not with owning the shares.

Too illiquid, he says, in much less polite words.

A secondhand story about hair loss enters the conversation too.

Conviction is one thing.

Evidence and the ability to trade are others.

Then the Warden returns home.

Oksi asks to stop at a home accessories shop before Stronghold.

He agrees eagerly.

After the week she has had, he knows what a few new objects might mean.

They leave late.

Comandante sleeps.

The road is quicker than usual.

But as Stronghold approaches, fog closes around the car.

The road becomes hard to see.

They arrive.

The children go to bed.

The artifacts come out of their wrapping.

And Oksi settles.

Almost at once, she seems calm.

The Warden watches and thinks about everything this house has demanded.

The digging.

The repairs.

The expense.

The arguments.

For this moment, he thinks, he can bear it.

Later he drives the garbage out through the fog.

At the litter plot, a tall figure seems to flash into view in the field.

Human-shaped.

For a moment he hesitates to open the car door.

Then the figure becomes what it was.

Mist and imagination.

There is no stranger waiting in the field.

Only the fog.

And behind it, the Stronghold, with its new artifacts and its sudden quiet.
