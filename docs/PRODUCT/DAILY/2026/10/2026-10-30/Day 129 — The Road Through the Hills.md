# Day 129 — The Road Through the Hills

Comandante wakes ready for battle.

Whatever he was exploring when he went to bed, he intends to continue in the morning.

The Warden's awakening is not gentle.

There is another shop expedition, undertaken for no grander purpose than keeping the little commander occupied.

There, Comandante claims a huge apple.

For the entire morning, the apple succeeds where more elaborate plans might have failed: it keeps him busy.

At Stronghold, they are attempting something ambitious.

A quiet day.

***

Oksi digs in the garden.

The Warden and the Middles keep watch over Comandante.

There are no major undertakings, no expedition to recover missing documents, no urgent race between Capital and Stronghold.

A short nap.

Then a walk with the children.

The sort of day that should be easy to describe, and is not necessarily easy to live.

***

When Oksi finishes gardening, she is hungry.

They head to the fresh-food store by the Warden's favourite road through the hills.

The road itself is part of the pleasure.

Oksi buys plenty.

Comandante finds another apple.

Two shopping trips. Two apples. A small, unexpectedly effective answer to a very energetic day.

The Warden makes a mental note: never take a hungry person grocery shopping.

Back home, there is dinner.

Then an enormous cake.

Jr finishes an oversized portion and wants more.

If only ordinary food inspired the same determination.

***

A new Spider-Man film for the Middles.

Comandante's bedtime.

Sirius to walk.

The evening settles into familiar duties.

Outside the house, however, the village is debating Halloween.

The village chat has its opponents and its supporters.

Stronghold, covered in decorations, has already taken a side.

The Warden and Oksi decide to buy sweets for the trick-or-treaters, and costumes for the Middles.

Not merely to decorate the house.

To join in.

***

A few days ago, Stronghold's identity was captured in a warm roast waiting after a disastrous journey.

Then came the pumpkin standoff.

Now there is a quieter scene: a garden, a drive through the hills, a cake, a film, and a family deciding how to take part in village life.

The sweets have not yet been bought.

The costumes have not yet been chosen.

But the decision has been made.

Sir Home is preparing to open its door.


*Archival note: The two apples were added retrospectively after the original Freeze, from the Warden's post-Freeze LI addition.*
