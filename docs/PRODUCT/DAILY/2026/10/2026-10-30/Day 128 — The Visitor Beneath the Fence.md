# Day 128 — The Visitor Beneath the Fence

*Reconstructed Chronicle. The original Living Input disappeared before it could be preserved. This account uses only the surviving facts approved by the Warden; their precise sequence is not known.*

A visitor finds a way into Stronghold.

Not through the gate.

Under the fence.

A puppy.

And there is Sirius, suddenly with someone to play with.

For all the work that has gone into making Stronghold secure and complete, life has discovered a small opening of its own.

***

There is an expedition, too.

The Warden, Captain Sparrow and Comandante go looking for kitchen ceramics.

White ceramics.

Decorations.

A warehouse full of the ordinary objects by which a building slowly acquires the character of a home.

Somewhere among the day's adventures, a rooster appears.

No great quest requires a rooster.

But a Chronicle is not only a record of great quests.

***

At Stronghold, the meat-and-potato roast reaches its end.

Comandante explores.

And makes a mess.

A house being turned into Sir Home must make room for that as well: for small hands, unexpected discoveries and things that refuse to stay neatly arranged.

The shelves can be hung.

The decorations can be placed.

Comandante will still find something to rearrange.

***

And Oksi says something the Warden will remember.

“The best husband in the world.”

After the previous evening's pumpkin standoff, those words belong in the record.

Not because they settle every disagreement.

Because they happened.

***

The children go to bed.

There is *Kung Fu Panda 4*.

There is the familiar litter duty.

The daily rituals remain, even when the day itself has slipped partly out of reach.

This Chronicle was recovered from fragments.

A puppy beneath the fence.

A rooster.

White ceramics.

A child's mess.

A wife's affectionate words.

Perhaps that is enough to recognize the day.

Not every part of a life survives in perfect order.

Sometimes what remains is the shape of a home, and the people and creatures moving through it.
