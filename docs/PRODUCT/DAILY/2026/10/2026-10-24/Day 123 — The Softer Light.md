# Day 123 — The Softer Light

Stronghold day.

Oksi wakes in a better mood.

Yesterday ended coldly. Today there is somewhere to go.

But first, a birthday.

Alisa has been invited to a classmate's party, and the Warden takes her there with Comandante.

Oksi will collect her shortly.

Shortly proves to be a flexible unit of time.

While the preparations continue, the Warden notices a delivery notification.

The angry plush bear he ordered some time ago has arrived.

He finds an excuse to go and collect it.

The bear is angry.

Oksi is not.

She likes it very much.

Then she goes for Alisa.

More time passes.

The two return with bags of decorations.

Stronghold has not yet been reached, but its contents have already increased.

***

Eventually, they set off.

The road, mercifully, is fast.

At Stronghold, Kadyr has been at work.

There are lamps on the deck and in the Royal Bathroom, otherwise known as Captain Nemo's Office.

The deck lamp is bright.

Perhaps too bright.

After the children are in bed, the Warden goes out again.

Water.

Batteries.

Light bulbs.

He replaces the deck bulb with something gentler.

Comandante has his evening activities.

The Middles have *Kung Fu Panda 2*.

Or would have had it, had they behaved.

The film does not survive the evening.

Eventually, everyone is in bed.

Stronghold is quiet.

The new light is softer.

***

Oksi is happy to be here, but tomorrow has already begun to trouble her.

She must leave the place she loves to collect the pets and bring them back.

And there are three viewings of Capital scheduled for the same day.

The Warden suggests a pause.

They have spent so much on Stronghold that selling Capital may not yet allow them to buy the apartment they want.

The viewings have a purpose.

But perhaps the purpose needs money they no longer have available.

Nothing is decided.

For now, they are in Stronghold.

A plush bear has made Oksi smile.

Kadyr's lamps are hanging where they belong.

The Warden has corrected one of them.

Outside, the next day's errands are waiting.

Inside, the deck has just enough light.
