# Day 122 — The Balcony and the Pit

The alarm rings.

The Warden does not hear it.

Oksi does.

She wakes him, and the day begins without tenderness.

On the way to school, Jr announces that he and Alisa want to make a poster for the Gazan concert.

The Warden has not been looking forward to Gazan.

Then he remembers where they are going.

Gorbushka.

Once, his parents took him there to his first rock concert.

Alisa.

He was somewhere between the ages of Maksim and his daughter now.

Later, he went there for punk concerts, descending from the balcony into the dancing pit, where individual people disappeared into the crowd.

His daughter is named Alisa for a reason.

When she was born, he brought her and Oksi home in a car decorated with a black flag bearing the band's A.

It was a private tribute.

Years later, she is preparing a poster for a singer her father barely knows.

***

Before vacation, the Warden tries to leave the office in order.

The Yandex preview is checked with Investor Relations.

Oil and Gas models go to Artem.

The pharmaceutical market report, after all its revisions, is good.

But it is not going out.

Andrey wants another question settled: can they use DSM Group's data safely, after the earlier trouble with InfoLINE?

The Warden suspects delay.

The permission question nevertheless remains.

He leaves it with Alexander and the juniors.

Then he hurries home.

Maksim and Alisa have been slow to answer their mother's calls.

Oksi is already angry.

The concert party leaves, narrowly on time.

***

The Warden arrives early at Gorbushka.

He remembers queues and drunken fans.

Instead, the entrance is clear.

There is time for soft drinks and crisps.

They sit in the parterre, where the dancing pit used to be.

The hall is full of schoolchildren.

“Gazan! Gazan!”

Some things do not change.

Gazan appears.

The children scream.

Alisa and Maksim record everything.

Jr rocks along with a poster drawn in pencil.

The Warden looks toward the balcony.

Somewhere up there, a little boy is watching Alisa.

Soon he will go down to the pit and disappear into the crowd.

The boy is still there, in a way.

And down below, his daughter bears the band's name.

***

The concert lasts about an hour.

Then they wait for the VIP photographs and autographs.

Rather a long wait, the Warden thinks, for such a short concert.

Eventually Gazan comes.

There are pictures.

There are signatures.

The children hand him their posters.

On the way back to the car, they seem absolutely happy.

The Warden has done what his parents once did for him.

He has taken his children to see someone they love.

***

At home, the happiness does not travel quite so far.

The children go to bed.

Oksi reproaches the Warden for having fun while she works all the time.

He answers only in his head.

The concert was her idea.

They had exchanged duties because going to Gorbushka seemed more exhausting than staying with Comandante.

But he thinks he knows what lies beneath the argument.

Friday is usually for Stronghold.

This Friday was not.

There is no resolution.

They go to bed cold toward one another.

The children have their photographs and autographs.

The Warden has an old balcony, a vanished dancing pit, and the memory of a black flag on a car carrying his newborn daughter home.

One evening has become two different memories.

Both will remain.
