# Day 124 — Under the Stars

Oksi leaves for Capital.

The Warden stays with the children.

It is another day divided between the two homes, with each parent taking a different part of the work.

Then Oksi sends a voice message.

She has lost her cardholder.

The bank cards can be replaced, more or less easily.

The driving licence is another matter.

By the time she returns, she is tired and sad.

The Warden helps bring in the pets.

There are more Stronghold decorations too.

The house continues to fill with things chosen for it.

Something important, meanwhile, is missing.

***

Oksi has run out of nasal spray.

The Warden takes Comandante to the pharmacy.

The Middles receive another chance to watch *Kung Fu Panda*.

Yesterday, the film was defeated by their behaviour.

Today, at least, there is a second attempt.

The children go to bed.

The Warden heads out again.

Litter.

Petrol.

The small necessities of a household do not arrange themselves.

Later, he takes Sirius for a walk.

It is late.

There are stars overhead.

He hopes the dog is happy here.

For a little while there are no errands to complete, no viewings to discuss, no papers to find.

Just Sirius, the night, and the place they have been trying so hard to make a home.

***

Then tomorrow returns.

The Gas Campaign is coming.

The Warden looks for the documents.

There is the cooker passport, the one that had been missing before.

But where is everything else?

Perhaps the papers have already been submitted.

Perhaps they are in Moscow.

He cannot remember.

Oksi's cardholder is lost.

The gas papers might not be.

But at this hour the difference offers little comfort.

He is anxious.

It is late.

There is nothing more he can usefully settle tonight.

He goes to bed.

The pets are here.

The house is fuller.

Outside, the stars are still there.
