# Operational Record

## Household Realm

### Family / Owling and Alisa

- Oksi and Sergey Jr left for a football competition; the Warden stayed with Owling and Alisa.
- A regular playground outing with Owling became difficult when Owling found his way home while the stroller remained at the playground.
- The Warden hoped Alisa could help retrieve the stroller, but she was not around.
- He therefore carried a fiercely struggling and yelling Owling back to the playground.
- Once reunited with the stroller, Owling fell asleep in it.
- Alisa later returned home after riding a bicycle with a new friend.
- The Warden would ordinarily have welcomed this straightforwardly, but in the moment it coincided with needing her help and with anxiety because he had not known where she had gone.
- Preserve both parts: the positive development of a new friend and the situational concern.

### Family / Stronghold Conflict

- Oksi and Jr returned carrying wall paint and new flowers.
- During dinner Jr spilled water.
- Oksi yelled at Jr and at the Warden; the Warden talked back.
- The Warden interpreted the exchange as evidence that the previous day's Stronghold argument had not yet settled.
- Later he chose an outside Stronghold circuit partly to avoid further escalation.
- Preserve this sequence without broader diagnosis or assigning objective blame.

### Stronghold / Furnishing

- The Warden completed the furnishing assembly.
- Chairs: six of six assembled.
- Table: one of one assembled.
- The furniture-assembly task is complete.

### Stronghold / Evening Circuit

- After Owling was in bed and the furniture was complete, the Warden carried empty boxes to the garbage lot and then went for fuel.
- He described this as his regular Stronghold evening meditation.
- On this occasion, the circuit also served the practical purpose of creating distance from the unresolved argument and reducing the chance of escalation.

### Mobility / Car

- The car's light bulbs failed.
- Replacement had previously been postponed.
- It is now a necessary maintenance task rather than optional deferred work.

## Reflection

Day 103 repeatedly separated people from the things they needed.

Owling reached home while the stroller remained at the playground. Alisa was safely riding with a new friend, but the Warden did not know that when he needed her help. The previous day's argument remained present even after the family returned with paint and flowers.

Some things did close cleanly. The last two chairs and the table were assembled. The empty boxes could leave Stronghold.

The Warden then took the familiar boxes-and-fuel circuit. It was an ordinary Stronghold evening meditation, but also a deliberate way to stay away long enough for the Household temperature not to rise further.

And the car returned one deferred task of its own. The bulbs that could previously wait can no longer wait.
