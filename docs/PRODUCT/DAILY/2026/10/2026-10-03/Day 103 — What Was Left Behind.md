# Day 103 — What Was Left Behind

Oksi and Jr leave for the football competition.

The Warden stays with Owling and Alisa.

Playground.

A regular venture.

Until Owling decides to go home.

He succeeds.

The stroller does not.

It remains at the playground.

This creates a problem.

The Warden needs to retrieve it.

Alisa could help pull it back.

Except Alisa is nowhere around.

So Owling must return to the playground too.

Owling strongly disagrees with this operational requirement.

The Warden carries him.

Owling fights.

Yells.

Struggles fiercely.

All the way back.

They reach the stroller.

Owling gets in.

And falls asleep.

Naturally.

Alisa eventually returns.

She has been riding a bicycle with her new friend.

Normally the Warden would simply be happy.

A new friend.

A bicycle.

Independence.

Today the happiness arrives mixed with irritation and anxiety.

He had needed her.

And more importantly, he had not known where she had gone.

Both things can be true.

Then Oksi and Jr return.

They bring paint for the walls.

New flowers.

Stronghold continues to absorb objects and intentions.

At dinner, Jr spills water.

Oksi yells at him.

Then at the Warden.

The Warden talks back.

Apparently yesterday's argument had not understood that a new day had begun.

It had come along.

Later Owling is finally in bed.

There is work that can actually be finished.

The chairs.

Yesterday:

Four of six.

Today:

Five.

Six.

Then the table.

Yesterday:

Zero of one.

Today:

One of one.

Six of six.

One of one.

Done.

The empty boxes remain.

So the Warden carries them out.

Garbage lot.

Then fuel.

His regular Stronghold evening meditation.

Driving.

Darkness.

Tasks simple enough to have edges.

Tonight there is another reason to go.

The Warden does not particularly want to be home.

Distance is preferable to escalation.

This does not resolve the argument.

It simply gives it fewer opportunities to become larger.

Boxes leave.

Fuel enters.

The road passes beneath the car.

Then the car presents its own overdue claim.

The light bulbs are gone.

The Warden had postponed replacing them.

For a while, postponement had been a choice.

Now it is not.

Another deferred task has returned.

Day 103 is full of things left somewhere else.

The stroller at the playground.

Alisa somewhere on her bicycle.

Yesterday's argument inside today's dinner.

Four unfinished chairs and an untouched table.

Empty boxes inside a newly furnished house.

A maintenance task postponed until the bulbs finally failed.

One by one, the Warden retrieves or removes them.

The stroller comes back.

Alisa comes home.

The chairs and table are finished.

The boxes go away.

The tank fills.

The argument remains.

And the bulbs wait for tomorrow.

Not everything left behind can be collected in one night.
