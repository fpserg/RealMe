# Day 107 — Flowers and a Ceasefire

Jr is still ill.

So the Warden stays a little longer in the morning.

With Owling.

Then the commute.

Messages from Oksi.

Angry ones.

The Warden has a theory about cold wars.

With Captain Sparrow, they do not remain cold for long.

They turn active.

Office.

Andrey has read the Platform presentation.

His feedback is positive.

Very positive, by his standards.

The AI-powered juniors have done their job.

Now Alexander and the Warden must do theirs.

Some tweaks.

A speech.

A presentation that can stand in front of management.

Meanwhile, Strategy is travelling.

Media.

Telegram.

Elsewhere.

The report has left the desk.

At lunch, Artem returns from Gelendzhik in conversation.

Biotech conference.

But the stories are about taxi drivers.

The Warden understands.

Such conferences are more for industry people than finance professionals.

Socializing matters.

And at Artem's age, the Warden would have been fascinated by the journey too.

The drivers.

The places.

The small details that make a trip more than its official agenda.

A RealMe way of seeing.

Still, he wonders whether he would have told those stories to his own boss.

Artem did.

The Warden takes it as trust.

Later, a call with the university professor.

The round table.

How they will participate.

Then she offers a new possibility.

Would he consider giving equity-research workshops to students?

The Warden had never imagined himself wanting that.

Now he is considering it.

A new room.

A new role.

But the hours belong to someone already.

Weekday evenings.

Saturdays.

Household.

On the way home, the Warden decides to act.

Flowers.

An attempt to bury the hatchet.

Oksi gives a brief smile.

Then accepts them coldly.

Later she goes shopping with Maksim.

Comes back with a new cashmere coat for him.

Not exactly a peace treaty.

Then the night opens.

Everything she had not said.

The Warden disagrees with much of it.

But he chooses not to answer every point.

Enough escalation.

At the end, he cannot yet tell.

Peace?

Ceasefire?
