# Day 107 — The Hatchet Is Buried

Jr is still ill.

So the Warden stays a little longer in the morning.

With Owling.

Then the commute.

Messages from Oksi.

Angry ones.

The Warden has a theory about cold wars.

With Captain Sparrow, they do not remain cold for long.

They turn active.

Office.

Andrey has read the Platform presentation.

His feedback is positive.

Very positive, by his standards.

The AI-powered juniors have done their job.

Now Alexander and the Warden must do theirs.

Some tweaks.

A speech.

A presentation that can stand in front of management.

Meanwhile, Strategy is travelling.

Media.

Telegram.

Elsewhere.

The report has left the desk.

At lunch, Artem returns from Gelendzhik in conversation.

Biotech conference.

But the stories are about taxi drivers.

The Warden understands.

Such conferences are more for industry people than finance professionals.

Socializing matters.

And at Artem's age, the Warden would have been fascinated by the journey too.

The drivers.

The places.

The small details that make a trip more than its official agenda.

A RealMe way of seeing.

Still, he wonders whether he would have told those stories to his own boss.

Artem did.

The Warden takes it as trust.

Later, a call with the university professor.

The round table.

How they will participate.

Then she offers a new possibility.

Would he consider giving equity-research workshops to students?

The Warden had never imagined himself wanting that.

Now he is considering it.

A new room.

A new role.

But the hours belong to someone already.

Weekday evenings.

Saturdays.

Household.

On the way home, the Warden decides to act.

Flowers.

An attempt to bury the hatchet.

Oksi gives a brief smile.

Then accepts them coldly.

Later she goes shopping with Maksim.

Comes back with a new cashmere coat for him.

Not exactly a peace treaty.

Then the night opens.

Everything she had not said.

The Warden disagrees with much of it.

But he chooses not to answer every point.

Enough escalation.

At the end, he cannot yet tell.

Peace?

Ceasefire?

Another morning arrives.

Owling wakes around seven.

Closer to normal.

Office again.

The Platform speech.

The rehearsal.

Oksi's angry messages continue, but then one changes the day's shape.

Owling is ill.

Come home.

Immediately.

Or at least before the trip to School 57.

The Warden has a problem.

Today is the rehearsal.

Tomorrow he must be home while Oksi visits the dentist.

There is no obvious free slot.

He accelerates.

He and Alexander speak before Andrey.

Thirty minutes allotted.

One hour used.

The Warden's part is largely all right.

Alexander's smaller section takes too much time.

They make corrections.

Then the Warden runs home.

No replies from Oksi.

He does not know which version of the afternoon is waiting.

Will he take Owling?

Has Oksi taken him along?

Has Jr missed School 57?

Home.

Nobody there.

The answer is already on the road.

Maksim comes back from school.

The Warden gets him food.

Then Oksi returns.

The Warden runs outside.

Helps carry Owling in.

He feels the fever.

Poor baby.

Then another journey.

Pick up Jr from School 57.

The bright boy has managed his homework.

And learned his verses by heart.

Home again.

Oksi has Spanish.

The Warden stays with Owling.

The child is no longer the little engine of the previous days.

Weak.

Silent.

Falling asleep in his father's arms.

Oksi finishes her lesson.

Medicine.

The fever does not go away.

Then she sees red dots on his feet.

Call an ambulance.

The Warden thinks it may be too much.

He calls anyway.

The ambulance arrives.

By then Owling seems much better.

The doctor examines him.

Leaves.

No diagnosis enters the Chronicle.

The parents sit beside their child.

He is energetic again.

And the Warden notices something else.

Warmth.

Between him and Oksi.

The warmth that had seemed absent for the whole week.

No speech announces it.

No argument settles every grievance.

They are simply there together.

Beside Owling.

When the child finally sleeps, they have a normal evening as a couple.

The Warden knows the illness is not over merely because Owling improved.

But the cold war, in his account, is.

Yesterday the flowers had opened the possibility.

Today the Warden gives it a name.

The hatchet is buried.
