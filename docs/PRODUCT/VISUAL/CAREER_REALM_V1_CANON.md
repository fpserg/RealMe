## Realm geography

Canonical progression:

**STRAIT / COAST → INHABITED INTERIOR → RISING INLAND HIGHLANDS**

This is geography, not a semantic pipeline.

The Lighthouse is deliberately farther away in perspective. Its apparent size may be small while its meaning survives through silhouette, contrast and coastal context.

Confluence is a full Domain-sized network rather than connective filler.

The Observatory is a modest working complex embedded within Strategy terrain rather than a palace, temple or endpoint.

Routes may connect Domains, bypass Confluence, leave laterally, continue south or extend inland beyond Observatory.

## Equal-weight constitution

- TMT carries weight through **extent**.
- Confluence carries weight through **connectivity**.
- Strategy carries weight through **elevation / breadth**.

**Equal weight ≠ equal landmark size.**  
**Equal weight ≠ equal pixels.**

## Semantic architecture

**The map is an index, not a filing cabinet.**

A work object may surface through multiple relevant semantic coordinates without duplication. A primary semantic home is optional and should only be assigned where one naturally exists.

**Containment ≠ relevance.**  
**Map centrality ≠ default routing.**

Semantic zoom combines visual approach with information disclosure:

**World → Career → Domain → Locus → relevant activity/history/state.**

## Cross-scale behavior

At mobile/Realm reduction:

- Lighthouse survives through contrast and silhouette;
- Confluence survives through topology and density;
- Observatory survives through dome/highland silhouette.

At World scale Career may read primarily as one macro-geographic character rather than requiring every Domain to remain individually legible.

**Perspective may reduce scale without reducing meaning.**  
**Distance reduces detail before meaning.**

## Relationship to Household

World orientation currently established:

- Household — NW;
- Career — NE;
- Third — South.

Career's NW water is the strait facing Household. The Career Realm master may imply a distant opposite shore but does not bake recognizable Household landmarks into Career geography.

Household and Career both contain relational centres, but this is a discovered rhyme rather than a universal template:

- Family — rooted relational centre;
- Confluence — flowing relational centre.

Do not force this structure onto Third Realm.
