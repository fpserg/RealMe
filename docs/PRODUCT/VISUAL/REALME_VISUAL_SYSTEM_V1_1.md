# REALME VISUAL SYSTEM v1.1

Status: APPROVED  
Canonical orientation: 9:16 PORTRAIT / MOBILE FIRST  
Origin: Capital B″ discovery → Stronghold falsification → Family replication  
Overarching visual language: PLAYABLE PAINTERLY

---

# I. PURPOSE

RealMe must simultaneously function as a rich inhabitable fantasy World, a mobile interface, a navigable geographic system, and a background for semantic UI.

Earlier artwork established strong World identity but often rendered too much geography at comparable levels of detail. Capital B″ accidentally demonstrated a better mechanism: meaningful geography remained materially readable while less relevant geography progressively dissolved into softer painterly masses. Controlled tests in Stronghold and Family showed that the mechanism survives different environments and weather.

The discovery is therefore canonicalized as the **Painterly Focus Gradient**.

---

# II. OVERARCHING STYLE

The canonical RealMe visual language remains:

# PLAYABLE PAINTERLY

RealMe is not globally defined as watercolor, oil painting, mobile-game concept art, photorealism, cinematic CGI, or storybook illustration.

The World must remain inhabitable, spatially coherent, geographically readable, materially convincing where interaction occurs, painterly rather than rendered, and visually quiet enough to support UI.

---

# III. PAINTERLY FOCUS GRADIENT

Rendering resolution decreases as geography becomes less relevant to immediate perception and interaction.

The gradient progressively reduces:

- object-level detail;
- edge definition;
- material specificity;
- local contrast;
- colour separation;
- texture resolution.

This is **not photographic depth-of-field**. Do not simulate lens blur, bokeh, camera focus or Gaussian blur. The World remains spatially present; it is painted with progressively less information.

# EDGES DISSOLVE BEFORE IDENTITY DOES.

A distant mountain may lose rocks, snow texture, vegetation and hard edges while remaining unmistakably a mountain. A distant city may lose windows, roofs and individual buildings while remaining unmistakably a city. Recognition survives after detail retreats.

---

# IV. SEMANTIC DEPTH

The Painterly Focus Gradient is not determined solely by physical camera distance.

Rendering resolution is governed jointly by:

- semantic importance;
- navigational importance;
- physical depth.

A canonical landmark may remain comparatively defined even when physically farther away. A meaningless foreground object may remain restrained even when close.

The hierarchy follows the meaning of geography, not a mechanical sharp-bottom / blurry-top formula.

---

# V. FOUR SEMANTIC RENDERING ZONES

These are conceptual zones, not visible borders or fixed horizontal bands.

## 1. SEMANTIC NUCLEUS — Material Painterly

Contains the Domain's primary canonical identity.

Use strongest material definition, recognizable architecture, controlled local detail, clear silhouette, readable entrances/routes where relevant, and selective natural contrast.

The nucleus must feel physically present without becoming a glowing game landmark, icon, monument or photorealistic insert.

## 2. INTERACTIVE FIELD — Defined Painterly

Contains geography capable of supporting navigation and semantic anchors: paths, courtyards, gardens, bridges, roads, nearby buildings, terraces, waterways, urban intersections and cultivated areas.

Retain readable forms and material identity with moderate detail and sufficient visual breathing room for UI.

## 3. SUPPORTING WORLD — Loose Painterly

Establishes Domain context: surrounding forest, outer city, fields, villages, valleys, secondary waterways, distant neighbourhoods and coastal terrain.

Consolidate objects into geographic masses: canopy rather than individual trees, city blocks rather than individual buildings, settlement masses rather than individual houses, cultivated patches rather than individual crops.

Use looser brushwork, fewer hard edges, compressed local contrast, reduced colour separation and lower information density.

## 4. WORLD CONTINUATION — Watercolor-Like Dissolution

Communicates scale, atmosphere, distance and continuity beyond immediate attention.

Use broad washes, loose pigment-like masses, softened silhouettes, atmospheric colour integration, minimal object-level articulation, low local contrast and restrained texture.

Individual objects largely disappear. Geographic identity remains.

The objective is not “look at this watercolor.” The objective is “the World continues beyond what currently requires attention.”

---

# VI. MATERIAL FOREGROUND RULE

The discovery must not become a global watercolor filter.

Near and semantically important geography remains materially convincing. Stone reads as stone, wood as wood, water as water, architecture retains construction and mass, vegetation retains enough physical character to feel inhabitable.

Canonical progression:

# MATERIAL FOREGROUND
→
# LOOSE PAINTERLY SUPPORTING GEOGRAPHY
→
# WATERCOLOR-LIKE WORLD CONTINUATION

---

# VII. ATTENTION GRADIENT

The Painterly Focus Gradient controls attention demand as well as detail.

As semantic relevance decreases:

DETAIL ↓  
EDGE DEFINITION ↓  
LOCAL CONTRAST ↓  
COLOUR SEPARATION ↓  
MATERIAL SPECIFICITY ↓

Large background structures may remain visible but should normally integrate increasingly into surrounding atmosphere rather than becoming competing cognitive anchors.

---

# VIII. LIGHT AS STRUCTURE

Light may reinforce semantic hierarchy through selective sunlight, inhabited windows, reflections, locally illuminated architecture, natural shadow and atmospheric gradients.

# LIGHT MAY GUIDE ATTENTION.
# LIGHT MUST NOT MARK INTERACTIVITY.

Avoid glowing outlines, magical halos, game-like hotspots and uniform golden lighting around important objects.

---

# IX. ATMOSPHERIC IDEALIZATION

RealMe is not required to reproduce the least attractive version of ordinary reality.

A normal Tuesday may contain beautiful evening light, rain reflections, warm windows, dramatic clouds, snow or atmospheric haze.

# ATMOSPHERIC IDEALIZATION IS ALLOWED.
# ONTOLOGICAL INFLATION IS NOT.

Capital may have beautiful evening light, but its ordinary apartment Home must not become a palace. Family may have beautiful countryside, but must not become an enchanted royal village. Stronghold may have spectacular mountains, but its Home must remain its canonical Home. TMT may have dramatic sea and sky, but its Lighthouse remains a working geographic landmark rather than a magical tower.

---

# X. WEATHER INDEPENDENCE

The Painterly Focus Gradient must work independently of rain, fog, snow, sunset, darkness or atmospheric haze. Weather may strengthen the effect but must not create it.

---

# XI. ARTWORK / UI RELATIONSHIP

Artwork represents geography, architecture, atmosphere and physical place.

UI represents semantic anchors, names, commitments, campaigns, navigation, state and progress.

Artwork must not anticipate every UI element through empty plaques, label-shaped clearings, icon pedestals, glowing hotspots or artificial anchor platforms.

# THE UI INHABITS THE WORLD.
# THE WORLD DOES NOT ADVERTISE THE UI.

Semantic anchors do not require pictorial equivalents. Bathroom does not require a bathtub. Bedroom does not require a bed. YNDX does not require a corporate logo. Anchor placement identifies a navigational coordinate rather than literally illustrating its semantic meaning.

---

# XII. SCALE AND ZOOM CONTINUITY

The Painterly Focus Gradient applies at Domain, Realm and World scale.

At Domain scale, the semantic nucleus is the Domain landmark and local navigable geography. At Realm scale, major Domain landmarks become semantic nuclei and routes/geography connect them. At World scale, Realm-scale geographic identities become semantic nuclei.

Approaching a place should feel as though previously loose geography gradually resolves.

# ZOOM REVEALS INFORMATION.
# ZOOM DOES NOT REINVENT IDENTITY.

---

# XIII. ANTI-FAILURE RULES

Reject an image for:

- **Global Watercolor Failure** — everything is equally loose; primary geography lacks material presence.
- **Photographic Blur Failure** — distance resembles optical depth-of-field.
- **Uniform Detail Failure** — foreground and horizon invite comparable inspection.
- **Foreground Noise Failure** — micro-detail competes with semantic anchors.
- **Landmark Inflation Failure** — a landmark becomes unnaturally monumental merely to remain readable.
- **Background Landmark Failure** — secondary distant architecture becomes a competing cognitive anchor.
- **Storybook Failure** — excessive cuteness, whimsy or illustration weakens geographic weight.
- **Generic AI Fantasy Failure** — visible stylistic tricks dominate Domain identity.
- **Semantic Leakage Failure** — artwork starts illustrating UI categories rather than geography.

---

# XIV. VALIDATION TESTS

Every new Domain, Realm or World image should pass:

1. **Identity Test** — canonical geographic identity survives.
2. **Material Test** — primary geography remains physically inhabitable.
3. **Attention Test** — the eye prioritizes meaningful geography.
4. **Dissolution Test** — information progressively retreats outside meaningful geography.
5. **Geography Test** — distant terrain remains understandable after detail retreats.
6. **UI Test** — semantic anchors can occupy the image without fighting the artwork.
7. **Blur-vs-Paint Test** — simplification feels painted rather than optically blurred.
8. **Storybook Test** — sufficient physical and geographic seriousness remains.
9. **Cross-Scale Test** — the place could plausibly resolve from its Realm-scale representation.

---

# XV. VALIDATION HISTORY

## Capital B″ — PASS

Accidental discovery. Material foreground, inhabited warmth, readable Home, progressively softened city, atmospheric skyline and reduced background attention demand.

## Stronghold 40B — PARTIAL PASS

Controlled falsification under clear weather. Background quieting, mountain simplification and attention hierarchy succeeded. Watercolor/painterly treatment spread too strongly into primary geography and reduced material/navigation definition.

Lesson: do not watercolorize the whole frame.

## Family 40D — PASS

Refined replication in pastoral geography. Ancient tree remained dominant; settlement, water and paths remained navigable; countryside consolidated and horizon dissolved without disappearing.

Cross-Domain conclusion: the mechanism survives urban, alpine/fortified and pastoral environments, and survives rain and clear weather.

# XVII. GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

RealMe should never force the Warden to choose between a beautiful World and a usable interface.

The World may remain rich, but richness must have hierarchy. Geography immediately relevant to the Warden resolves. The greater World remains present without demanding equal attention. As the Warden approaches it, it may resolve in turn.

# EDGES DISSOLVE BEFORE IDENTITY DOES.

# THE WORLD CONTINUES BEYOND WHAT CURRENTLY REQUIRES ATTENTION.
