# II. WORLD GEOGRAPHY CONSTITUTION

World topology:

- Household — NW continental Realm.
- Career — NE continental Realm.
- Third — southern continental Realm.
- Centre — irregular inter-Realm sea / straits; owned by no Realm.

# SEA SEPARATES REALMS.
# LAND CONNECTS DOMAINS.
# THE CENTRE BELONGS TO NO REALM.

Realms are continent-scale landmasses, not necessarily islands. Realm territory may continue beyond the visible viewport.

The World viewport crops geography rather than enclosing it.

Third explicitly continues through the southern/bottom viewport; no canonical southern coastline is visible. Household and Career may likewise continue beyond their appropriate outer edges.

Small incidental islands may exist in inter-Realm seas without constituting Realms or Domains.

No central hub, palace, fourth Realm or mandatory landmark occupies the World centre.

# IV. SEMANTIC ZOOM CONSTITUTION

WORLD → REALM → DOMAIN → LOCUS → relevant activity/history/state.

# SEMANTIC ZOOM = VISUAL ZOOM + INFORMATION DISCLOSURE.

A World-scale Realm is not a miniature Realm screen. It is the Realm's geographic signature.

When detail disappears, geography may inherit meaning.

Different Domains survive zoom by different mechanisms:

- landmark / silhouette;
- terrain / material;
- network / density;
- geographic position;
- colour / contrast;
- activity/process at closer scales.

Do not use monumental scale merely to preserve recognition.

# USE SILHOUETTE, ISOLATION, CONTRAST, COLOUR AND MODEST SEMANTIC EXAGGERATION — NOT MONUMENTALITY.

At World scale:

- Stronghold survives through snowy highland + dark-red home.
- Family survives through tree + rooted territorial pocket.
- Capital survives through separate urban field.
- Lighthouse survives through isolated coastal silhouette.
- Confluence survives through convergence + localized blue + terrain nucleus.
- Observatory survives through dome/highland silhouette.
- Gifted survives through compact crystalline environment.
- Tower survives through pale vertical silhouette.
- RealMe survives primarily through promontory position + purple experimental glow + golden glint.

# V. INTERACTION / CLICKABLE GEOGRAPHY

The World is an interaction surface, not only artwork.

# CLICKABLE DOMAIN CENTRES REQUIRE BREATHING ROOM.

Each Domain must retain a distinct spatial centroid sufficient for reliable interaction even where surrounding landscapes overlap softly.

Middle Domains must not be pushed toward one pole merely for composition:

- Family should remain broadly between Stronghold and Capital.
- Confluence should remain broadly between Lighthouse and Observatory.

Territorial distinction may be suggested by terrain, water, vegetation, density and colour.

# DISTINCT TERRITORIES, NOT ENCLOSURES.

No hard Domain borders, glowing outlines, complete rings, map-node circles or baked-in UI.

Artwork creates place. UI locates semantics within place.
