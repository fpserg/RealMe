## Reflection

Day 033 was defined by decisions rather than accomplishments.

The future of the Stronghold became clearer through comparison instead of construction, as two different landscape philosophies produced a balanced plan. An accidental purchase during the morning expedition unexpectedly became the day's most effective conflict-resolution tool, reminding the Warden that small preparations often matter more than grand intentions.
