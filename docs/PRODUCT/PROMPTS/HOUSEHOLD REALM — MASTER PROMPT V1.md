# HOUSEHOLD REALM — MASTER PROMPT V1
## RealMe Atlas — Three Domains, One Geography

Create a wide **16:9 hand-painted fantasy atlas illustration** of the **Household Realm**.

This is the first Realm-level synthesis of the established RealMe Atlas visual language.

The Household Realm contains three established Domains:

- **Stronghold**
- **Family**
- **Capital**

These Domains already possess distinct canonical visual identities.

The purpose of this image is NOT to place three miniature Domain illustrations beside one another.

The purpose is to reveal the **single continuous geography that contains all three**.

The Realm map must therefore answer:

> What does Household look like when Stronghold, Family and Capital are understood as different places within the same lived territory?

The governing principle is:

# SAME ARTIST. SAME ATLAS. GREATER DISTANCE.

Moving from Domain → Realm means:

# REMOVE DETAIL.
# PRESERVE IDENTITY.
# REVEAL GEOGRAPHY.

Do not change artistic style when zooming outward.

---

# 1. REALME ATLAS SCALE PRINCIPLE

RealMe uses one continuous visual language across:

WORLD MAP → REALM MAP → DOMAIN VIEW

All levels should look as though the same imaginary atlas artist painted the same World at different viewing distances.

The difference is information density.

## DOMAIN VIEW

Shows:

- recognizable architecture;
- gardens;
- courtyards;
- local paths;
- small signs of life;
- moderate environmental detail.

## REALM MAP

Shows:

- Domain landmarks as simplified visual glyphs;
- major terrain;
- mountains;
- rivers;
- forests;
- cultivated territory;
- cities;
- major routes;
- geographic relationships between Domains.

## WORLD MAP

Shows:

- Realm-scale landmasses;
- major geography;
- Realm identity;
- highly simplified Domain landmarks.

This image is a:

# REALM MAP.

Therefore Domain-level details must disappear.

The viewer should feel:

> I have risen higher above the exact same painted World.

Not:

> I have opened a different kind of map.

# 2. CORE HOUSEHOLD IDEA

Household represents:

# BELONGING THROUGH PLACE.

It contains three different forms of home and family geography:

## STRONGHOLD

A home being built and stewarded at the edge of wilderness.

## FAMILY

An accumulated landscape of belonging, generations and connection.

## CAPITAL

The geography of ordinary weekday family life inside Moscow.

These are not competing interpretations of Household.

They are three different spatial expressions of it.

The Realm map should make them feel naturally connected.

# 4. DOMAIN EQUALITY

Stronghold, Family and Capital are equal Domains within Household.

None is the capital of the Realm conceptually.

None is the Realm's main Domain.

None should dominate because its natural subject is visually dramatic.

This is especially important for Stronghold.

Snowy mountains and fortifications naturally attract attention.

Control their scale so that Stronghold remains comparable in conceptual weight to Family and Capital.

Likewise, do not make Capital enormous merely because Moscow is geographically large.

Domain importance is represented through:

- strong silhouette;
- compositional space;
- geographic identity;
- clear surrounding territory;

not through physical monumentality.

The three Domain glyphs should have **broadly comparable visual weight**.

terrain becomes progressively greener and more inhabited.

# 13. THE RIVER SYSTEM

Use water as one of the Realm's major geographic organizers.

A river or connected water system should emerge from the northern mountain / foothill territory and travel through Household.

It may:

- descend from the Stronghold region;
- pass through forests;
- approach or form Family's water;
- continue toward gentler territory;
- eventually bend toward or disappear into the wider landscape near Capital.

Do NOT force the river to literally connect every landmark if geography becomes artificial.

It should feel natural.

The river is a geographic system, not a symbolic line connecting Domains.

At Realm scale it should be readable as one of the major compositional gestures.
