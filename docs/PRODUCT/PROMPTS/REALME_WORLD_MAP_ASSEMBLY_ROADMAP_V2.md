# Purpose

This document defines the production roadmap for assembling the RealMe visual World from the inside out.

It incorporates the visual discoveries made during the canonical development of:

- Stronghold;
- Family;
- Capital;
- Household Realm assembly.

The objective is to create a coherent spatial system across:

1. World View
2. Realm View
3. Domain View

The World must not be generated as one illustration that simultaneously invents:

- geography;
- Realm identity;
- Domain identity;
- architecture;
- mobile composition.

Instead:

1. establish global visual and spatial grammar;
2. discover each Domain as a real place;
3. freeze approved Domain identities;
4. derive Realm geography from those established Domains;
5. derive the final World Map from established Realms.

The final result should feel like one continuous fantasy World through which the Warden can zoom.

---

# I. CORE PRINCIPLE

The hierarchy is:

WORLD DEFINES LAWS
        ↓
DOMAINS ESTABLISH IDENTITY
        ↓
REALMS ESTABLISH GEOGRAPHIC RELATIONSHIPS
        ↓
WORLD MAP ASSEMBLES COMPLETE GEOGRAPHY

The World Map must not invent Domains.

Realm Maps must not reinvent Domains.

Once a Domain becomes canonical, later map generations inherit it.

A Domain seen from the World should be recognizably the same place that appears when the Warden enters it.

---

# II. CANONICAL OVERRIDE PRINCIPLE

This rule is absolute.

When an approved canonical Domain image conflicts with:

- an earlier roadmap;
- an earlier prompt;
- an earlier conceptual description;
- conversational memory;
- generic fantasy conventions;

the approved canonical Domain takes precedence.

The hierarchy of visual authority is:

1. latest Warden-approved canonical Domain image;
2. latest Warden-approved Realm image;
3. latest approved Domain prompt;
4. current roadmap;
5. older prompts and concepts.

Older concepts are retired once explicitly superseded.

They must not silently reappear in later generations.

---

# III. DOMAIN FREEZE PRINCIPLE

Once the Warden freezes a Domain, its fundamental identity becomes fixed.

A frozen Domain establishes:

- dominant landmark;
- architectural language;
- geographic character;
- major colour/material cues;
- compositional identity;
- map-scale glyph.

Later Realm and World images may:

- simplify it;
- reduce detail;
- change apparent scale;
- integrate surrounding geography.

They may NOT:

- replace its landmark;
- change its architectural type;
- change its fundamental geography;
- resurrect discarded concepts;
- invent a different symbolic representation.

Simplification is allowed.

Redesign is not.

---

# IV. UNIVERSAL IMAGE ARCHITECTURE

Every map-related RealMe image uses:

- 16:9 landscape;
- preferred resolution 2560 × 1440;
- minimum resolution 1920 × 1080;
- PNG, WebP or high-quality JPG.

Do not create portrait-native Domain or Realm images.

The same World must work at:

WORLD VIEW → REALM VIEW → DOMAIN VIEW

Scale changes primarily through detail.

Style does not.

The viewer should feel:

> I moved closer to the same map.

Not:

> I entered a different illustration.

---

# V. MOBILE-FIRST SAFE CORRIDOR

RealMe is primarily viewed on a vertically oriented iPhone.

Every 16:9 image must therefore be composed around a central portrait-safe corridor.

Essential geography must survive aggressive cropping from both horizontal sides.

The safe corridor must contain:

- all essential Realm geography;
- all major Domain landmarks;
- important routes between Domains;
- enough surrounding terrain to preserve relationships.

Outer horizontal areas are expendable.

They may contain:

- secondary landscape;
- distant city;
- mountains;
- forests;
- fields;
- coastline;
- atmospheric continuation.

Cropping should remove atmosphere rather than ontology.

---

# VI. SHARED VISUAL LANGUAGE

All RealMe geography belongs to one painted atlas.

Use:

- high three-quarter aerial perspective;
- mild isometric influence;
- hand-painted fantasy atlas illustration;
- visible painterly texture;
- broad geographic masses;
- selective architectural definition;
- restrained detail;
- warm directional sunlight;
- cooler shaded areas;
- atmospheric depth;
- softened horizons;
- natural haze;
- readable silhouettes.

The established Household canonical images provide the first major visual benchmark.

The governing principle is:

# SAME ARTIST.
# SAME ATLAS.
# SAME WORLD.

Avoid:

- photorealism;
- glossy CGI;
- Unreal Engine aesthetics;
- hyper-detailed AI concept art;
- vector graphics;
- infographic styling;
- uniform sharpness;
- excessive micro-detail.

---

# VII. GEOGRAPHY OVER OBJECTS

A major lesson from Stronghold, Family and Capital development is that RealMe environments must be constructed primarily as geography.

At Domain scale:

individual structures may be recognizable.

At Realm scale:

structures become landmark masses embedded within geography.

At World scale:

Domains become simplified glyphs.

Therefore:

FORESTS become canopy masses.

FIELDS become cultivated geographic patches.

CITY BLOCKS become urban masses.

MOUNTAINS become major silhouettes.

SETTLEMENTS become clustered forms.

Do not fill empty space with individually resolved objects merely because generation allows it.

---

# VIII. DETAIL GRADIENT

Every RealMe image must have an intentional hierarchy of information.

Near primary landmarks:

moderate detail.

Middle geography:

simplified detail.

Distant geography:

broad painted masses.

Detail must decrease with distance.

This principle is especially important for Capital:

# CITY EXTENT INCREASES WITH DISTANCE.
# CITY DETAIL DECREASES WITH DISTANCE.

---

# IX. SEMANTIC SEPARATION

Artwork represents:

- places;
- architecture;
- geography;
- terrain;
- atmosphere.

RealMe UI represents:

- Realm names;
- Domain names;
- Today;
- Horizons;
- Commitments;
- Campaigns;
- status;
- progress;
- navigation;
- selections.

Therefore artwork contains:

NO TEXT.

No Realm names.

No Domain names.

No fake fantasy names.

No map titles.

No legends.

No UI.

No map markers.

No written banners.

Artwork is geography.

RealMe owns semantics.

## Canonical Architecture

The central landmark is a pale cream / off-white urban apartment building derived from the real Household apartment building.

It should read clearly as:

# APARTMENT HOUSE

- ministry;
- luxury hotel;
- aristocratic residence;
- university;
- government building.

Not:

> This is the most important building in the city.

# XVII. DOMAIN EMPHASIS AT REALM SCALE

A major lesson from Household Realm V1–V4 is that Domains can dissolve too far into landscape.

This is undesirable.

Realm maps are geography, but their Domains must remain readable.

Use subtle emphasis rather than artificial icons.

Domain emphasis may come from:

- silhouette;
- local contrast;
- slightly stronger architectural definition;
- controlled clearing around landmark;
- roads and rivers directing the eye;
- colour grouping;
- local sunlight;
- recognizable terrain association.

Do NOT use:

- glowing outlines;
- UI markers;
- labels;
- giant structures;
- unnatural scale inflation.

The target is:

# LANDMARKS EMBEDDED IN GEOGRAPHY,
# NOT LANDMARKS LOST IN GEOGRAPHY.

---

# XVIII. HOUSEHOLD EQUAL-WEIGHT RULE

Stronghold, Family and Capital are equal Domains.

They do not need identical physical size.

They need comparable conceptual readability.

Stronghold should not dominate merely because mountains are dramatic.

Family should not disappear because its architecture is modest.

Capital should not disappear because the urban environment merges into the horizon.

At first inspection, the viewer should discover three distinct geographic anchors.
