# CAPITAL DOMAIN — MASTER PROMPT V3
## RealMe Atlas — Home, Courtyard, Arteries

Create a wide **16:9 hand-painted fantasy atlas illustration** of the **Capital Domain**.

Capital is one of the three currently known Domains of the **Household Realm**, alongside **Stronghold** and **Family**.

Use the established canonical RealMe visual language of **Stronghold V5** and the lessons learned through the development of **Family**.

Use the attached photograph of the real apartment building as architectural DNA for the central Household home.

Capital is **Moscow**, but translated into the RealMe Atlas visual language.

Do NOT create a fantasy capital city.

Do NOT create a seat of power.

Do NOT depict Career.

Capital is where ordinary weekday Household life happens:

- the family wakes up in the apartment;
- children leave for school and kindergarten;
- adults leave for work and daily responsibilities;
- household work and errands happen;
- people move through streets and courtyards;
- everyone returns home;
- the city continues around them.

Capital represents:

**DAILY LIFE + MOVEMENT + RETURN**

Its permanent visual identity is:

# PALE HOME
+
# GREEN COURTYARD
+
# CITY ARTERIES

The surrounding Moscow cityscape is **supporting geography**, not part of the primary glyph.

The governing art-direction principle is:

# SAME ARTIST. SAME ATLAS. SAME WORLD.

---

# 1. REALME ATLAS SCALE PRINCIPLE

RealMe uses one continuous visual language across:

WORLD MAP → REALM MAP → DOMAIN VIEW

Scale changes through **detail, not style**.

At Domain level, more evidence of lived reality becomes visible.

But the image must remain fundamentally a **painted atlas landscape**.

The viewer should feel:

> I moved closer to the same map.

Not:

> I entered a different kind of illustration.

Do not create:

- cinematic city concept art;
- architectural visualization;
- photorealistic Moscow;
- dense city simulation;
- board-game city tile;
- videogame environment.

---

# 2. CAPITAL'S CORE GLYPH — CRITICAL

Capital's permanent three-form glyph is:

# PALE URBAN HOME
+
# GREEN RESIDENTIAL COURTYARD
+
# TWO OR THREE CITY ARTERIES

Everything else is supporting geography.

At Domain scale, these forms receive moderate detail.

At Realm scale, they simplify.

At World scale, Capital may become little more than:

**one pale building beside a green patch, embedded within several intersecting urban lines.**

Therefore the major forms must already be extremely strong.

If the image is heavily blurred, the viewer should still perceive:

1. one pale central building;
2. one green courtyard mass;
3. several strong street gestures;
4. surrounding warm-grey city.

The central Household home is based on the attached real photograph.

Preserve its recognizable architectural DNA:

- pale cream / off-white façade;
- approximately five to six storeys;
- solid masonry construction;
- restrained early-to-mid 20th-century Moscow residential proportions;
- simple rectangular windows;
- restrained horizontal façade articulation;
- modest projecting elements;
- relatively simple roofline;
- ordinary urban residential character.

# APARTMENT BUILDING

Do NOT transform it into:

- palace;
- mansion;
- luxury hotel;
- ministry;
- university;
- guild headquarters;
- aristocratic residence;
- monumental civic building.

Its importance comes entirely from:

# THIS IS HOME.

# 5. HOME MUST REMAIN ORDINARY

The Household building should have visual character but no grandeur.

Avoid:

- monumental entrances;
- elaborate cornices;
- excessive façade ornament;
- complicated roofs;
- grand symmetry;
- ceremonial stairs;
- prestigious landscaping.

It should feel like a recognizable, slightly aged, functioning Moscow apartment building.

Some windows may glow.

Some façade elements may differ slightly through accumulated renovations.

Mature trees may partially obscure portions of the building.

The building belongs to the city.

The city was not built around it.

Create a recognizable green residential courtyard immediately beside and around the Household home.

# 7. CITY ARTERIES — THIRD PRIMARY FORM

Use approximately **2–3 principal urban routes** around the central neighbourhood.

These should be clearly readable as large compositional gestures.

They may include:

- a broad street;
- a tree-lined avenue;
- a tram route;
- a secondary intersecting street.

The routes should NOT radiate perfectly from Home.

The Household exists inside an already functioning urban system.

Use believable urban geometry:

- intersections;
- one strong diagonal;
- parallel or perpendicular routes;
- slightly irregular older street patterns.

The streets carry life outward from Home and back again.

Conceptually:

MORNING:

HOME → COURTYARD → STREETS → CITY

EVENING:

CITY → STREETS → COURTYARD → HOME

Do not illustrate this literally.

Let the geography imply it.

---

# 8. PUBLIC TRANSPORT

Include one restrained but recognizable sign of Moscow public transport.

A **tram** is preferred.

Place it on one of the principal routes.

It should remain small enough that it does not become a landmark.

Its purpose is to communicate:

**weekday movement through the city.**

A second tram may appear elsewhere if composition naturally supports it, but avoid creating a transport scene.

No railway terminal.

No transport hub.

No giant station.

Public transport is simply part of ordinary urban circulation.

---

# 9. MOSCOW — GEOGRAPHIC IDENTITY

Capital is the RealMe interpretation of **Moscow**.

Allow the wider city to contain subtle geographic and architectural cues that make this identity believable.

Possible distant cues include:

- broad low-rise and mid-rise urban fabric;
- older masonry blocks;
- tree-lined streets;
- occasional taller modern structures;
- distant river geometry;
- bridges;
- restrained church domes or towers;
- varied historical layers of architecture.

A distant Orthodox church or group of domes is appropriate.

It should function as **geographic identification**, not religious symbolism.

It says:

> This city is Moscow.

It does NOT say:

> This Domain is about religion.

Keep such landmarks distant and subordinate to Home.

Do not create a postcard skyline.

Do not concentrate famous monuments.

Do not turn Capital into tourist Moscow.

This is **lived Moscow**, not represented Moscow.

---

# 10. URBAN BLOCKS AS GEOGRAPHY — CRITICAL

Do NOT treat the city as a collection of individual buildings.

Treat urban blocks as **geographic masses**.

Apply the same visual logic that Stronghold uses for forests and Family uses for fields.

Near Home:

individual buildings may remain recognizable.

Middle distance:

several buildings merge into:

**ROOF MASS + FAÇADE PLANES + TREE CANOPY.**

Far distance:

entire blocks become:

**WARM-GREY / OCHRE URBAN SHAPES.**

Toward the horizon:

the city becomes:

**ATMOSPHERIC URBAN TEXTURE.**

The city may be enormous.

The amount of individually resolved architecture must not be enormous.

**inhabited land → wilderness.**

**settlement → cultivated land → countryside.**
