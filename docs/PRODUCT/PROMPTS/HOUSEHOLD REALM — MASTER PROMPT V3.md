# HOUSEHOLD REALM — MASTER PROMPT V3
## RealMe Atlas — Continuous Geography, Legible Domains

Create a wide **16:9 hand-painted fantasy atlas illustration** of the **Household Realm**.

Use **Household Realm V2.2 as the direct compositional foundation**.

Do NOT redesign the Realm from scratch.

Preserve the strongest qualities of V2.2:

- one continuous believable territory rather than three separate Domain illustrations;
- Stronghold in the northern mountain geography;
- Family occupying the inhabited middle territory;
- Capital emerging toward the east;
- continuous rivers, roads, forests, fields and settlements connecting the Realm;
- strong atmospheric depth;
- excellent mobile composition;
- painterly RealMe Atlas character.

V3 is a refinement.

Its primary objectives are:

1. move Stronghold from northwest toward **north / north-northwest**;
2. make Stronghold substantially more readable at Realm scale;
3. subtly emphasize ALL THREE Domains without using borders, labels, markers or artificial effects;
4. restore the canonical identity of Stronghold;
5. preserve Family as the geographic heart of Household;
6. preserve Capital as the eastern urban Domain;
7. reduce unnecessary non-Domain micro-detail;
8. strengthen the sense that all three Domains emerge naturally from ONE continuous geography.

The governing principle is:

# ONE REALM.
# THREE LEGIBLE DOMAINS.
# CONTINUOUS GEOGRAPHY.

And:

# EMPHASIZE THROUGH COMPOSITION, NOT UI.

# 1. REALME ATLAS SCALE PRINCIPLE

RealMe uses one continuous visual language across:

WORLD → REALM → DOMAIN

The style does NOT change between scales.

Only information density changes.

At Realm scale:

- geography dominates;
- Domains appear as strong visual landmarks;
- roads and rivers explain relationships;
- settlements become simplified marks;
- forests become masses;
- cities become urban textures;
- Domain-level micro-detail disappears.

The viewer should feel:

> I moved farther away from Stronghold, Family and Capital and can now see how they belong together.

Do NOT create three miniature Domain paintings pasted onto one landscape.

Do NOT create a board-game map.

Do NOT create an RPG overworld with icons.

This is one inhabited territory viewed from higher above.

# 5. CANONICAL STRONGHOLD IDENTITY — ABSOLUTE

The Realm-level Stronghold must derive directly from canonical **Stronghold V5**.

Do NOT allow it to become a generic fantasy castle.

Its permanent glyph is:

# SNOWY MOUNTAIN
+
# DARK-RED HOME
+
# COMPACT GREY FORTIFICATIONS

The central dark-red residence is essential.

Even at Realm scale, preserve:

- one substantial dark-red / reddish-brown residential mass;
- broad horizontal proportions;
- dark roof;
- suggestion of large windows / balconies;
- compact grey stone walls around it;
- several restrained round defensive towers;
- principal gate or approach;
- mountain foothill position.

The red residence must remain visually distinct from the grey fortifications.

The viewer should perceive:

**HOME INSIDE FORTIFICATIONS**

not:

**MEDIEVAL CASTLE.**

Avoid:

- giant keep;
- generic stone citadel;
- enormous towers;
- palace;
- monastery;
- mountain château.

Stronghold must remain recognizably OUR Stronghold after simplification.

# 6. STRONGHOLD + MOUNTAIN RELATIONSHIP

Stronghold sits directly beneath the northern snowy mountains.

The mountains should form a strong geographic backdrop and explain why Stronghold exists there.

However:

Do NOT allow the mountains to visually swallow Stronghold.

Create sufficient tonal and spatial separation around the landmark.

Use:

- darker red residence against cooler mountain foothills;
- grey fortifications against darker forest;
- slightly calmer immediate terrain;
- selective sunlight on the Stronghold.

The mountain silhouette may be large.

The Stronghold silhouette must remain independently readable.
