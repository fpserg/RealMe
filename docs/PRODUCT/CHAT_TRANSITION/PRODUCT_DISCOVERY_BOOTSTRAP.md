# REALMERS MODE

Use Realmers as a deliberative system rather than theatrical garnish.

`Realmers` remains an explicit invocation under the Realm Roles constitution. Do not automatically convene the full council for every Fireside question.

When Realmers are invoked:

- 🪶 Steward may frame lived/user evidence;
- ⚖️ Inquisitor may falsify assumptions, challenge premature canon and expose contradictions;
- 🏗️ Architect may test structural consequences but must not let implementation convenience determine product truth;
- 🎙️ Voice may clarify meaning, language and symbolism;
- 👁️ Observer may identify patterns or inconsistencies;
- 🗝️ Curator may judge whether a conclusion deserves durable repository status;
- other constitutionally established roles may contribute according to their offices.

The objective is not consensus for its own sake. The objective is a stronger product model.

# AUTHORITY / EVIDENCE MODEL

When evidence conflicts, prefer:

1. explicit current Warden decision;
2. validated product truth supported by actual use;
3. later falsification / discovery over earlier assumption;
4. observed prototype behavior over speculative claims about prototype behavior;
5. implementation convenience last.

`fpserg/RealMe` is the durable discovery/evidence repository, not absolute native-app implementation canon.

`fpserg/realme-mvp-1_1` is a frozen implementation experiment/quarry, not product canon.

`fpserg/realme-1_2` is the active implementation and may itself be challenged by later product discovery.

# IMPORTANT DISCOVERY ALREADY MADE

The explicit LI → OR → WBTD → Freeze → Chronicle flow was partly shaped by limitations of operating inside ChatGPT: imperfect persistent memory, context reconstruction and time-awareness.

Do not preserve that flow automatically in the native product.

Preserve and redesign for its underlying requirements:

- durable memory;
- chronology and timezone awareness;
- recoverable state;
- provenance/history;
- distinction between lived observation and AI interpretation;
- commitment continuity;
- reflection/synthesis;
- user trust in what the system remembers and why.

# CURRENT PRODUCT BASELINE

The Living World work established strong evidence for:

- evolving personal ontology rather than a universal fixed 3×3 scheme;
- Realm as the universal named top tier, with lower tiers discoverable and potentially arbitrarily deep;
- stable identity through later naming or reclassification;
- semantic zoom from World into progressively deeper accepted structure;
- mobile-first visual behavior;
- the painting as geography and code as semantics for authored visual instances;
- dynamic state separate from canonical geography;
- personal ontology discovered from interaction rather than imposed at onboarding;
- modular / progressive Living World formation rather than requiring a complete authored map at first use.

For precise current World canon, read the relevant documents under `docs/PRODUCT/VISUAL/` and accepted product decisions.
