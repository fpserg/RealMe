# 📖 Day 047 — Places Becoming Home

## 🌊 Beyond the Stronghold Gates

The Stronghold gained a new piece of geography.

Only ten minutes away, the family discovered a beach. The children were immediately happy there, and returning required no campaign, construction or improvement. It was simply a place to enjoy.

This subtly expanded what the Stronghold means. Its surrounding world is beginning to reveal itself not merely as territory requiring stewardship, but as a landscape in which family life can happen.

---

## 🧭 From Patches Toward Architecture

RealMe encountered resistance at one of its foundations.

Repeated attempts to repair Commitment recognition through individual patches did not produce reliable behavior. Rather than continuing indefinitely along the same path, development began moving toward a simpler proposition: one coherent logic operating across views and input types.

Whether that architecture will solve the problem remains unknown. But the nature of the problem became clearer. Reliability cannot come from accumulating exceptions if RealMe is ultimately meant to maintain one coherent model of the World.

---

## 🧸 Things Returning

A bag of Sergey Jr's old clothes returned to usefulness when Oksi discovered that they now fit Owling.

Nothing new needed to be acquired. Something preserved from one child's earlier years simply found its next place in the Household.

---

## 🌍 Closing Chapter

Day 047 was quiet, but two parts of the World became slightly more coherent.

Around the Stronghold, geography began turning into lived family territory. Inside RealMe, accumulated implementation complexity forced attention back toward architectural unity.

Both developments concerned the same underlying movement: finding what already exists, understanding how it belongs, and allowing it to serve the World more naturally.
