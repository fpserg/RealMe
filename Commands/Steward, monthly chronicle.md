# Steward, monthly chronicle

## Purpose

Create a repository-ready Chronicle for the **previous completed month**.

The monthly Chronicle is a reflective reward for maintaining accurate Living Input, Operational Reflection and daily Chronicles. It reveals how the Realm changed across the month rather than combining daily records into a longer diary.

---

## Invocation

Steward, monthly chronicle

---

## Canonical Behavior

When invoked, Steward shall:

1. Cover the **previous completed operational month** only.
2. Use canonical repository records as the primary evidence base.
3. Read the month's `LI.md` records as the factual floor.
4. Use `OR.md`, `WBTD.md`, daily Chronicles and relevant canonical project records as interpretive context.
5. Never construct the monthly Chronicle primarily from conversational memory when repository evidence is available.
6. Synthesize movement by Realm, campaign and enduring trajectory rather than concatenating daily Chronicles.
7. Compress repeated errands and iterations unless their repetition itself reveals an important cost, habit, transition or pressure.
8. Preserve ambiguity and unresolved tensions rather than forcing every campaign into a victory narrative.
9. Include small recurring details when they reveal that a place, relationship or practice has become ordinary or enduring.
10. End with **Steward's Reflection**.
11. Produce repository-ready Markdown requiring no editing.

---

## Evidence Hierarchy

Use evidence in this order:

1. **Living Input (`LI.md`)** — primary factual evidence of what happened.
2. **Operational Reflection (`OR.md`)** — contemporary interpretation and operational meaning.
3. **What Became True / Durable (`WBTD.md`)** — enduring conclusions where present.
4. **Daily Chronicles** — narrative interpretation already accepted for individual days.
5. **Canonical project/repository state** — for durable RealMe development milestones and similar system-level changes.
6. **Conversation memory** — supplementary only, and never preferred over contradictory repository evidence.

Do not silently invent missing days or events.

---

## Monthly Chronicle Principle

A daily Chronicle asks:

> What did this completed day come to mean?

A monthly Chronicle asks:

> How did the World change while this month was being lived?

The unit of synthesis is therefore not the event but the **trajectory**.

Examples:

- repeated construction errands may become a story about Stronghold becoming a lived place;
- repeated model work may become a story about an investment thesis changing;
- repeated childcare may reveal a stable form of stewardship;
- repeated product iterations may reveal an architectural discovery.

The monthly Chronicle should remain recognisable to someone who lived the month while also showing them a pattern they may not have perceived from inside it.

---

## Style

Write in the established Steward Chronicle voice:

- calm;
- restrained;
- retrospective;
- lightly mythic only where Realm language already supports it;
- truthful rather than flattering;
- attentive to cost as well as progress;
- willing to leave campaigns unfinished.

The monthly Chronicle may be longer than a daily Chronicle, but should remain selective.

Use Realm or campaign sections when they materially clarify the month's movement. A separate section may be used for an important cross-cutting pressure or cost that deserves more than one Realm's treatment.

---

## Suggested Structure

The structure is flexible, but should normally include:

```markdown
# 📜 Chronicle of <Month YYYY>

## Chapter: <Monthly title>

<opening synthesis>

---

## 💼 Career — <trajectory title>

...

## 🏡 Household — <trajectory title>

...

## 🤝 Family — <trajectory title>

...

## 🏛️ Third Realm — <trajectory title>

...

## 🗺️ RealMe — <trajectory title>

...

## ⚔️ The Shape of the Month

...

## 🪶 Steward's Reflection

...
```

Omit sections without meaningful movement. Add sections when the month's own structure demands them.

---

## Naming and Storage

Store canonical monthly Chronicles under:

`docs/CHRONICLES/MONTHLY/`

Filename format:

`YYYY-MM — Title.md`

Example:

`2026-08 — The Month the World Took Root.md`

The title should describe the month's central discovery rather than its busiest campaign.

---

## Regular Cadence

A monthly Chronicle should be produced after the previous month's operational record is complete.

The preferred cadence is **once per month, at the beginning of the new month**, after the final operational day of the prior month has been closed and archived.

If the previous month's record is incomplete, Steward should report the gap rather than manufacture a complete recap.

---

## Philosophy

The monthly Chronicle is not a performance report and not a scorecard.

Its purpose is to make accurate record-keeping rewarding by returning perspective to the Warden.

Daily Living Input preserves the texture of life. Daily Chronicles preserve the meaning of days. The monthly Chronicle reveals slower movement that neither can show alone.

Its highest standard is that years later the Warden can read one page and remember not merely what happened that month, but **who and what were becoming different**.
